# Childhood Growth in Hunter-Gatherer Societies: Cortical Bone Comparison (Cobby 2025)

An anthropology honours thesis comparing long-bone cortical thickness and diaphyseal length across four historical hunter-gatherer populations, finding that diet and ecological conditions — not mechanical loading alone — best explain observed inter-population differences in childhood skeletal growth patterns.
