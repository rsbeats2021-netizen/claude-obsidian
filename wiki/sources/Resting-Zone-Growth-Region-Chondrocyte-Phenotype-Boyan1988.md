# Resting Zone vs. Growth Region Chondrocyte Phenotype In Vitro (Boyan et al. 1988)

A foundational 1988 paper from Barbara Boyan's group — the same lab lineage behind [[Membrane-Effects-Sex-Hormones-Growth-Plate-Chondrocytes-Thesis|ElBaradie's 2012 thesis]] already in this domain — showing resting-zone and growth-region costochondral chondrocytes retain distinct phenotypes even after isolation into in vitro culture. This establishes that zone-specific chondrocyte identity is at least partly cell-intrinsic, not purely a product of the in vivo positional microenvironment — an early precursor finding to the modern genetic/transcriptomic confirmation of resting-zone stem-cell identity ([[PTHrP-Resting-Chondrocytes-Wnt-Inhibitory-Environment|Hallett et al. 2021]]) covered elsewhere in [[Epiphyseal Skeletal Stem Cells and Hedgehog Signaling]].

## Relevance to This Domain

A foundational precursor paper for [[Epiphyseal Skeletal Stem Cells and Hedgehog Signaling]], and confirms the Boyan/Schwartz lab (behind [[Membrane-Effects-Sex-Hormones-Growth-Plate-Chondrocytes-Thesis|ElBaradie 2012]]) has been working on resting-zone chondrocyte identity questions since at least 1988.
