# Letrozole PK: Serum Estradiol/Estrone in Postmenopausal Breast Cancer (Faltinová et al. 2023)

A clinical pharmacokinetics study using highly sensitive LC-MS/MS to measure letrozole's estrogen-suppression effect in postmenopausal breast cancer patients — the same drug ([[Letrozole-Bone-Laying-Hens|already covered]] in this domain for laying-hen bone effects, and via the broader [[Aromatase-Inhibitors-Near-Final-Height-Males|AI-for-height]] and [[Aromatase-Inhibitors-Short-Stature-Cochrane-Review|Cochrane review]] literature for pediatric growth-plate use) but in its dominant clinical application: adjuvant breast cancer treatment.

> [!contradiction] Flagged as topically unrelated to this domain's growth-plate focus despite sharing the drug (letrozole) with several on-topic sources: this is an oncology pharmacokinetics study in postmenopausal women, not a growth/bone outcome study, following the same "same drug, different clinical context" flagging pattern as this domain's fulvestrant-oncology papers.

## Relevance to This Domain

Confirms letrozole's estrogen-suppression pharmacokinetics with high analytical precision, potentially useful background for [[Aromatase]], but the clinical population and outcome (breast cancer treatment monitoring) has no direct growth-plate relevance.
