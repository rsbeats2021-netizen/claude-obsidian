# Research brief — The cartilage circadian clock

## Core claim
Growth plate cartilage runs its own local circadian clock, and PTH signalling
resets it. Everything downstream of that is currently hypothesis.

## Key points (tier-tagged)
- **Tier 2** — PTH signalling resets the circadian clock specifically within
  cartilage in organ-cultured murine femurs — a previously uncharacterised link
  between PTH/PTHrP signalling and circadian biology in the growth plate
  (Okubo 2015).
- **Tier 2** — The clock is local: measurable in isolated tissue with no brain
  attached.
- **Tier 2** — Structural significance: PTH/PTHrP already sits at the centre of
  growth plate regulation via the feedback loop controlling chondrocyte
  maturation. The same axis now carries timing information.
- **Tier 3 — speculative** — Sleep disruption, shift patterns and feeding
  timing become *plausible* inputs. Not demonstrated.

## The reason this deck exists
Slides 6–8 are the actual point. This is a textbook case of a real mechanism
being converted into confident sleep-optimisation advice before anyone measures
a height outcome. The deck names that pattern explicitly: "real pathway,
invented protocol." That's more useful to the audience than the finding itself.

## Editing constraint

wording. Removing it turns this deck into exactly the thing it criticises.
