# SIRT1 in Bone Metabolism and Osteoporosis: Review (Chen et al. 2024)

A comprehensive review of SIRT1's broader bone-metabolism role, directly extending [[SASP-Chondrocyte-Osteoarthritis-Peptide-Chondroprotection-Linkova|Linkova et al.]]'s SIRT1-Sox9 interaction finding already in this domain to the wider osteoporosis/bone-metabolism literature — confirming SIRT1 as a genuinely significant, still-emerging molecule in skeletal biology beyond its cartilage-specific role.

## Relevance to This Domain

Substantially reinforces and broadens [[Sox9]]'s SIRT1-regulation thread already established via [[SASP-Chondrocyte-Osteoarthritis-Peptide-Chondroprotection-Linkova|Linkova et al.]], extending it to bone metabolism/osteoporosis generally.
