# Anabolic Steroids Distort (Not Just Enlarge) Craniofacial Growth (Barrett & Harris 1993)

A dose-response extension of this domain's nandrolone-craniofacial-growth thread ([[Nandrolone-Craniofacial-Growth-Rats-Fischman|Fischman et al.]], [[Anabolic-Steroids-Mandibular-Growth-Rats-Gebhardt|Gebhardt & Pancherz]]): using a three-arm dose comparison (control, low-dose, high-dose mimicking actual human abuse levels) in 80 rat pups, nandrolone phenpropionate increased nearly all craniofacial dimensions 3-5%, but the high-dose group specifically produced a genuine shape distortion — a maxillomandibular anteroposterior discrepancy from maxillary excess — not merely a proportionately larger, normally-shaped face. Motivated explicitly by the 1988 Olympics doping controversy and documented adolescent steroid-misuse prevalence (up to 19% of 11th-grade males in some U.S. surveys cited in the paper).

> [!key-insight] This is the clearest evidence in this domain's craniofacial-anabolic-steroid cluster that the effect is dose-dependent in *kind*, not just magnitude: low doses proportionately enlarge the face, but abuser-level doses specifically distort facial proportions — directly relevant to concerns about adolescent AAS misuse producing discernible, non-reversible facial changes.
