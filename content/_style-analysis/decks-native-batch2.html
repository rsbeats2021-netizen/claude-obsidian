Can a dead follicle come back?

It was never dead. JCI, 2011.

SWIPE →

02WHAT WE KNOW

Read this first

I see MPB explained wrong constantly. People say the follicle DIES and that after 5 years it is GONE. Is that true? I went through 3 papers, 1 of them from 2011 using paired biopsies from the same men, and this is what they actually found.

  • Educational only. NOT medical advice.
  • Every claim is cited on slide 9.
  • I flag where the evidence is weak.

3 PAPERS

03PAPER 1

THIS IS THE ONE THAT MATTERS. JCI, 2011.

Journal of Clinical Investigation

Vol. 121 · 2011 · Garza LA, Yang CC, Zhao T, et al.

Bald scalp in men with androgenetic alopecia retains hair follicle stem cells but lacks CD200-rich and CD34-positive progenitor cells

Paired biopsies were taken from bald and haired scalp in the same men. KRT15-high stem cells were maintained in bald scalp, while CD200hi ITGA6hi and CD34hi progenitor populations were markedly diminished.

So the STEM CELLS are still there at year 10 and year 20. What has gone is the step that turns them into working cells, and only 2 of the 3 markers dropped.

GARZA 2011

04THE ACTUAL DEFECT

The conversion is what breaks

Both scalps have the stem cells. Only the haired one still makes progenitors. THAT is the broken step, and this is why the field talks about reactivation rather than replacement.

NOT A DEAD FOLLICLE

05WHY IT SHRINKS

Every cycle comes back thinner

A normal anagen phase runs 2 to 6 years. DHT shortens it a little each round, so by the time you see it in a mirror you have already lost 3 or 4 cycles of shaft thickness.

MINIATURISATION

06THE NUMBERS

What the trials actually show

Both are MAINTENANCE first, regrowth second. Over 5 years the 1mg group held roughly stable while placebo kept falling, so most of that gap is loss you avoided, not hair you grew back.

MAINTENANCE FIRST

07WHERE I'M UNSURE

The part I can't promise you

Retained stem cells means reversal is BIOLOGICALLY plausible. Does that mean anything on the market does it today? No. Here is where I keep landing after 3 papers.

  • Nobody has shown a reliable way to restart that conversion step in humans.
  • Longer miniaturisation means more fibrosis around the bulge, which is harder to undo.
  • The 2011 finding is about tissue, not about a product you can buy.

PLAUSIBLE ≠ AVAILABLE

08WHAT I'D DO

Time is the whole variable

If the defect is a conversion step and fibrosis makes it harder, then EARLY is doing most of the work. At Norwood 2 you have far more to protect than at Norwood 5.

  • Photograph the same 2 areas monthly, same light.
  • Compare the vertex against the back of your head, that region is your control.
  • Decide with a clinician, not with a comment section.

MEASURE, DON'T GUESS

03THE CURVE

More is not more

This is an INVERTED U, described in 1908, and it applies to nearly every stimulant. Past your own peak, 400mg puts you lower than the 200mg you started with.

YERKES-DODSON

04WHAT SURVIVES

Ranked by human trials

Caffeine has HUNDREDS of human trials. Creatine has hundreds at 3 to 5g, with a smaller but real cognition set. Theanine has maybe 20. After that the line falls off a cliff.

SAVE THIS SLIDE

05THE BASELINE

Sleep sets the ceiling

Every compound is measured against a baseline that sleep sets. Running 5 hours instead of 8? Then you are paying for something 3 extra hours gives you free, at a bigger effect size.

THE BIGGEST EFFECT SIZE

06TOLERANCE

Receptors answer back

Sustained stimulation reduces receptor density. The 200mg that worked in week 1 does nothing by week 12, and the fix most people reach for is 400mg, which restarts the same loop.

Things I stopped buying

Run it like a study

  • Pick 1 measure before you start, not after.
  • Stop it and see if the measure moves back.

  • Educational only. NOT medical advice.
  • No doses, no compounds, no sources to buy.

HARM REDUCTION

03THE 4 GAPS

What gets left out

  • The actual dose, and how it changed across the 12 weeks.
  • What ELSE was run alongside it, which is nearly always more than 1 thing.
  • Bloodwork before and after, with the panel named.
  • What STOPPED working, and what they did when it did.

A post missing all 4 is a testimonial. That is not an insult, it is a different kind of document, and 4 of the 4 I checked were missing every one.

4 OF 4 MISSING

04THE AXIS

Shutdown is the expected result

Your own production shuts down within about 2 weeks. That is not a side effect, it is the MECHANISM working. Recovery is usual but past 12 months of exposure it gets far less predictable.

HPTA SUPPRESSION

05NOT LOCAL

Receptors are everywhere

Androgen receptors sit in nearly every tissue you have. Whatever effect you want at 1 site arrives at all 4 of these too, which is why this is never a local decision.

SYSTEMIC BY DEFINITION

06THE AGE PROBLEM

If your plates are open

This is the ONE case where I will be blunt. Aromatised oestrogen drives plate closure. Under 18, using them can end the 4 or 5cm you were trying to protect, permanently.

  • Plate closure is permanent. There is no version of undoing it.
  • A hand x-ray tells you your status in about 10 minutes.
  • Under 18, this is a conversation with a doctor. Full stop.

IRREVERSIBLE

07WHAT TO ASK

How to read any post

I use these 3 questions on every claim in this space now. Of the 4 posts I checked, all 4 failed at question 1, which saves a lot of time.

  • What was the dose, and over how many weeks?
  • What does the bloodwork show, before and after?
  • Who benefits if I believe this?
