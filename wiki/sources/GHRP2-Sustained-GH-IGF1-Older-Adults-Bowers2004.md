# GHRP-2 Sustains GH/IGF-I Elevation in Older Adults (Bowers et al. 2004)

Demonstrates that a GH-releasing peptide (GHRP-2), given by continuous 30-day infusion, sustains elevated pulsatile GH secretion and downstream IGF-I/IGFBP levels in older men and women — a pharmacological approach to restoring more youthful GH-axis activity, distinct from direct exogenous GH or IGF-I administration covered elsewhere in this domain.

## Relevance to This Domain

Adds a GH-secretagogue (rather than direct hormone replacement) approach to [[Growth Hormone-IGF-1 Axis]], relevant to the anti-aging/GH-optimization literature adjacent to this domain's pediatric-growth focus.
