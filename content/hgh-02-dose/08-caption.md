Someone randomised GH-deficient adolescents to 0.3 vs 0.7 mg/kg/week and followed
them to adult height. More than double the dose bought 4.6 cm.

The surprise: bone age advanced ~1 year per year in BOTH groups. The high dose
did not burn through the plates faster. Insulin moved instead.
