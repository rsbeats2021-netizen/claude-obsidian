# GH, IGF-I, and Exercise Effects on Muscle in Hypophysectomized Rats (Grossman et al.)

A NASA-Ames/UCLA collaboration (spaceflight and disuse-atrophy research context) testing GH and IGF-I effects, with and without exercise, on non-weight-bearing fast-twitch muscle fibers in hypophysectomized rats — the same surgical model used in [[IGF1-GH-Differential-Effects-Growth-Plate-Hunziker|Hunziker et al. 1994]] to isolate GH/IGF-I action from endogenous confounds, but applied here to skeletal muscle rather than growth-plate cartilage.

> [!gap] Read via abstract/title page only; specific quantitative findings not extracted. Muscle physiology (not growth-plate/bone) is a tissue context distinct from most of this domain's other GH-IGF-1 axis sources.

## Relevance to This Domain

Extends [[Growth Hormone-IGF-1 Axis]] to a muscle-tissue context using the same hypophysectomized-rat experimental logic as [[IGF1-GH-Differential-Effects-Growth-Plate-Hunziker|Hunziker et al. 1994]], illustrating that GH/IGF-I's systemic reach extends well beyond the growth plate this domain otherwise focuses on.
