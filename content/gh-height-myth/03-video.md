The growth hormone research. Both halves are real studies. That's what makes this interesting.

[cover card]

One says GH reliably adds height in short kids. The other says it does nothing, or makes things worse.

[both citing real data]

Meta-analyses of kids with idiopathic short stature: treated groups ended up 0.84 standard deviations taller than controls. Roughly four to six centimetres of adult height.

[the pooled estimates]

A 1997 study of boys who were short but not GH deficient: untreated reached 162.0 cm on average. GH treated reached 154.2 cm.

[the bad outcomes]

Different populations and different designs. Pooled analyses lean on idiopathic short stature groups, many uncontrolled.

[at the same time]

Not that GH works. Not that it doesn't.

[of this whole topic]

Where gains do happen they run to roughly thirty five thousand dollars per inch of adult height.
