# RUNX2 Maintains Cranial Base Synchondrosis Chondrocytes (Hallett et al. 2025)

From the same Ono/Hallett lab lineage as [[PTHrP-Resting-Chondrocytes-Wnt-Inhibitory-Environment|Hallett et al. 2021]] and [[Hedgehog-Resting-Zone-Chondrocytes-Osteoblast-Transformation|Orikasa et al. 2023]] already central to this domain's epSSC cluster, this paper extends the RUNX2 transcription factor (already tracked in [[Molecular Regulators of Growth Plate Chondrocyte Maturation]]) to an entirely different growth-plate-like structure: cranial base synchondroses, the bidirectional cartilage growth centers driving anteroposterior skull-base growth. RUNX2 loss directly explains human RUNX2 haploinsufficiency's clinical phenotype (cleidocranial dysplasia's deficient midfacial growth) — connecting a gene this domain already tracks in long-bone growth plates to a distinct craniofacial growth structure.
