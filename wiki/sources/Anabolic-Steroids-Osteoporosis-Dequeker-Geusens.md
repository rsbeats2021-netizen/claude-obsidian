# Anabolic Steroids and Osteoporosis (Dequeker & Geusens)

Reviews anabolic androgen use specifically for osteoporosis treatment — extending this domain's anabolic-androgen literature (otherwise dominated by pediatric growth-promotion contexts) to a geriatric/bone-density-preservation application.

## Relevance to This Domain

Adds an osteoporosis-treatment application context to [[Testosterone]]'s anabolic-androgen catalog, complementing the domain's otherwise pediatric-growth-focused anabolic-steroid literature.
