# Liquiritigenin's Anti-Inflammatory Mechanism via NF-κB/iNOS (Kim et al.)

Extends [[Liquiritigenin-Osteoblast-Osteoclast-Dual-Effects|Uchino et al.]]'s bone-cell findings with an anti-inflammatory mechanism (NF-κB-dependent iNOS/cytokine suppression) that mechanistically echoes [[ERalpha-DNA-Damage-Chondrocyte-Senescence-Osteoarthritis|Zhang et al. 2023]]'s NF-κB-mediated ERα-protective pathway already in this domain — suggesting NF-κB suppression may be a shared downstream mechanism across multiple bone/cartilage-protective compounds in this domain's corpus.

## Relevance to This Domain

Adds an anti-inflammatory (NF-κB/iNOS) mechanism to [[Liquiritigenin-Osteoblast-Osteoclast-Dual-Effects|Uchino et al.]]'s bone-cell findings, and connects to the NF-κB pathway already flagged as relevant to chondrocyte protection via [[ERalpha-DNA-Damage-Chondrocyte-Senescence-Osteoarthritis|Zhang et al. 2023]].
