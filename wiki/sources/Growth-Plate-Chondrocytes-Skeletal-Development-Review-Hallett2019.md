# Growth Plate Chondrocytes: Skeletal Development Review (Hallett, Ono, Ono 2019)

A comprehensive review by the same Ono-lab authors (Shawn Hallett, Noriaki Ono) behind [[PTHrP-Resting-Chondrocytes-Wnt-Inhibitory-Environment|Hallett et al. 2021]] and [[Hedgehog-Resting-Zone-Chondrocytes-Osteoblast-Transformation|Orikasa et al. 2023]] already central to this domain's [[Epiphyseal Skeletal Stem Cells and Hedgehog Signaling]] cluster — this 2019 review predates and lays the conceptual groundwork for their later stem-cell-specific experimental discoveries.

## Relevance to This Domain

Background/foundational review from the same Ono lab already central to [[Epiphyseal Skeletal Stem Cells and Hedgehog Signaling]], tracing this research program's conceptual development before its later stem-cell-specific findings.
