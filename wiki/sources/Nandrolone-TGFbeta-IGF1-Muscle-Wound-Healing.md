# Local Nandrolone, TGF-β1/IGF-1, and Muscle Wound Healing (Al-Abdullah & Al-Mashhadane 2023)

Adds a fourth context to this domain's nandrolone/Deca-Durabolin risk-benefit spectrum (alongside achondroplasia growth benefit, reproductive-organ toxicity, and telomere elongation): local application at a muscle wound site upregulated TGF-β1 and IGF-1, associated with improved healing. This links nandrolone's tissue-regenerative effects specifically to growth-factor pathways already central to this domain ([[Growth Hormone-IGF-1 Axis]]) rather than purely to classical androgen-receptor signaling.
