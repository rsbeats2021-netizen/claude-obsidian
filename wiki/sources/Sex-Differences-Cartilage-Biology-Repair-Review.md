# Sex Differences in Cartilage Biology and Repair: A Modern Review (2023)

A modern comprehensive review directly relevant to this domain's extensive sex-dimorphism-in-chondrocyte-response literature (the Boyan/Schwartz/Ornoy lineage: Nasatzky 1993, Ekstein 2005, Raz 2005), arguing this remains an underappreciated methodological issue — many cartilage-biology studies continue to arbitrarily assign sex demographics despite well-established clinical sex differences in cartilage degeneration and repair outcomes.

## Relevance to This Domain

A modern synthesizing review situating this domain's decades-spanning [[Sex-Dependent-Estradiol-Chondrocyte-Differentiation-Nasatzky1993|Boyan/Schwartz/Ornoy sex-dimorphism lineage]] within current field-wide methodological concerns about sex as a study variable in cartilage research.
