Ten weeks of nandrolone. And the mechanism explains why recovery isn't guaranteed.

[cover card]

Come off, restart the axis, recover. The framing is that shutdown is temporary and reversible by default.

[as a speed bump]

Nandrolone decanoate, twice weekly, for ten weeks in male rats. High dose — and the point is what high dose does to tissue, not what typical use does.

[was 15 mg per kg]

Destruction of the basement epithelial lining. Tubular degeneration and necrosis in the testis and epididymis.

[was structural]

Exogenous nandrolone suppresses the hypothalamic-pituitary-testicular axis. LH and FSH fall.

[is the hpt axis]

Fewer Sertoli cells. Falling intratesticular testosterone — which the testis needs at concentrations far above blood levels to function at all.

[downward]

Anyone using these for height still has open growth plates, meaning a reproductive axis that hasn't finished maturing — being suppressed before it completed.

[takeaway card]

Interrupting a system mid-construction isn't the same as pausing a finished one.
