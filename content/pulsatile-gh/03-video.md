Growth hormone. Pattern beats quantity, and that was shown in 1988.

[cover card]

How many units. How much your body makes.

[is about amount]

Hypophysectomised rats — no endogenous growth hormone at all. Then GH was infused either in pulses or continuously, and skeletal tissue was measured.

[removed the pituitary]

Pulsatile infusion more effectively increased IGF-1 messenger RNA specifically in skeletal tissue than continuous infusion did.

[in skeletal tissue]

Two protocols delivering identical total hormone can produce different skeletal responses. Dose alone doesn't determine the outcome — timing is part of the signal.

[total dose is incomplete]

Natural GH release comes in bursts, largest during deep sleep, with low troughs between. That trough is not wasted time — it's part of the signal structure.

[is strongly pulsatile]

Hypophysectomised rats, IGF-1 mRNA as the endpoint, not final height. It establishes that pattern matters mechanistically — not by how much in humans.

[takeaway card]

Pattern matters. Magnitude in humans unmeasured.
