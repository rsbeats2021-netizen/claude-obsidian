# hGH Enhances Both Chondrogenesis and Osteogenesis in Chondroprogenitor Cells (Maor et al. 1989)

A foundational 1989 finding that hGH acts on chondroprogenitor cells — cells not yet committed to either the cartilage or bone lineage — to directly enhance both chondrogenesis and osteogenesis, suggesting GH's growth-promoting effect operates partly at the level of progenitor-cell fate/differentiation, not just proliferation of already-differentiated chondrocytes.

## Relevance to This Domain

Extends [[Growth Hormone-IGF-1 Axis]] and [[Epiphyseal Skeletal Stem Cells and Hedgehog Signaling]] with foundational evidence that GH acts at the chondroprogenitor stage, promoting both chondrogenic and osteogenic differentiation.
