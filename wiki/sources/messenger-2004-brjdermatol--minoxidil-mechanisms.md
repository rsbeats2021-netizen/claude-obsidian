# Minoxidil: mechanisms of action on hair growth

**Design.** Narrative review. Tier B for the clinical effect, tier C/D for the mechanism.

## Key claims
- Minoxidil is a **prodrug**. Sulfotransferase (**SULT1A1**) in liver and hair follicle converts it to **minoxidil sulfate**, the active species.
- The proposed mechanism is opening of **ATP-sensitive potassium (K_ATP) channels**, hyperpolarising cells and lowering intracellular free calcium.
- **The important caveat:** this idea "has been difficult to prove and to date there has been no clear demonstration that K_ATP channels are expressed in the hair follicle."
- Other described in-vitro effects: stimulation of cell proliferation, **inhibition of collagen synthesis**, stimulation of **VEGF** and prostaglandin synthesis.
