## Hunziker et al. 1994, J Clin Invest (rat)

QUOTE: "Stem cell cycle time was shortened from 50 to 15 and 8 d after treatment
with IGF-I and GH, respectively."
→ GH shortened the stem cell cycle more than IGF-1 did. Both work; GH acts
harder on the reserve pool.

QUOTE: "Growth plate tissue was subdivided into stem (germinal or reserve) cell,
proliferating, and hypertrophic zones"

QUOTE: "When the maximal chosen dose of rhGH was exceeded, growth plate height
did not remain stable but decreased"
→ More is not linearly better, even in the animal model.
