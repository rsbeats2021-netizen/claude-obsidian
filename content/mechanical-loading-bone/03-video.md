Bone reads force. And cartilage has a dedicated receptor for sensing it.

[cover card]

Chondrocytes sense mechanical load through a three tiered system: ion channels at the membrane, then intracellular signalling, then gene expression changes. Force gets converted into transcription.

[is a real cascade]

Estrogen receptor alpha works as a ligand independent mechanotransducer in chondrocytes. It senses force with no estrogen bound to it at all, a job completely separate from its hormone role.

[is the receptor involved]

Appropriate mechanical loading maintains ERa expression, which keeps chondrogenic genes like COL2 and SOX9 running. SOX9 is the master transcription factor for chondrocyte identity.

[builds tissue]

Excessive or damaging loading inhibits ERa expression. COL2 and SOX9 get downregulated, while MMP13 and ADAMTS4 get upregulated.

[does the opposite]

It does, through named receptors and measurable gene changes. The question is where your loading sits on the dose response curve, because both sides of that curve are real.

[takeaway card]

Build or degrade. Same mechanism decides.
