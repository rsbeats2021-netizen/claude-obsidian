# Insulin's Role in Regulating Local Growth-Plate Aromatase (Hu et al. 2025)

Investigates a mechanistic link this domain hadn't yet covered: whether hyperinsulinemia (from obesity) disrupts childhood linear growth by altering *local* aromatase expression within growth-plate cartilage itself, using a high-fat-diet juvenile rat model. This connects two previously separate threads in this domain — [[Growth Hormone-IGF-1 Axis|insulin/IGF signaling]] and [[Aromatase|local aromatase activity]] — proposing insulin as an upstream regulator of the very enzyme responsible for estrogen-driven growth-plate maturation.

> [!gap] Read via abstract/introduction; the specific quantitative findings and proposed molecular mechanism linking insulin receptor signaling to aromatase transcription were not extracted in full detail.

## Relevance to This Domain

A novel mechanistic bridge between [[Growth Hormone-IGF-1 Axis]] and [[Aromatase]] — proposes that obesity-driven hyperinsulinemia's known association with altered growth trajectories may work partly through local aromatase regulation, a pathway not previously connected in this domain.
