# IGF-I/IGFBP Mediates Nandrolone's Anabolic Effect in Diaphragm Muscle (Lewis et al. 2002)

Extends this domain's anabolic-androgen mechanism literature to a specific molecular pathway: nandrolone's anabolic effect on diaphragm (respiratory) muscle is shown to be modulated by local IGF-I and IGF-binding-protein activity, directly connecting the anabolic-steroid and GH/IGF-1 axis literatures already covered in this domain. Clinically relevant given nandrolone's real-world use to counteract respiratory-muscle wasting in conditions like COPD.
