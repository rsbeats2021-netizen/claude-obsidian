# Androgen Receptor Distribution Across Bone Cell Types (Notelovitz)

Reviews androgen (testosterone and adrenal androgens like DHEA) effects on bone and muscle, noting a specific receptor-distribution detail: androgen-receptor dominance on osteoblasts and osteocytes specifically (versus osteoclasts), with the osteocyte mechanosensory network modulating the osteoclast/osteoblast remodeling coupling that androgens influence.
