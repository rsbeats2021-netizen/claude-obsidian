# Sex- and Hormone-Specific Long-Bone Growth Effects in Gonadectomized Mice (Ornoy et al. 1994)

From the same Ornoy research lineage as [[Sex-Dependent-Estradiol-Chondrocyte-Differentiation-Nasatzky1993|Nasatzky et al. 1993]] and [[Testosterone-Sexual-Dimorphism-5AlphaReductase-Raz2005|Raz et al. 2005]] already central to this domain's sex-dimorphism cluster: directly compares estradiol and testosterone's whole-animal effects on long-bone growth (weight and nose-to-tail length) in gonadectomized male and female mice, finding gender- and hormone-specific response patterns — a whole-animal complement to this lab's otherwise mostly cell-culture-based sex-dimorphism findings.

## Relevance to This Domain

A whole-animal (rather than cell-culture) data point for the Ornoy lab's sex-dimorphism research program, complementing [[Sex-Dependent-Estradiol-Chondrocyte-Differentiation-Nasatzky1993|Nasatzky et al. 1993]] and [[Testosterone-Sexual-Dimorphism-5AlphaReductase-Raz2005|Raz et al. 2005]].
