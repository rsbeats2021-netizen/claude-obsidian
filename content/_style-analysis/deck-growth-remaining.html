09THE TWIST

Boys and girls run the identical curve

10HOW TO READ IT

What to look at on the film

  1. The finger epiphyses. Narrower than the shaft, equal to it, or capping over it?
  2. The thumb. Its plate closes on a predictable schedule relative to the fingers.
  3. The distal phalanges. Any fusion here puts you past 96%.
  4. The wrist. If only radius and ulna remain open, growth is nearly finished.

ASK FOR A HAND RADIOGRAPH

12THE CATCH

Predictions drift, and they drift predictably

Older prediction tables overestimate when the bone age is advanced and underestimate when it is delayed. Treat the output as a range, and get it read by someone who does this.

A RANGE, NOT A PROMISE
