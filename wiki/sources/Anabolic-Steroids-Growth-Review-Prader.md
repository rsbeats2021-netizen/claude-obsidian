# The Influence of Anabolic Steroids on Growth (Prader)

A classic pediatric endocrinology review framing anabolic steroids as the only well-characterized growth-stimulating option for children with hereditary short stature or constitutional delay of growth where no treatable underlying cause exists — foundational historical context for this domain's many later anabolic-androgen-for-growth clinical trials (Stirling 1994, Bergadá 1995, Soliman 2025).

## Relevance to This Domain

Foundational historical framing for [[Testosterone]]'s anabolic-androgen-for-growth clinical literature already well-represented in this domain.
