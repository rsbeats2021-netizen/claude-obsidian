# Sex-Specific Non-Genomic Estrogen Effects on Chondrocyte Membranes via PKC (Sylvia et al. 2000)

Extends [[Estradiol-Nongenomic-Chondrocyte-Maturation-Stages-Schwartz1996|Schwartz et al. 1996]]'s foundational non-genomic estrogen finding with a specific sex-dependent mechanism: E2 directly alters growth-plate chondrocyte membrane fluidity and activates PKC in a non-genomic manner specifically in female (not male) rat cells, with the PKC inhibitor chelerythrine blocking E2-dependent alkaline phosphatase activity — direct evidence that PKC is the downstream signal-transduction step in this sex-specific pathway.

## Relevance to This Domain

Extends [[Estradiol-Nongenomic-Chondrocyte-Maturation-Stages-Schwartz1996|Schwartz et al. 1996]]'s non-genomic estrogen finding with the specific PKC-mediated, female-specific signal-transduction mechanism, directly complementing [[Estrogen-IP3-Calcium-Growth-Plate-Sex-Specific-Ekstein2005|Ekstein et al. 2005]]'s later calcium-signaling work in the same lab lineage.
