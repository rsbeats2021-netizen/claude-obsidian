GHRH and GHRP. And why one of them is not a somatostatin blocker.

[cover card]

Cloning the GHS receptor, and then finding its natural ligand ghrelin, showed this was a separate physiological system for controlling growth hormone rather than a chemical trick that borrows an existing one. As the review puts it, the dual control of GH secretion became a trinity.

[and their own ligand]

This asymmetry decides how any stack behaves over time and it is the most practically useful thing in this post. Fourteen days of continuous GHRH infusion did not desensitise the pituitary at all.

[at the same rate]

Thirty days of continuous GHRP-2 drove GH more than 3 fold on day 1 and more than 1.8 fold by day 14 and 30. Still working, visibly smaller, and the fade stabilising rather than continuing to fall.

[the bowers data]

Two accelerators on genuinely different wiring do multiply rather than overlap, and that is the best-supported claim here by some distance. And both of them answer to somatostatin, which is why the timing of a pulse ends up mattering more than the size of the dose you put into it.

[takeaway card]

Different pathways multiply. The brake still decides.
