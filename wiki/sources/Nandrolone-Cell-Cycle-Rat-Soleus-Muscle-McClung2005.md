# Nandrolone Modulates Cell-Cycle Regulation in Overloaded Skeletal Muscle (McClung et al. 2005)

Extends this domain's anabolic-androgen mechanism literature to skeletal muscle cell-cycle regulation: nandrolone decanoate modulates cell-cycle regulatory protein expression specifically in functionally overloaded (mechanically stressed) rat soleus muscle, complementing [[IGF1-IGFBP-Diaphragm-Nandrolone-Lewis2002|Lewis et al. 2002]]'s parallel finding of IGF-I/IGFBP-mediated nandrolone action in diaphragm muscle — together mapping nandrolone's anabolic mechanism across two distinct skeletal muscle types.

## Relevance to This Domain

A skeletal-muscle cell-cycle mechanism for [[Testosterone]]'s anabolic-androgen literature, complementing [[IGF1-IGFBP-Diaphragm-Nandrolone-Lewis2002|Lewis et al. 2002]]'s diaphragm-muscle findings.
