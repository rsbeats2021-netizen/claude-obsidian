# Interferon Gamma Decline and the Geroprotective Peptide AEDG (Linkova & Kuznik 2012)

A Russian-language immunosenescence paper studying age-related decline in lymphocyte-secreted interferon gamma, and how the short peptide AEDG (Ala-Glu-Asp-Gly, also called epitalon) may counteract this by binding a complementary sequence in the interferon-gamma gene promoter, activating thymic lymphocyte proliferation.

> [!contradiction] Flagged as topically unrelated to this domain: an immune-system aging paper from the Khavinson peptide-bioregulator research tradition, with no connection to steroid hormones or growth-plate/bone biology.
