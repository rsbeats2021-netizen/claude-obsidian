# Skeletal Development (Basic and Applied Bone Biology, 2nd Ed., Ch. 5)

A textbook chapter, not a primary research paper — included because it supplies the foundational tissue-level framework (intramembranous vs. endochondral ossification, ossification centers, blastema, woven vs. lamellar bone) that every mechanistic paper in this domain's growth-plate cluster implicitly assumes but rarely spells out from first principles. Notably confirms RUNX2's role in intramembranous ossification (directing mesenchymal blastema cells to become osteoblasts) — the same transcription factor this domain already tracks via [[Runx2]] for its distinct role in the endochondral/hypertrophic-chondrocyte pathway, showing RUNX2 sits at a genuine branch point between the skeleton's two formation modes.
