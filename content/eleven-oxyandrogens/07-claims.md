# Claims — eleven-oxyandrogens

## Barnard et al. 2018, J Steroid Biochem Mol Biol 183:192-201 (PMID 29936123)
Verified against the PubMed record, 3 Oct 2026.
QUOTE: "we show that the 11-oxygenated androgen precursors of adrenal origin are
the preferred substrate for AKR1C3. In particular we show that the enzymatic
efficiency of AKR1C3 is 8- and 24-fold greater for 11-ketoandrostenedione than for
the classic substrates androstenedione and 5α-androstanedione, respectively."
QUOTE: "increased ratios of AKR1C3:17βHSD2 significantly favours the flux through
the 11-oxygenated androgen pathway as compared to the classical or
5α-androstanedione pathways"

## Compendium — pathway and regulation
- "the adrenal cortex's zona reticularis produces androgens via the
  adrenal-specific enzyme CYP11B1 (11β-hydroxylase), while downstream peripheral
  conversion steps ... occur in adipose, kidney, liver, and immune cells"
- "The ACTH axis is the master regulator of 11-oxyandrogen production: stress,
  sleep deprivation, low caloric intake, and hypoglycemia elevate ACTH, driving
  CYP11B1 expression and 11OHA4 output, with 11-oxygenated androgens showing a
  circadian rhythm (morning peaks, nocturnal nadirs) similar to cortisol"
- "insulin upregulates both AKR1C3 and SRD5A1, so post-prandial insulin spikes
  acutely increase peripheral 11KT synthesis"
- "adrenarche (zona reticularis activation) is the developmental trigger ... after
  which 11OHA4 becomes the most abundant adrenal androgen precursor by mass"
- "21-hydroxylase-deficient congenital adrenal hyperplasia shunts accumulated
  androstenedione/testosterone through CYP11B1, making 11KT the dominant
  circulating androgen in these patients"
- "in PCOS, insulin-induced AKR1C3 in adipocytes drives androgen excess"
- "Circulating 11-KDHT levels are reported to be 20- to 89-fold lower than DHT
  levels, reflecting relatively inefficient 5α-reduction of 11KT by SRD5A1 (the
  C11-keto group sterically impairs SRD5A1 substrate recognition)"
- "Exogenous DHT enanthate cannot be converted into 11-KDHT because DHT lacks the
  11-carbon hydroxyl group that defines all 11-oxyandrogens"
- "HSD11B1 attenuates AKR1C3-mediated biosynthesis of 11-ketotestosterone in
  adipose tissue ... circulating 11-ketotestosterone increased 2-3 fold in
  individuals with type 2 diabetes after 35 days of the selective oral HSD11B1
  inhibitor AZD4017"
