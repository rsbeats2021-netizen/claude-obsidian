# Research brief — What the AI evidence base actually contains

## Core claim
The randomised evidence for aromatase inhibitors increasing adult height is
four trials and 207 participants — and the only trial that measured actual
adult height found no meaningful difference.

## Key points (tier-tagged)
- **Tier 1** — Systematic review found only 4 RCTs meeting inclusion criteria;
  207 total participants, 84 on active intervention; male children/adolescents
  with short stature vs placebo.
- **Tier 1** — Short-term outcomes (**predicted** adult height) improved in all
  four.
- **Tier 1** — Only ONE trial reported actual/near-final adult height, under
  non-randomised extension conditions, and showed **no meaningful difference**.
- **Tier 1** — Predicted adult height is a surrogate endpoint. The distinction
  between surrogate and measured outcome is the point of the deck.

## Why this deck matters
It is the strongest available correction to how confidently AIs get discussed
in this space, and it does not require asserting harm — only pointing at what
was and wasn't measured. Pairs with `aromatase-inhibitor-monitoring-gap`
(that deck is about unmeasured *safety*, this one about unmeasured *efficacy*).

## Deliberately not included
No dosing, no regimen. Prescription drugs with real BMD consequences.
