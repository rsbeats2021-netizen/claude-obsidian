## Notes
- Slide 4 is the strongest single visual concept across all six decks: the
  hormone is present, the receiver is broken, the plates stay open. Spend the
  design time there rather than on slide 3.
- Slides 7–8 are what makes this deck credible rather than clickbait — it
  presents genuine counter-evidence to its own thesis. Don't cut them for length.
- Verify author/year at the PMC page for any figure before publishing; the
  catalogue lists licence but attribution lines need checking.
