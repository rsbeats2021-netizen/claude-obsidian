## Warm-up, weeks 1-3

| Days | Action | Not this |
|---|---|---|
| 1-7 | Use the account like a person. Follow 20-40 relevant accounts, watch the niche, comment where you have something real to say. No posting. | No mass-follow, no pods |
| 8-14 | 3-4 low-stakes posts on the account's single topic. Consistency of subject is the point; you are teaching the classifier. | No cross-topic |
| 15+ | Normal cadence from the deck library. | — |

Bio, first three posts and pinned post should all say the same thing about what the account is.
Anything that fakes a signal gets accounts limited, and on health-adjacent topics that tends to be permanent.

## Cross-account rule
Before posting, check [[decks/]] for the claim IDs. Four accounts run by one person repeat the same
three favourite facts unless something stops it. The deck ledger is that something.
