# Gene Therapy (TGF-β1 + IL-10) Directs BMSC Chondrogenic Differentiation

A gene-therapy approach to cartilage tissue engineering: lentiviral co-transfection of TGF-β1 and IL-10 into rabbit bone-marrow-derived mesenchymal stem cells successfully induced chondrogenic differentiation, complementing this domain's small-molecule approaches to directing MSC fate toward cartilage.

## Relevance to This Domain

A gene-therapy (rather than small-molecule) approach for [[Cartilage-Tissue-Engineering-Small-Molecules]]'s MSC-to-chondrocyte differentiation literature.
