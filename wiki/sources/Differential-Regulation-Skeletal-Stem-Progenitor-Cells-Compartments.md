# Differential Regulation of Skeletal Stem/Progenitor Cells Across Compartments (Solidum et al. 2023)

A review that situates this domain's growth-plate-specific epSSC findings within the broader skeletal stem/progenitor cell (SSPC) field: bone marrow was long considered the primary SSPC reservoir, but growth plate, periosteum, and calvarial sutures each host their own distinct SSPC populations with different niche environments, embryonic origins, and age/stress sensitivities.

> [!gap] A review article synthesizing prior literature rather than presenting new primary data; the growth-plate-specific mechanistic detail already covered in [[Epiphyseal Skeletal Stem Cells and Hedgehog Signaling]] (Hedgehog activation, Wnt inhibition, PTHrP marking) is not superseded or extended here, only contextualized.

## Relevance to This Domain

Useful framing context for [[Epiphyseal Skeletal Stem Cells and Hedgehog Signaling]]: confirms the growth-plate epSSC population this domain has focused on is one of several distinct, non-interchangeable SSPC niches in the skeleton, each independently regulated.
