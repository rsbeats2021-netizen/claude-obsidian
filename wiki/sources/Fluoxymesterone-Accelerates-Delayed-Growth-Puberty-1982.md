# Fluoxymesterone Accelerates Growth and Puberty in Delayed Boys (1982)

An early (1982) clinical precedent adding fluoxymesterone — an oral 17α-alkylated synthetic androgen — to this domain's catalog of anabolic-androgens used to accelerate growth and pubertal development in constitutionally delayed boys, alongside [[Anabolic-Steroids-Growth-Review-Prader|Prader's broader anabolic-steroid-growth review]] and this domain's nandrolone/oxandrolone literature.

> [!gap] As with most of this domain's anabolic-androgen-for-growth sources, the paper doesn't appear (from abstract-level detail) to report final adult height outcomes or address the accelerated-bone-age tradeoff — whether the accelerated growth comes at the cost of earlier growth-plate closure and reduced final height, the central tension this domain has repeatedly flagged for this drug class.

## Relevance to This Domain

Extends [[Testosterone]] and [[Anabolic-Steroids-Growth-Review-Prader]]'s anabolic-androgen-for-growth-acceleration literature with an early oral-agent (fluoxymesterone) clinical precedent.
