# Endocrine Regulation of the Growth Plate: A Comprehensive Review (Nilsson et al. 2005)

From the same NICHD (Jeffrey Baron) lab lineage already central to this domain's epSSC and estrogen-senescence clusters, this review synthesizes the full endocrine network governing longitudinal bone growth — growth hormone, IGF-I, glucocorticoid, thyroid hormone, estrogen, androgen, vitamin D, and leptin — providing a unified framework connecting this domain's many individual hormone-specific findings.
