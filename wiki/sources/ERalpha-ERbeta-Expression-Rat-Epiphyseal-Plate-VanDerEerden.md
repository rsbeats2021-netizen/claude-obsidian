# Independent Confirmation: ERα/ERβ Expression in the Rat Growth Plate (Van der Eerden et al. 2001)

An independent research group (Leiden University Medical Center, the Netherlands) confirms and extends [[ERalpha-ERbeta-Expression-Rat-Rabbit-Growth-Plate-Postnatal-Nilsson1999|Nilsson et al. 1999]]'s Gothenburg-lineage finding of ERα/ERβ expression across growth-plate zones and postnatal ages, directly mapping mRNA and protein expression in the rat tibial growth plate along with characterizing the hormonal regulation of that expression — valuable as a genuinely independent confirmation (different lab, different country) of a finding otherwise concentrated within one research lineage in this domain.

## Relevance to This Domain

Independent (non-Gothenburg-lineage) confirmation of ERα/ERβ growth-plate expression patterns for [[Estrogen Receptor (ER)]], strengthening confidence that [[ERalpha-ERbeta-Expression-Rat-Rabbit-Growth-Plate-Postnatal-Nilsson1999|Nilsson et al. 1999]]'s findings reflect general biology rather than lab-specific artifacts.
