# GH Secretory Pattern: Differential Peak vs. Trough Effects (Gevers, Wit, Robinson 1996)

Extends the GH-pulsatility literature (already covered via [[Pulsatile-GH-IGF1-mRNA-Isgaard1988|Isgaard et al. 1988]] and [[GH-Pulsatility-Hepatic-Receptors-IGFBP-Bick1992|Bick et al. 1992]]) with specific attention to which component of the pulsatile pattern matters — showing the peak and trough phases of GH secretion have genuinely distinct regulatory effects, not merely differing in magnitude. From the same Evelien Gevers research lineage as this domain's other GH-single-cell-mechanism papers.

## Relevance to This Domain

Adds peak-vs-trough specificity to [[Growth Hormone-IGF-1 Axis]]'s pulsatility literature, from a research lineage (Gevers) already well-represented in this domain.
