# Abaloparatide vs. PTH(1-34): Continuous-Infusion Catabolic Effects on Bone

Abaloparatide is a PTHrP(1-34) analog — directly related to the PTHrP molecule central to this domain's [[PTHrP-Ihh Feedback Loop]] — approved as an osteoanabolic (bone-building) therapy when given by intermittent daily injection. This study instead used continuous infusion in young female mice, finding that under continuous dosing, abaloparatide produces the same catabolic (bone-degrading) effect as continuously-infused PTH(1-34) — a reminder that PTH/PTHrP-pathway drugs' therapeutic direction (anabolic vs. catabolic) depends critically on dosing pattern, not just the molecule itself.

> [!gap] Read via abstract; full histomorphometry and gene-expression comparison data not extracted. Mouse model; clinical abaloparatide is administered by intermittent injection specifically to achieve the opposite (anabolic) effect from what this continuous-infusion study demonstrates.
