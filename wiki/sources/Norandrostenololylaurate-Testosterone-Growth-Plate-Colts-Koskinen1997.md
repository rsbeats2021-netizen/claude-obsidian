# Anabolic Steroid Effects on Testosterone, Libido, and Growth-Plate Closure in Colts (Koskinen & Katila 1997)

A veterinary equine study testing long-term anabolic steroid (19-norandrostenololylaurate) treatment's effects on serum testosterone, sexual behavior, and growth-plate closure timing in young male horses — extending this domain's anabolic-androgen-and-growth-plate-closure literature to a new species (horses) with direct radiographic growth-plate-closure endpoints.

## Relevance to This Domain

Adds an equine species data point to [[Testosterone]]'s anabolic-androgen catalog and [[Epiphyseal Fusion Mechanisms]]'s closure-timing literature, directly measuring growth-plate closure (not just growth velocity) as an anabolic-steroid outcome.
