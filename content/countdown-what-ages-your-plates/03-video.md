4 things. Number 1 isn't the one people argue about.

[cover card]

Methylphenidate promoted premature growth plate closure in cell culture, and a 410 patient study found reduced weight and a temporary growth halt. Real signal, size not settled.

[stimulant medication]

Obesity drives high insulin, and insulin appears to raise aromatase expression inside growth plate cartilage. More local aromatase means more local estrogen at the closure switch.

[being overweight]

Testosterone and its derivatives raise growth velocity and skeletal maturation at the same time. Part of every androgen aromatises into estrogen, which is the closure signal.

[androgens]

It's not close. A man with a broken estrogen receptor never stopped growing.

[estrogen, by miles]

Three of the four converge on the same enzyme and the same hormone. This isn't four separate problems, it's one mechanism with several inputs.

[in that ranking]

Ranking things separately implies they're independent. Fixing number 3 changes number 1, because that's the pathway it runs through.

[takeaway card]

The ranking is useful. The separation is fake.
