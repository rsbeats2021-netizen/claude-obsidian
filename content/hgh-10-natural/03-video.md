Raising IGF-1. One trial moved it with no GH change.

[cover card]

So the chain is GH rises, IGF-1 rises, height follows. A 2025 trial in short-stature children broke that link in a way worth looking at carefully, because it got the IGF-1 rise without the GH rise, and it did it with no drug at all.

[gh up, igf-1 up, height up]

One group did a structured jumping programme for 24 weeks. The other two were observed over the same period with no intervention, which gives you two separate controls rather than one.

[the trial]

The exercise group finished with higher height, higher serum IGF-1, and a higher IGF-1 to IGFBP-3 molar ratio than both control groups, all at p < 0.05 against the untreated group. So a purely mechanical intervention moved the hormone that everyone treats as something you can only change pharmacologically.

[the result]

Here is the finding that makes this worth a post. There was no significant difference in serum GH between the groups, before or after, at p > 0.05.

[the interesting part]

The GH to IGF-1 chain is not one-directional and GH is not its only input. Which lands in the same place as the injection studies: the IGF-1 that matters is made locally, and more than one thing controls how much of it gets made.

[takeaway card]

The axis has more than one lever.
