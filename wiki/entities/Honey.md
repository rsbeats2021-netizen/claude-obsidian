# Honey

Natural sweetener produced by honeybees, composed of 200+ compounds (predominantly fructose and glucose, ~80% simple sugars by weight, plus minerals, phenolics, and enzymes). Glycemic index varies widely by botanical source (32–85), averaging somewhat below refined sugar's ~60–68.

---

## Role in This Wiki

Central substance across the [[Health and Nutrition]] domain, seeded by [[Honey-and-Diabetes|Honey and Diabetes]]. Four research angles are tracked:

- [[Honey Hypoglycemic Mechanism]] — honey's potential to lower or moderate blood glucose in diabetic and healthy subjects
- [[Honey in Diabetic Wound Healing]] — honey's topical use in managing slow-healing diabetic wounds
- [[Honey and Bone Metabolism]] — honey (especially [[Tualang Honey]]) as a potential osteoporosis-prevention agent, the domain's largest evidence cluster
- [[Honey Composition and Bioactivity]] / [[Honey Microbiome]] — what's actually in honey, and how raw vs. processed honey differ chemically and microbially
