# Rapid Non-Genomic Estradiol Effects on Chondrocytes: An Early Boyan/Schwartz Finding (1996)

Foundational early evidence, from the same Boyan/Schwartz lab lineage as [[Estrogen-IP3-Calcium-Growth-Plate-Sex-Specific-Ekstein2005|Ekstein et al. 2005]] and [[Membrane-Effects-Sex-Hormones-Growth-Plate-Chondrocytes-Thesis|ElBaradie's thesis]], establishing that 17β-estradiol has rapid, direct, non-genomic effects on chondrocytes — tested at two distinct maturation stages in male and female rat costochondral cartilage — predating and likely informing this lab's later, more mechanistically detailed non-genomic/membrane-signaling work.

## Relevance to This Domain

An early foundational paper (1996) in the Boyan/Schwartz lab's non-genomic estrogen-signaling research program, predating [[Estrogen-IP3-Calcium-Growth-Plate-Sex-Specific-Ekstein2005|Ekstein et al. 2005]] and [[Membrane-Effects-Sex-Hormones-Growth-Plate-Chondrocytes-Thesis|ElBaradie's thesis]].
