# GH Receptor Signaling Drives Osteogenesis from Bone Marrow Progenitor Cells (Cool et al. 2005)

Directly links GH receptor signaling to osteoblast-lineage commitment from bone marrow progenitor cells (BMPCs) — a mechanistic complement to [[GH-Replacement-Hematopoiesis-GHD-Children-Kawa2014|Kawa et al. 2014]]'s finding that GH also stimulates the erythroid (hematopoietic) lineage from the same general marrow-progenitor compartment, suggesting GH broadly supports multiple, distinct marrow-resident progenitor fates.

## Relevance to This Domain

Extends [[Growth Hormone-IGF-1 Axis]]'s bone-marrow-progenitor thread with an osteogenic-lineage-specific mechanism, complementing [[GH-Replacement-Hematopoiesis-GHD-Children-Kawa2014|Kawa et al. 2014]]'s hematopoietic-lineage finding from the same broad progenitor pool.
