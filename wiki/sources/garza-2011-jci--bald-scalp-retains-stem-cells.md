# Bald scalp retains stem cells but loses progenitors

**Design.** Paired biopsies of bald and haired scalp from the same men. Tier B — human tissue, paired design.

## Key claims
- **KRT15hi stem cells were maintained** in bald scalp.
- **CD200hi ITGA6hi and CD34hi progenitor populations were markedly diminished.**
- The defect is therefore in the **conversion** of stem cells to progenitors, not in the loss of stem cells.

## The weaker downstream claim
"Therefore AGA is reversible" is **tier D**. Retained stem cells establish plausibility only; no product has been shown to restart the conversion step in humans. Keep the two apart — see [[bald-scalp-retains-stem-cells]].
