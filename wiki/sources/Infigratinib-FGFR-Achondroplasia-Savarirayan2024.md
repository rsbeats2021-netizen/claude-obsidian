# Infigratinib for Achondroplasia (Savarirayan et al. 2024)

A major NEJM clinical trial testing infigratinib — an orally bioavailable FGFR1-3-selective tyrosine kinase inhibitor — in achondroplasia, the FGFR3-gain-of-function skeletal condition already covered in this domain via [[TYRA-300-FGFR3-Inhibitor-Chondrodysplasia|Starrett et al. 2025]]'s TYRA-300 mouse-model work. This represents a second, clinically advanced (human trial, not mouse model) FGFR-inhibitor approach to the same disease mechanism.

> [!gap] Read via abstract/introduction; specific trial results (growth outcomes, safety profile) not extracted in full detail.

## Relevance to This Domain

A clinically advanced complement to [[TYRA-300-FGFR3-Inhibitor-Chondrodysplasia|TYRA-300]] — worth comparing the two FGFR-inhibitor approaches (infigratinib's broader FGFR1-3 selectivity vs. TYRA-300's narrower FGFR3-selective, mutation-specific targeting) once full results are available.
