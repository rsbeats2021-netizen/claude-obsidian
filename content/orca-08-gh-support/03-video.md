The GH support stack. Every entry graded by what the evidence actually is.

[cover card]

High-dose GH has two documented costs and they are different in kind. One is metabolic: fasting insulin P = 0.011 and C-peptide P = 0.019 rose on the high arm in Mauras.

[the two leaks]

GH upregulates type 2 deiodinase, free T4 declines significantly by about 1.09 pmol/L, and GH blunts TSH pulsatility so the routine screen misses it. In hypopituitary adults, GH replacement unmasked central hypothyroidism in 36 to 47% of patients who looked euthyroid before starting.

[the strongest entry]

Holding blood glucose at 4.5 to 5.5 mmol/L with exogenous insulin in someone with a working pancreas is targeting the bottom of the normal band with a drug the literature describes as having a narrow therapeutic window. A 30-year-old using insulin to stimulate GH convulsed, required intubation, and glucose infusion alone was not enough to stabilise him.

[why the range matters]

Fasting insulin and HbA1c, because those are the markers that moved in the trial. And IGF-1 against the 2 SDS ceiling, since 28% of the high-dose arm overshot it.

[the monitoring set]

T4 is close to mandatory on high-dose GH and it is the entry with real numbers behind it. The insulin sensitivity side is a real target with reasonable agents and no combination evidence.

[takeaway card]

Replace T4. Protect sensitivity. Do not add insulin.
