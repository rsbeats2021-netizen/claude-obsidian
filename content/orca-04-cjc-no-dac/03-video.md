Mod-GRF 1-29. The accelerator that does not wear out.

[cover card]

A 29-amino-acid GHRH analogue with four substitutions, D-Ala2, Gln8, Ala15 and Leu27, engineered for stability against enzymatic breakdown. Its half-life is roughly 30 minutes, which sets the window in which it can co-stimulate with anything else.

[mechanism]

This is the single most useful fact about this compound and it separates it from the entire GHRP class. Fourteen days of continuous GHRH infusion did not desensitise the pituitary.

[the key property]

CJC-1295 with DAC binds albumin and extends half-life to 6 to 8 days, producing continuous GHRH receptor occupancy. That is mechanistically the opposite of what this compound does.

[the version trap]

If the limit is somatostatin tone, the lever is when you use it rather than how much. Around 70% of daily GH comes out in early sleep, during the window when somatostatin has naturally withdrawn.

[what offsets it]

The co-stimulation window is set by the 30-minute half-life, so a GHRP and this have to arrive close together to get the synergy rather than two separate smaller pulses. And because the GHRH arm does not fatigue while the ghrelin arm does, the component you would cycle or rest is the GHRP, not this one.

[the second one]

Mechanistically the cleanest compound in this axis. Its ceiling is somatostatin tone rather than dose, its effect is bounded by physiological GH release, and the DAC version is a different drug that should not be substituted.

[takeaway card]

The accelerator that holds. Still bounded by the brake.
