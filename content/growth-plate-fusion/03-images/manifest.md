## Licence tiers — what applies to this channel

The channel is monetised (ebook, consultations), so it counts as **commercial use**:

- ✅ **CC BY / CC BY-SA / public domain** — usable, credit required
- ❌ **CC BY-NC** — non-commercial only, so off-limits here. This excludes several
  otherwise-relevant papers in the library (Shim 2015, *The Growth Plate Structure*,
  *Height Gains from Longitudinal Bone Growth*).
- ❌ **All rights reserved** (Elsevier, Springer, Wiley, most legacy journals)
- ⚠️ ResearchGate/Sci-Hub copies carry no licence — the hosting site doesn't change
  the underlying rights.

Safe hunting grounds: PubMed Central OA subset, PLOS, eLife, Frontiers, BMC,
MDPI, Scientific Reports, Wikimedia Commons.
