# cAMP-Dependent Modulation of Calcium Signaling in Articular Chondrocytes (D'Andrea et al. 1996)

Establishes cAMP/calcium crosstalk in articular chondrocytes — forskolin and cAMP amplified ATP-induced calcium oscillations and could independently trigger calcium transients — the same forskolin/cAMP signaling axis central to [[PTHrP-HDAC4-MEF2-Chondrocyte-Hypertrophy-Kozhemyakina2009|Kozhemyakina et al. 2009]]'s PTHrP-hypertrophy-repression mechanism already in this domain, here characterized in a different chondrocyte population (articular rather than growth-plate).

## Relevance to This Domain

Foundational cAMP/calcium-signaling context for [[PTHrP-Ihh Feedback Loop]]'s forskolin/cAMP-based mechanisms, extended here to articular chondrocytes.
