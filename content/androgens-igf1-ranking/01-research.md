# Research brief — Androgens and IGF-1: induction is not dependence

## Core claim
"Effect on IGF-1" is two different measurements that rank in **opposite** orders.
How hard a compound **induces** IGF-1 is one list. How much its anabolic effect
**depends** on the IGF-1 receptor is another. Trenbolone is last on induction and
first on dependence. DHT is first on induction. Blood IGF-1 is a third list, and
that one is really a ranking of aromatization, not of androgen potency.

## Key points (tier-tagged)

Tier key used here, since the pipeline has no written legend:
**Tier 1** = primary read, verbatim quote in `07-claims.md`.
**Tier 2** = primary literature, single model or species, quote still owed.
**Tier 3** = reported in a secondary AI synthesis with a resolvable DOI, primary
not yet opened. Tier 3 numbers do not go on a slide.

- **Tier 1 — the IGF-1 promoter is an androgen target, directly.** Two androgen
  response elements sit between −1320 and −1420 bases of the human IGF-1 upstream
  promoter, they bind the AR DNA-binding domain in gel-shift, testosterone recruits
  AR to them in genomic DNA, and they are activated **5-fold more by AR than by the
  glucocorticoid receptor** (Wu 2007, abstract verified, PMID 17363459). This is
  what makes "androgens raise IGF-1" a transcriptional fact rather than a
  correlation, and it is the paper the whole topic rests on.
  Cells were **HepG2 and LNCaP** — liver and prostate lines, not muscle. The
  "additive AREs" and "DNA-binding-deficient AR abolishes it" details are not in the
  abstract and stay unverified.
- **Tier 1/3 mixed — induction ranking: DHT = testosterone > nandrolone >
  trenbolone.** DHT and testosterone **each** raised IGF-1 **7-fold** in mRNA and
  in secreted protein in human prostatic stroma, flutamide-abolished, with IGF-1R
  unchanged and no change in proliferation (Le 2006, abstract verified,
  PMID 16368782). Nandrolone +58–96% mRNA in diaphragm, 5-fold during regeneration
  (unverified). Trenbolone 1.7× in bovine satellite cells (unverified) and **zero**
  in muscle in vivo (verified, see the Pampusch bullet).
  DHT and testosterone are **equal** in this paper — do not write DHT > T.
- **Tier 1 — dependence ranking inverts it: trenbolone first, nandrolone last.**
  Trenbolone's proliferative effect in bovine satellite cells is **suppressed** by
  each of GDPβS, an MMP2/9 inhibitor, CRM197, AG1024, AG1478 and AG879 — it routes
  GPCR → MMP2/9 → hbEGF → EGFR/erbB2 → IGF-1R **without raising IGF-1 at all**
  (Thornton 2015, abstract verified, PMID 26440329; the verb is "suppressed", not
  "abolished", and JB1 and flutamide are from different papers). Nandrolone's
  anti-atrophy
  actions run through calcineurin–NFAT, Numb/Notch and rapamycin-insensitive
  protein synthesis, and survive with IGF-1 unchanged.
- **Tier 1 — the blood-IGF-1 arm is an estrogen arm.** The IGF-1 rise from
  trenbolone/estradiol cattle implants is the **estradiol's** doing: in the same 20
  steers, TBA alone did not raise longissimus IGF-1 mRNA (**P = 0.99**) while E2
  alone raised it 58% at day 7 and 78% at day 28, and TBA/E2 raised it 65% at day
  28; IGF-1R, AR and ERα mRNA did not move in any arm (Pampusch 2008, abstract
  verified, PMID 18676717). In humans, testosterone replacement raised
  24-h mean GH 0.7 → 1.4 µg/L, GH pulse amplitude 3.1 → 5.2 µg/L and serum IGF-1
  0.9 → 1.1 U/mL in hypogonadal men, and **tamoxifen reversed all three**
  (Weissberger & Ho 1993, verified, PMID 8501143). In pubertal boys, DHT failed to
  reproduce testosterone's effect on GH secretory **pattern** (Veldhuis 1997,
  verified, PMID 9329378). Predicted order, therefore:
  testosterone > boldenone > nandrolone > trenbolone = 0 > DHT (0 to negative).
  **Three limits on that order, all found by verifying it, none optional:**
  (i) the human serum IGF-1 effect is **small** — about +22% in Weissberger and a
  non-significant +16% in Urban's elderly men (141 → 163 ng/ml, n = 6), which are
  the same modest effect detected once and missed once, not a contradiction;
  (ii) Urban is **not** a failure of aromatization — estradiol doubled (17 → 36
  pg/ml, significant) and IGF-1 still did not reach significance, so the splitting
  variable is baseline deficit and power, not the conversion step;
  (iii) Veldhuis measured **ApEn**, the orderliness of GH secretion, not IGF-1 — so
  no one in this set has shown that a non-aromatizable androgen *lowers* serum
  IGF-1 in men. The bottom of this ranking is mechanism, not measurement.
  The large, reliable androgen effect on this axis is on **GH pulse amplitude** in
  frank hypogonadism, not on the IGF-1 number.
- **Tier 1 — nandrolone owns the IGFBP switch.** IGFBP-3 **+69%** and IGFBP-4
  **−40%** in diaphragm, with muscle IGF-1 **+50%** and fibre CSA +20% (type IIa)
  and +30% (type IIx/b), at 6.6 mg/kg/day infused continuously for 17 days in
  1-yr-old female rats (Lewis 2002, **full text verified**, PMID 11788382). IGFBP-4
  is inhibitory, so a fall in it raises free IGF-1 — the authors' own reading is
  "coordinated changes in the muscles to liberate more free IGF-I".
  **Serum IGF-1 did not move**: 287.6 ± 106.1 (control) vs 297.5 ± 86.0 ng/ml, so
  the local/systemic split is real and quotable. State it as a null, not as an
  equivalence: n = 7 per group with SDs around 37% of the mean could not have
  detected a modest rise.
- **Tier 3 — boldenone is a genuine hole.** Checked against the web, not just the
  synthesis: rabbit, lamb, buffalo-calf, horse and veal-calf studies exist and
  report growth performance, carcass traits, thyroid and reproductive hormones,
  haematology and oxidative stress. None found that measured IGF-1, IGF-1R, splice
  variants or IGFBPs. Boldenone cannot be ranked on evidence, only predicted from
  mechanism.

## The argument worth keeping
The induction/dependence inversion is the whole topic, and trenbolone is the proof
of it. A compound can be the one that **needs** IGF-1 signalling most while being
the one that **produces** IGF-1 least. Every lay version of this subject treats
"raises IGF-1" as a single ranked property, and that is the error — it collapses a
production measurement and a receptor measurement into one word.

The second keeper is the aromatization rule — the reason a steroid raises your
IGF-1 blood number is substantially the fraction of it that turns into estrogen,
and tamoxifen reversing the whole effect is the cleanest demonstration of it.
Say it at that size, though. Verifying this one shrank it: the human serum IGF-1
response to testosterone is ~15–25%, the robust effect is on GH pulse amplitude,
and nobody has shown a non-aromatizable androgen *lowering* IGF-1 in men. It is a
real rule about a modest effect, not a switch.

## Where this cuts against the account's own subject
For height specifically, the IGF-1 ranking and the height ranking are partly
**opposed**. The compound that best raises circulating IGF-1 is the one that
aromatizes most, and estradiol is what drives growth-plate senescence and fusion —
see `estrogen-sets-final-height` and `trenbolone-bone-length`. And none of these
four compounds was ever measured on **growth-plate** IGF-1: the data are muscle,
prostate, liver and bone. Ranking them "best for IGF-1" and implying "therefore
best for growth" is exactly the move this vault exists to refuse.

## Honest limits — put this on the caveat slide
There is **no head-to-head study**. Not one paper compares these four compounds on
the same IGF-1 endpoint, so every ranking here is a synthesis across species (rat,
mouse, cattle, sheep, human), tissues and dose ranges.

That has a specific consequence worth stating rather than hedging around:
trenbolone's AR binding affinity is comparable to or above DHT's, so if the route
is AR → ARE → IGF-1, trenbolone should top the induction list, not bottom it. The
most likely explanation is that the ranking partly measures **where people looked**
— DHT was measured in human prostatic stroma, trenbolone in bovine satellite cells.
Ligand-specific AR conformation and in-vivo feedback suppression of endogenous
testosterone are the other two candidates. Until someone runs it head to head,
state the induction list as "as measured in the tissues where each was measured".

Splice variants (Ea / Eb-MGF / Ec) are the weakest section in the whole area: no
compound comparison exists, and testosterone raised Ea and Eb only in castrated or
hypophysectomised animals, not intact ones. Low confidence, flag it or drop it.

## Deck candidates
1. **B-COUNTDOWN** — the ranking, counting down, with the last slide turning on
   itself: the list implies a single property that does not exist.
2. **C-MYTH-FACT** — "raises IGF-1" vs "needs IGF-1", trenbolone as the case.
3. **H-GAPS** — boldenone. Sold for decades, never measured on the axis everyone
   claims it works through.
