# A Botanical Extract as a Potential GH-Alternative for Bone Growth

Tests a novel herbal formula (Astragalus membranaceus, red ginseng, Cervi Parvum Cornu — "Keyfobell powder") as a potential natural alternative to GH therapy, finding it promotes longitudinal bone growth in both hypothalamic cell culture and Sprague-Dawley rats by upregulating circulatory IGF-1, apparently acting upstream via GHRH/ghrelin mRNA expression — a botanical-supplement angle on this domain's GH/IGF-1-axis literature, alongside the domain's existing Astragalus-related honey/health-supplement threads.
