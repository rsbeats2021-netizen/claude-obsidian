# Foundational Discovery: Androgen Receptors in Human Osteoblasts (Colvard et al. 1989)

The original PNAS paper establishing that normal human osteoblast-like cells express functional androgen receptors at all — a decade before [[Androgen-Receptor-Upregulation-Osteoblasts|Wiren et al. 1999]]'s finding (already central to this domain) that DHT up-regulates those same receptors. This is the foundational proof-of-concept every later AR-in-bone-cells mechanism in this domain implicitly builds on.

## Relevance to This Domain

The foundational discovery paper for [[Androgen Receptor (AR)]]'s entire bone/osteoblast thread, directly preceding and enabling [[Androgen-Receptor-Upregulation-Osteoblasts|Wiren et al. 1999]]'s mechanistic work.
