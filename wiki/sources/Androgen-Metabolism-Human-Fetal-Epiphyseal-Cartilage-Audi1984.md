# Androgen Metabolism in Human Fetal Epiphyseal Cartilage (Audi et al. 1984)

A human-tissue complement to [[Testosterone-Metabolism-Rabbit-Cartilage|Takahashi et al. 1984]]'s rabbit cartilage findings already in this domain — direct demonstration that human fetal epiphyseal cartilage and its chondrocytes actively metabolize androgens in primary culture, foundational evidence that local androgen conversion machinery is present in human growth-plate tissue specifically, not just animal models.

## Relevance to This Domain

Human-tissue foundational evidence for local androgen metabolism in [[Estrogen vs Androgen in Male Bone Health]]'s direct-androgen-action cluster, complementing the mostly rabbit/rodent evidence base with human fetal tissue data.
