GH after your. Bones stop lengthening. They keep changing.

[cover card]

So the usual assumption is that growth hormone becomes irrelevant to your skeleton the moment your growth plates fuse. No plate, no lengthening, nothing left to gain, stop wasting your money.

[the assumption]

Length comes from the growth plate, through endochondral ossification, and that process has a hard end date when the plate fuses. That is the one everyone measures.

[length and width]

All of that is mechanism, so here is the outcome that matters. GH replacement therapy stimulates bone turnover, improves bone mass during childhood and adolescence, and decreases fracture incidence by 3 or 4 fold.

[the human endpoint]

After fusion, GH stops being a height drug and continues as a bone drug. Width, cortical mass and fracture risk are all still live outcomes, and all three have data behind them.

[takeaway card]

Stop measuring height. Start measuring the bone.
