Reviewing bone age. Where the number actually comes from.

[cover card]

In mice, epiphyseal fusion happened at 3 weeks in phalanges and 4 weeks in metacarpals, tibia and femur still open at 12 weeks. Rats, same order: 12 and 16 weeks vs still open.

[(tldr so you can place it in your map)]

At one week the metacarpal growth rate was 49% of the tibial rate and proliferation per column was 46%. Every marker of growth plate senescence, the plate running out of divisions, was more advanced in the hand bones.

[the hand runs at half speed]

Wrist, knee and ankle imaged in 958 people aged 14 to 21. Pubertal stage correlated with fusion at every site, 0.514 to 0.598 in males.

[human data, 958 people, mri]

BMI correlated with plate maturity, 0.186 to 0.384. Physical activity: no significant correlation in males at any site, one very low correlation at the female wrist (0.095), nothing else.

[takeaway card]

Being heavier tracked faster maturation. Training did not.
