# Short Peptides as Signaling Molecules in Homeostasis Regulation (Khavinson & Solov'ev 2013)

A general historical/theoretical review of short-peptide biotechnology, tracing the field from Vincent du Vigneaud's 1953 synthesis of oxytocin (the first synthetic peptide) through the Russian peptide-bioregulator research tradition centered at the St. Petersburg Institute of Bioregulation and Gerontology since the 1970s.

> [!contradiction] Flagged as topically unrelated to this domain: a broad historical/theoretical introduction to peptide bioregulator research generally, not focused on steroid hormones or growth-plate biology. Part of the same research tradition as [[Peptide-Regulation-Chondrogenic-Stem-Cell-Differentiation|Linkova et al. 2023]] (the one on-topic paper from this group).
