# Systemic and Local Regulation of the Growth Plate: A Comprehensive Review (Van der Eerden et al. 2003)

A comprehensive Leiden-group review synthesizing both systemic (endocrine: GH, IGF-I, thyroid, estrogen, androgen) and local (paracrine: PTHrP/Ihh, FGF, BMP) regulation of growth-plate chondrocyte proliferation and differentiation — from the same research group as [[ERalpha-ERbeta-Expression-Rat-Epiphyseal-Plate-VanDerEerden|Van der Eerden et al.'s ERα/ERβ expression paper]], providing broader context for their specific findings.
