Your bone age. AND ALMOST NOBODY KNOWS THIS

[cover card]

gets treated as one number describing your whole skeleton. It is read from an X-ray of your hand and wrist.

[bone age]

senesce at different times. Growth plate senescence runs earlier in small bones.

[different bones]

from what you want. The bones read for bone age are the ones furthest along.

[which is backwards]

MRI of 958 people aged 14 to 21. Wrist, knee and ankle growth plates measured.

[human imaging]

Pubertal stage predicted fusion far better than age did. Correlation around 0.5 to 0.6 for stage.

[it also found]

between the sexes. Complete fusion happened about two years earlier in girls.

[and a two year gap]

A useful clinical estimate. Not a physical fact.

[so what is bone age]

GH and IGF-1 drive proliferation. They move height fast and bone age slowly.

[what actually sets it]

is never how tall you are now. It is how much proliferative capacity remains.

Same age. Completely different runway.
