# Aromatase Expression Directly Confirmed in Human Growth Plate Tissue

Directly detects aromatase transcripts (RT-PCR) and protein (immunohistochemistry) in human growth-plate specimens obtained during epiphysiodesis surgery (performed to intentionally halt growth in one leg to match the other) — with aromatase protein concentrated in the zone of maturation and hypertrophic zone, greatest in the hypertrophic zone specifically. This is direct human-tissue confirmation, at the precise anatomical location, of the local aromatization this domain's estrogen-driven-fusion model requires.

> [!key-insight] The hypertrophic-zone concentration of aromatase protein lines up precisely with [[ERalpha-ERbeta-Expression-Rat-Rabbit-Growth-Plate-Postnatal-Nilsson1999|Nilsson et al. 1999]]'s finding (same batch) that ER expression in the hypertrophic zone rises specifically just before fusion — together these two papers suggest the hypertrophic zone is where local estrogen synthesis (aromatase) and local estrogen reception (ER) converge to trigger fusion, rather than either function being distributed evenly across the growth plate.

## Relevance to This Domain

Direct human-tissue proof of local aromatase expression at the anatomically precise growth-plate zone this domain's fusion model requires, complementing [[Local-Estrogen-Production-Growth-Plate-Cartilage-Nilsson2001|Nilsson et al. 2001]]'s cell-line functional data with in vivo human localization.
