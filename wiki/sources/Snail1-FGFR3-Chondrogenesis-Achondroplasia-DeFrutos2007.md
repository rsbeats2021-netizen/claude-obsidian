# Snail1 as a Downstream FGFR3 Transcriptional Effector in Achondroplasia (de Frutos et al. 2007)

Identifies Snail1 as the specific transcription factor executing FGFR3 signaling's downstream effects in chondrogenesis and achondroplasia — supplying a mechanistic link between the FGFR3 gain-of-function mutation (already central to this domain via [[TYRA-300-FGFR3-Inhibitor-Chondrodysplasia|TYRA-300]] and [[Infigratinib-FGFR-Achondroplasia-Savarirayan2024|infigratinib]]) and the transcription-factor level of chondrocyte regulation covered in [[Molecular Regulators of Growth Plate Chondrocyte Maturation]].

## Relevance to This Domain
