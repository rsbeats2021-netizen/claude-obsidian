# GH Secretagogues Ipamorelin and GHRP-6 Increase Bone Mineral Content (Svensson et al.)

Extends this domain's GH-secretagogue literature ([[GHRP2-Sustained-GH-IGF1-Older-Adults-Bowers2004|Bowers et al. 2004]]'s GHRP-2 work, and the MK-677 mention already noted elsewhere) with a directly measured skeletal endpoint: 12 weeks of ipamorelin or GHRP-6 treatment increased bone mineral content in adult female rats via serial DXA measurement, comparably to direct GH administration — moving this domain's GH-secretagogue evidence from "raises GH/IGF-I levels" to "measurably improves a bone outcome."

## Relevance to This Domain

A skeletal-outcome data point for [[Growth Hormone-IGF-1 Axis]]'s GH-secretagogue thread, complementing [[GHRP2-Sustained-GH-IGF1-Older-Adults-Bowers2004|Bowers et al. 2004]] with a directly measured BMC endpoint rather than hormone-level elevation alone.
