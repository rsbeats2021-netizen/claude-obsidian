# Are Resting-Zone Chondrocytes Bona Fide Stem Cells? (Lui 2020)

A review by Julian Lui at NICHD's Section on Growth and Development — the same institutional lineage as Jeffrey Baron's lab, already central to this domain via [[Growth-Plate-Senescence-Epiphyseal-Fusion-Review|Nilsson & Baron 2004]] and [[Depletion-Resting-Zone-Chondrocytes-Growth-Plate-Senescence|Schrier et al. 2006]] — that directly poses and synthesizes the answer to the question implicitly running through this domain's whole [[Epiphyseal Skeletal Stem Cells and Hedgehog Signaling]] cluster: are the growth plate's resting-zone chondrocytes genuine stem cells? The review credits recent lineage-tracing and next-generation sequencing technology (the same methodological wave behind [[PTHrP-Resting-Chondrocytes-Wnt-Inhibitory-Environment|Hallett et al. 2021]] and [[Human-Growth-Plate-Transcriptional-Atlas-GH-Stem-Cells|Chu et al. 2025]], both already in this domain) with finally making the question answerable.

> [!key-insight] This review functions as an external, independent confirmation that this domain's epSSC cluster — built up incrementally across [[Skeletal-Stem-Cells-Growth-Plate-Hedgehog-Signaling|Trompet et al. 2024]], [[Hedgehog-Resting-Zone-Chondrocytes-Osteoblast-Transformation|Orikasa et al. 2023]], [[PTHrP-Resting-Chondrocytes-Wnt-Inhibitory-Environment|Hallett et al. 2021]], and [[Depletion-Resting-Zone-Chondrocytes-Growth-Plate-Senescence|Schrier et al. 2006]] — represents the actual current consensus shape of this research question in the field, not an idiosyncratic synthesis specific to this vault.

> [!gap] Read via abstract/introduction; the review's own specific conclusions about *which* signaling pathways are most established (beyond what's already covered) were not extracted in full.
