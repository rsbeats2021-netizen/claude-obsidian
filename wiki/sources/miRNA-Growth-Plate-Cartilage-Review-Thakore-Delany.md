# miRNA-Based Regulation in Growth Plate Cartilage: Review (Thakore & Delany)

Introduces an entirely new regulatory layer to this domain: microRNA (miRNA)-based post-transcriptional regulation of the same PTHrP-centered chondrocyte-maturation pathways already covered via [[PTHrP-Ihh Feedback Loop]] and [[Molecular Regulators of Growth Plate Chondrocyte Maturation]]. miRNAs modulate proliferation, differentiation, matrix synthesis, and hypertrophy gene networks, interacting with (rather than replacing) the transcription-factor and paracrine-signaling mechanisms already central to this domain.

> [!key-insight] This is the first source in this domain to address post-transcriptional (miRNA-mediated) regulation specifically — every other mechanistic source covers transcriptional (Sox9/Runx2/MEF2), paracrine (PTHrP/Ihh), or receptor-level (ER/AR/GPER-1) control. miRNA regulation is a genuinely distinct additional layer sitting between gene transcription and protein function.

## Relevance to This Domain

Adds an entirely new regulatory layer (post-transcriptional/miRNA) to [[PTHrP-Ihh Feedback Loop]] and [[Molecular Regulators of Growth Plate Chondrocyte Maturation]], complementing this domain's existing transcriptional and paracrine-signaling focus.
