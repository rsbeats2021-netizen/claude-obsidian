# Progesterone-Induced Local GH Secretion Drives Breast Stem/Progenitor Proliferation (Lombardi et al. 2014)

Adds an extrapituitary GH source to [[Growth Hormone-IGF-1 Axis]]: normal breast epithelium secretes its own GH locally in response to progesterone, and this locally-produced GH increases proliferation of breast tissue-resident stem/progenitor cells — a tissue-autonomous GH-production-and-action loop distinct from the pituitary-GH-to-target-tissue model this domain otherwise assumes.

> [!gap] Breast tissue/stem cell biology in an oncology-adjacent (Research Oncology department) research context; translation to growth-plate or other GH-responsive stem-cell populations already in this domain isn't established.

## Relevance to This Domain

A fourth tissue-distinct GH/stem-cell axis for [[Growth Hormone-IGF-1 Axis]] (alongside growth-plate epSSCs, circulating VSELs, and bone-marrow hematopoietic progenitors) — and the first showing GH can be locally produced by the target tissue itself rather than arriving purely via systemic circulation.
