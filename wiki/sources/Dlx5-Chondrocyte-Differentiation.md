# Dlx5 Positively Regulates Chondrocyte Hypertrophic Differentiation (Ferrari & Kosher 2002)

Using in vivo misexpression experiments in developing chick limbs, Ferrari & Kosher (2002) show that the homeobox transcription factor Dlx5 is expressed specifically in prehypertrophic/hypertrophic chondrocytes and functions as a positive driver of the transition from proliferative to hypertrophic chondrocyte state — forcing extra Dlx5 expression accelerated hypertrophic differentiation, while reducing it delayed the transition.

## Relevance to This Domain

One of three molecular-regulator papers (alongside [[Sox9-Growth-Plate-Chondrocyte-Fate|Dy et al. 2012]] and the general framework in [[Role-of-Growth-Plate-Longitudinal-Bone-Growth|Pines & Hurwitz]]) synthesized into the new concept [[Molecular Regulators of Growth Plate Chondrocyte Maturation]]. Complements rather than contradicts the other papers in that cluster — Dlx5 and Sox9 act at overlapping but distinguishable stages of chondrocyte maturation.
