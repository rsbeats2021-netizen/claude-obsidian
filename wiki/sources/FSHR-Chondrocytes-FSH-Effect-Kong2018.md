# FSH Receptor Expression and Direct FSH Effects on Chondrocytes (Kong et al. 2018)

Introduces a genuinely novel hormone-chondrocyte axis to this domain: follicle-stimulating hormone receptor (FSHR), conventionally understood as a gonad-specific receptor mediating pituitary control of ovarian/testicular function, is directly expressed in chondrocytes, and FSH itself has a measurable direct effect on chondrocyte behavior. This raises the possibility that FSH — already known to rise sharply at menopause and implicated in some bone-loss mechanisms independent of estrogen — could also act directly on cartilage, a mechanism this domain's estrogen/androgen/GH-centric framework hadn't previously considered.

## Relevance to This Domain

A novel gonadotropin-chondrocyte connection for [[Molecular Regulators of Growth Plate Chondrocyte Maturation]], extending this domain's hormone-chondrocyte literature beyond the usual estrogen/androgen/GH axes to include FSH directly.
