# Intermittent PTHrP Dosing Improves MSC Chondrogenesis, Reduces Hypertrophy (Fischer et al. 2015)

Applies a dosing-pattern principle already established for PTH in osteoporosis therapy (intermittent dosing is anabolic, continuous exposure is catabolic) to a different problem: human mesenchymal stromal cells (MSCs) intended for cartilage regeneration suffer from phenotype instability and premature hypertrophy, limiting their clinical use. Intermittent (rather than constant) PTHrP(1-34) exposure improves chondrogenic differentiation and reduces this premature hypertrophy — a practical dosing-pattern solution directly relevant to [[PTHrP-Ihh Feedback Loop|this domain's PTHrP/Ihh mechanism understanding]].
