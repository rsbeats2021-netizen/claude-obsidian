# Estrogen and Mechanical Occlusal Factors in Idiopathic Condylar Resorption

Complements [[DHT-Estradiol-TMJ-Osteoarthritis-Sex-Specific-Tomura|this domain's TMJ-osteoarthritis sex-hormone study]] with a related craniofacial-joint condition: idiopathic condylar resorption, studied in a rat model combining estrogen manipulation (via an ovarian insufficiency model) with mechanical occlusal interference — testing whether hormonal and mechanical factors interact to drive jaw-joint bone loss.
