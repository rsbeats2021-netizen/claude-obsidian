# Video script — Six months of low magnesium

145 words, ~48s at 178 wpm. Target band 45-90s.

Spoken lines only. Visual cue in brackets.

---

Six months of low magnesium. And the animals weighed the same as controls.

[cover card]

If body weight had dropped, this would just be malnutrition slowing everything down. The deficit was specific to cartilage.

[is the whole point]

Ten percent of nutrient requirement, for six months. Not an extreme deprivation model — a sustained partial shortfall.

[was moderate]

Disordered chondrocyte column formation, thinner growth plates, reduced articular chondrocyte density, and reduced extracellular matrix proteoglycan.

[was the architecture]

Sox9 immunolocalisation was reduced in both growth plate and articular chondrocytes — Sox9 being the master transcription factor for chondrocyte identity.

[points at the mechanism]

Magnesium insufficiency is common, cheap to correct, and carries no adverse-effect profile at dietary levels. Very little else in this topic has that combination.

[more than it gets]

Deficiency studies tell you what happens below the line. They are routinely quoted as if they showed benefit above it.

[takeaway card]

Below adequate is a problem. Above adequate is a separate question.
