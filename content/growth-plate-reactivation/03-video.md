Growth plate closure. Which means closure is maintained by conditions, not sealed permanently.

[cover card]

A scoliosis treatment. A flexible cord is fixed along one side of the spine so that side is mechanically constrained and the other keeps growing.

[is vertebral tethering]

In an animal model, tethering reactivated vertebral growth plate function. Plates that had gone dormant resumed activity under mechanical load.

[was reactivation]

Chondrocytes are mechanically responsive cells. Compression and tension change their gene expression, matrix production and proliferation directly, without any hormone involved.

[is mechanobiology]

An event happens once and is finished. A state is held in place by conditions and can change when they change.

[what closure is]

Mechanical loading is a real signalling input to cartilage, strong enough to change plate behaviour. That's true whether or not your plates are open, and it's the reason loading matters for bone at every age.

[takeaway card]

Force is a signal your cartilage is already listening to.
