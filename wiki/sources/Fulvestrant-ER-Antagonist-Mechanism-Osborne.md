# Fulvestrant: A Novel-Mechanism ER Antagonist (Osborne et al. 2004)

A third fulvestrant-in-oncology paper in this ingest batch, alongside [[Fulvestrant-Breast-Cancer-Review-Nathan-Schmid|Nathan & Schmid 2017]] and [[Fulvestrant-Immune-Response-Breast-Cancer-Dabrosin|Abrahamsson et al. 2020]]. This earlier (2004) paper provides the original mechanistic case for fulvestrant as a genuinely new drug class — a pure ER antagonist/degrader with no agonist effects, distinguishing it from tamoxifen and other SERMs.

> [!contradiction] Flagged as topically unrelated to this domain's growth-plate/bone focus, for the same reason as the other two fulvestrant papers: same drug and receptor as [[Fulvestrant-Precocious-Puberty-McCune-Albright|Sims et al. 2012]], but entirely an oncology mechanism/efficacy paper.
