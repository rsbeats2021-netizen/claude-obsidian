[cover card]

Given for an unrelated tumour. It blocks FGFR signalling broadly instead of targeting one receptor.

[a pan-fgfr inhibitor]

It's caused by a gain of function mutation in FGFR3. The receptor is stuck partly on, and constant FGFR3 signalling suppresses chondrocyte proliferation in the growth plate.

[start with achondroplasia]

Block FGFR broadly and you get overgrowth. The mutation that overactivates FGFR3 gives you the opposite.

[is the mirror image]

TYRA-300 and infigratinib are purpose built selective inhibitors. Infigratinib reached a 2024 NEJM trial with a matching mouse model behind it.

[three drugs are in trials]

Decades old, cheap, over the counter for motion sickness. It turned out to inhibit the same pathway and reached Phase 2 in Japan in kids with achondroplasia.

[is an old antihistamine]

These are treatments for a specific genetic skeletal dysplasia, tested in patients who have it. None of this is a height pathway for people without the mutation.

[takeaway card]
