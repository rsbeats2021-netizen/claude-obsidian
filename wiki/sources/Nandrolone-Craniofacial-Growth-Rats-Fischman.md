# Nandrolone Alters Craniofacial Growth in Rats

Extends this domain's anabolic-androgenic-steroid literature to a novel anatomical site: chronic nandrolone phenylpropionate administration in female Sprague-Dawley rats produced roughly 20% increased body weight alongside measurable changes in total skull length and other cephalometric parameters — demonstrating anabolic-steroid growth effects are not confined to long-bone growth plates but extend to craniofacial skeletal development, with implications for facial-growth risk in adolescent anabolic-steroid misuse.
