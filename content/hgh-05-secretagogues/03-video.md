GH secretagogues. Day one is not day thirty.

[cover card]

They ran GHRP-2 as a continuous 30-day subcutaneous infusion in healthy older men and women, and measured pulsatile GH secretion at three points: day 1, day 14 and day 30. On day 1 the infusion stimulated pulsatile GH secretion by more than 3 fold versus baseline.

[thirty days of ghrp-2]

By day 14 and again at day 30, the same infusion in the same people was driving GH by more than 1.8 fold. Still statistically significant, still genuinely working, and roughly 40 percent smaller than it was two weeks earlier.

[the fade]

What they genuinely do is produce pulsatile GH, which from the Isgaard and Bick data is the pattern the growth plate and muscle actually respond to. So the mechanism is right and the size of the effect shrinks by about 40 percent within two weeks and then stabilises there.

[what they do and do not do]

So judge one at week four, never at week one, because week one flatters it by roughly 40 percent and that gap is a receptor property rather than anything you can dose around. And expect the bone effect to show up as size rather than density, because size is what was actually measured.

[takeaway card]

Judge a secretagogue at week four.
