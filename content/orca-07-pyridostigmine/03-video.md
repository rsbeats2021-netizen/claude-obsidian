# Video script — Pyridostigmine

CHOLINESTERASE INHIBITOR · ORAL · HUMAN GH-AXIS PROBE

---

Pyridostigmine. It doubled the GH response to exercise.

[cover card]

The same exercise after pyridostigmine gave 587.3, P < 0.005. Peak GH went from 6.5 to 13.5 µg/l, P < 0.01.

[cappa 1993]

Spoudeas gave two GHRH stimuli 90 minutes apart on a pyridostigmine background. It enhanced the first GH response with an increased release rate.

[and this is the catch]

In that same experiment, the second GH responses were not augmented. Same background, same session, 90 minutes later, and the benefit was gone.

[the real limit]

Marcell ran it in young and old men. It raised GH during maximal exercise, 20.9 against 4.9, and submaximal, 12.3 against 4.7, both P < 0.05.

[the second limit]

These are dose-related and they are the reason the research protocols use modest oral doses rather than pushing it. The GH studies above used 30 and 60 mg orally.

[the practical one]

If it only improves the pulse it is timed to, then the entire mitigation is placement rather than frequency. Around 70% of daily GH comes out in early sleep when somatostatin has already withdrawn, which is the window where the brake is lowest to begin with.

[what offsets it]

Every human study quoted here uses pyridostigmine as a research probe, to measure how much somatostatin is currently holding GH down in a given person. That is what the pyridostigmine test is for.

[what this actually is]

The best-evidenced somatostatin lever available, in humans, at an oral dose, with a doubling of exercise GH measured directly. Treat it as a timing tool rather than a daily compound.

[takeaway card]

A real lever with one window per pull.
