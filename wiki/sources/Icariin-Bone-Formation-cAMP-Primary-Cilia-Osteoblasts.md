# Icariin Promotes Bone Formation via Primary-Cilia cAMP Signaling in Osteoblasts

A novel natural-compound mechanism for this domain's cAMP-signaling cluster: icariin, a flavonol glycoside from Epimedium (traditionally known as "horny goat weed"), promotes bone formation in growing rats by activating cAMP signaling specifically within osteoblast primary cilia — a specific subcellular organelle location this domain's other cAMP-chondrocyte papers ([[Induction-Increased-cAMP-Chondroprotective-Karsdal2007|Karsdal]], [[cAMP-ECM-Gene-Expression-Rat-Chondrocytes-Rockel2009|Rockel]]) hadn't identified.

## Relevance to This Domain

A novel botanical-compound (icariin) and specific subcellular mechanism (primary-cilia cAMP signaling) for this domain's growing cAMP-in-bone/cartilage-signaling cluster.
