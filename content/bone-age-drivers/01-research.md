# Research brief — What actually drives bone age

## Core claim
Bone-age progression is driven by five hormone axes acting together. Single-
hormone explanations keep producing wrong predictions because they describe one
input to a multi-input system.

## Key points (tier-tagged, all Tier 1 — clinical review synthesis)
- GH/IGF-1 — drive proliferation and growth velocity; move height fast and bone
  age comparatively slowly.
- Thyroid hormone — required for normal chondrocyte maturation; sets tempo.
- Estrogens — dominant driver of skeletal maturation and eventual fusion.
- Androgens — increase growth velocity **and** accelerate skeletal maturation
  simultaneously. The central tradeoff of this whole domain.
- Bone age vs chronological age — bone age indexes remaining growth capacity;
  chronological age doesn't.

## Editing note
Slide 8's claim — that anything accelerating growth velocity via androgens also
