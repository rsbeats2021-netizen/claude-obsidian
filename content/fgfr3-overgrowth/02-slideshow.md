## Notes
- Slide 4 carries the deck. One axis, two opposing arrows — get that right and
  every other slide is supporting material.
- Slide 6 is the retention beat. "A motion sickness pill and a bespoke oncology
  molecule aiming at the same receptor" is the most shareable line here.
- Slide 8 framing is **required**, per `01-research.md`. This must not read as
  a height-enhancement pathway for people without the mutation.
- Pairs with `resting-zone-stem-cells` — the catalogue surfaced a paper on
  FGFR3 disrupting resting-zone chondrocyte turnover via CREB, which is a
  direct mechanistic bridge between the two decks.
