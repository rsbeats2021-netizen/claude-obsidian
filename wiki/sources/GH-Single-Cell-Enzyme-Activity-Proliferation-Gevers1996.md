# Single-Cell Enzyme Activity and Proliferation: GH Effects (Gevers et al. 1996)

A 1996 Journal of Bone and Mineral Research paper from Evelien Gevers' group measuring enzyme activity at single-cell resolution across growth-plate zones in response to GH — methodologically notable as an early precursor to the single-cell approaches ([[Human-Growth-Plate-Transcriptional-Atlas-GH-Stem-Cells|Chu et al. 2025]]'s scRNA-seq, [[PTHrP-Resting-Chondrocytes-Wnt-Inhibitory-Environment|Hallett et al. 2021]]'s label-retention flow cytometry) that now dominate this domain's most recent growth-plate stem-cell literature.
