# High-Dose rhGH During Puberty Increases Near-Final Height: RCT (Mauras et al. 2000)

A major randomized, multicenter trial (Genentech Cooperative Study Group) testing whether high-dose rhGH treatment specifically timed to puberty — matching the natural amplitude-modulated increase in endogenous GH secretion during puberty — improves near-final height outcomes in GH-deficient patients compared to standard dosing. This is one of the higher-quality clinical-trial-level sources in this domain's GH-height literature, directly informing dosing-strategy questions for pubertal GH-deficient patients.

## Relevance to This Domain

A high-quality randomized clinical trial for [[Growth Hormone-IGF-1 Axis]]'s height-outcome literature, directly testing a dosing strategy (high-dose, pubertally-timed) against this domain's broader GH-therapy efficacy questions.
