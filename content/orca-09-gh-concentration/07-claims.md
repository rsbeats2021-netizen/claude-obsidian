## MODEL
Sprague-Dawley rats, GnRHa (triptorelin) slow-release from 2 weeks of age,
repeated every 2 weeks, to 8 weeks old. Chondrocytes from the tibial growth plate
by trypsin-collagenase digestion, monolayer culture. So: **sex-hormone-suppressed
pubertal rat growth plate chondrocytes.**

## TIME COURSE — Table 1
Proliferation rose to day 4 and then fell: "以第 4 天时的促增殖效应最强" (strongest
at day 4). From day 5 the effect weakened; day 7 vs day 4 was significant, P < 0.01.
PCNA followed the same shape, peak at day 4.
→ Continuous GH exposure has a time optimum as well as a concentration optimum.

## EGF, THE PAPER'S ACTUAL SUBJECT
EGF mRNA rose from 6 h, peaked at **18 h**, then fell significantly by 24 and 36 h
(each vs 18 h, P < 0.05). Dose-response at 18 h: 50 ng/mL the first significant
concentration, **peak at 100 ng/mL**, declining above, though 1000 remained above
control. EGF peptide by ELISA peaked at 23 h.

## HOW THIS SITS WITH THE OTHER IN VITRO WORK
- Lindahl 1986 (rat chondrocyte colony formation, agarose suspension): threshold
  10 ng/mL, **maximal 40 ng/mL**, reduced at 80 and 160, no effect at 160.
- Maor 1989 (hGH on chondrogenesis, [3H]thymidine, 20-200 ng/mL tested):
  **maximal 25-50 ng/mL**.
- Pan 2010 (MTT + PCNA, monolayer, GnRHa-suppressed pubertal): **maximal 100 ng/mL**.
All three find an inverted U. The peak differs by endpoint, culture system and
model. Pan's is the sex-hormone-suppressed pubertal model and the widest sampled
range.

## WHAT THIS PAPER DOES NOT SAY
It reports nothing about human serum GH concentrations and nothing about any
human dose in IU or mg. Any conversion from "100 ng/mL in culture medium" to a
human injection dose is not in this paper and is not supported by it.
Two specific reasons a conversion is not straightforward:
1. The growth plate is avascular and diffusion-limited, so the concentration
   reaching a chondrocyte is not the serum concentration.
2. Culture medium has no GH-binding-protein buffering or hepatic clearance
   equivalent to the intact animal.
