# Video script — Epitalon

KHAVINSON TETRAPEPTIDE (AEDG) · TELOMERASE · PRIMATE DOSE DATA

Epitalon. The one with a mechanism that gets worse the longer you run it.

[cover card]

Same-study, same-species head to head comparisons put Epitalon at roughly 100 to 1000 times the potency of Epithalamin. In rhesus monkeys, 10 µg per animal per day of Epitalon matched 5 mg per animal per day of Epithalamin, a 500-fold gap.

[than its parent]

Epitalon extends telomeres in normal cells via hTERT. In breast cancer cell lines 21NT and BT474 it does it by a different route, alternative lengthening of telomeres, and it does so dose-dependently.

[the one that matters]

All claimed relevance is extrapolated from adult, aged or ovariectomised models. And its human dosing follows the family tradition of pulsed 10 to 30 day courses, with the longest documented programme being Epithalamin given as 6 courses over 3 years.

[the same gap]

The claim that a four residue peptide binds DNA with sequence specificity is considered biologically implausible, since conventional transcription factors need 30 to 60 or more residues for that. No independent laboratory has replicated the core mechanism, and the efficacy literature has not been replicated outside Russia.

[the family problem]

If you run it, the documented pattern is pulsed courses of 10 to 30 days with long gaps, not continuous daily use. Every human regimen located across this family follows that shape.

[what the tradition actually is]

The most specific and testable mechanism in its family, with rare primate dose data and a clear potency ratio against its parent compound. It is also the only one with a mechanism that gets more concerning with more exposure.

[takeaway card]

Real mechanism. Wrong direction risk. Keep it short.
