### Vocabulary (`concepts/vocabulary/`)

Critical pharmacological terms that recur across multiple sources. Each page explains the term and lists every source that mentions it, with a specific note on *how* that source uses/discusses it — not just that it's mentioned.

- [[Aromatase]] — the androgen-to-estrogen enzyme; 7 sources across bone, brain, and avian physiology (developing)
- [[Androgen Receptor (AR)]] — direct androgen signaling, contested at the growth plate; 6 sources (developing)
- [[Dihydrotestosterone (DHT)]] — the non-aromatizable androgen; the domain's most cross-referenced term, 8 sources (developing)
- [[Estrogen Receptor (ER)]] — ERα/ERβ; the one term bridging the Endocrinology and Health and Nutrition domains, 4 sources (developing)
- [[5-Alpha-Reductase]] — the testosterone-to-DHT enzyme; 3 sources (developing)
- [[Sox9]] — master chondrocyte transcription factor; 3 sources (developing)
- [[Runx2]] — hypertrophic-transition transcription factor; 3 sources (developing)
- [[Growth Hormone-IGF-1 Axis]] — the systemic growth driver; 4 sources (developing)
- [[Testosterone]] — the parent androgen molecule; 9 sources (developing)
- [[PTHrP-Ihh Feedback Loop]] — the paracrine circuit governing chondrocyte maturation pace; 4 sources (developing)
- [[GPER-1 (GPR30)]] — the transmembrane estrogen receptor that drives proliferation instead of closure; 3 sources (developing)
- [[Kartogenin]] — small-molecule cartilage-regeneration compound; 2 sources (developing)
- [[FGFR3]] — the achondroplasia gene, with three independent drug strategies now tracked; 8 sources (developing)
- [[OPG-RANKL Axis]] — the osteoclast-regulating bone-remodeling pathway multiple hormones converge on; 3 sources (developing)
- [[HDAC (Histone Deacetylases) in Chondrocytes]] — isoform-specific epigenetic regulation of chondrocyte behavior; 4 sources (developing)
- [[cAMP Signaling in Chondrocytes and Osteoblasts]] — a context-dependent second messenger, protective or destructive depending on trigger; 5 sources (developing)
- [[Osteolectin]] — a novel juvenile growth-plate proliferation factor with human genetic backing; 1 source (developing)
- [[Catecholamines and Beta2-Adrenergic Receptor]] — the first sympathetic-nervous-system link to growth-plate biology; 1 source (developing)
- [[DHT Esters — Dosing and Concentrations]] — every published dose and the only two mg/mL concentrations for DHT heptanoate/enanthate and DHT propionate; ester mass-fraction arithmetic; 9 sources (developing)
