# IGF-1 Rescues FGFR3-Mutant Chondrogenic Cells from Apoptosis (Koike et al. 2003)

A direct mechanistic bridge between two of this domain's central pathways: IGF-1 (a key mediator of GH, per [[Growth Hormone-IGF-1 Axis]]) rescues chondrogenic cells expressing the achondroplasia-causing FGFR3(G380R) mutation from apoptosis via PI3K and MAPK signaling — suggesting GH/IGF-1 axis manipulation could theoretically complement FGFR3-targeted drugs like [[TYRA-300-FGFR3-Inhibitor-Chondrodysplasia|TYRA-300]] by protecting chondrocytes from the mutation's apoptotic consequences through an entirely separate pathway.

## Relevance to This Domain

A mechanistic bridge between [[Growth Hormone-IGF-1 Axis]] and [[TYRA-300-FGFR3-Inhibitor-Chondrodysplasia|this domain's FGFR3/achondroplasia drug-development thread]], via IGF-1's apoptosis-rescue effect on FGFR3-mutant cells.
