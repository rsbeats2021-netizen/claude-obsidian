# Growth factors of the growth plate

> **Scope.** Signalling molecules with a direct, evidenced action on chondrocytes in the epiphyseal plate. Excludes systemic hormones covered elsewhere ([[GH-IGF-1 Axis]]) except where they act locally. Every entry carries an evidence tier; most of this family is tier C, and the page says so rather than hiding it.

## 1. IGF-1
**What it is.** 70-aa peptide, mostly hepatic in circulation, also produced locally in the plate.
**Where it acts.** Proliferative and hypertrophic zones, via IGF-1R.
**Evidence.** Tier B for the systemic axis in humans; tier C for local action. Central to growth; the mediator of most GH effect. · [[GH-IGF-1 Axis]]

## 2. FGF / FGFR3
**What it is.** Fibroblast growth factor family signalling through FGFR3.
**Where it acts.** Proliferative zone. **Negative** regulator — activating mutations shorten bone.
**Evidence.** Tier A in humans by genetics: activating FGFR3 mutations cause achondroplasia. The clearest single gene-to-height link in the family.
**Niche relevance.** Heavily discussed; 8 posts, 237,319 views · [[fgfr3-and-plate-closure]]

## 3. PTHrP
**What it is.** Parathyroid hormone-related peptide.
**Where it acts.** Keeps chondrocytes proliferating and **delays** hypertrophy.
**Evidence.** Tier C. Forms a feedback loop with IHH that sets the pace of the whole plate.

## 4. Indian hedgehog (IHH)
**What it is.** Hedgehog-family morphogen from pre-hypertrophic chondrocytes.
**Where it acts.** Drives proliferation and induces PTHrP — the other half of the loop.
**Evidence.** Tier C. Loss collapses the columnar architecture. · [[Epiphyseal Skeletal Stem Cells and Hedgehog Signaling]]

## 5. BMPs
**What it is.** Bone morphogenetic proteins, TGF-beta superfamily.
**Where it acts.** Promote chondrocyte differentiation and hypertrophy.
**Evidence.** Tier C. Opposed by FGF signalling; the balance sets differentiation rate.

## 6. VEGF
**What it is.** Vascular endothelial growth factor.
**Where it acts.** Released by hypertrophic chondrocytes to invite vessel invasion at the ossification front.
**Evidence.** Tier C. The link between the avascular plate and the vascularised bone replacing it. · [[growth-plate-hypoxia]]

## 7. TGF-beta
**What it is.** Transforming growth factor beta.
**Where it acts.** Generally restrains terminal hypertrophy.
**Evidence.** Tier C. Context-dependent; direction varies by zone and concentration.

## 8. CNP (NPR-B)
**What it is.** C-type natriuretic peptide signalling through NPR-B.
**Where it acts.** Promotes proliferation and matrix production; **antagonises FGFR3**.
**Evidence.** Tier A in humans via the CNP-analogue achondroplasia trials — the family's strongest translational result.

## Comparison
| Member | Net effect on length | Acts on | Strongest evidence | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| IGF-1 | increase | prolif + hypertrophic | human axis data | B |
| FGF/FGFR3 | **decrease** | proliferative | human genetics | A |
| PTHrP | increase (delays exit) | proliferative | mouse | C |
| IHH | increase | proliferative | mouse | C |
| BMPs | increase differentiation | all zones | mouse | C |
| VEGF | enables ossification | hypertrophic | mouse | C |
| TGF-beta | restrains hypertrophy | context | mouse | C |
| CNP | increase | proliferative | human trials | A |

## Gaps
- Only **FGFR3** and **CNP** have tier A human evidence. Everything else is mechanistic or animal work.
- Local versus circulating IGF-1 contributions are not cleanly separated in humans.
- No member of this family has a demonstrated intervention that raises final height in a non-deficient child.
