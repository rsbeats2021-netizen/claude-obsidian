# Direct Sex Steroid Action on Rabbit Cartilage Cells (Corvol et al. 1987)

An early (1987) foundational demonstration of direct sex-steroid effects on cartilage cell metabolism across fetal-to-postnatal ages and both sexes — a precursor finding to [[Sex-Steroid-Regulation-Cartilage-Bone-Proliferation|Somjen et al. 1991]]'s later, more detailed direct-androgen/estrogen cartilage work already in this domain.

## Relevance to This Domain

A foundational precursor to [[Sex-Steroid-Regulation-Cartilage-Bone-Proliferation|Somjen et al. 1991]] and [[Estrogen vs Androgen in Male Bone Health]]'s direct-cartilage-action evidence cluster.
