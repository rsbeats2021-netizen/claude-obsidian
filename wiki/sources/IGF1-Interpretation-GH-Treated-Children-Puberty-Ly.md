# Interpreting IGF-1 in GH-Treated Children During Early Puberty (Ly et al.)

A clinically important caveat for [[Growth Hormone-IGF-1 Axis]]'s use as a treatment-monitoring biomarker: serum IGF-1 interpretation becomes genuinely confounded during early puberty in GH-treated children, since endogenous pubertal hormonal changes shift the IGF-1 baseline independent of GH dosing itself.

## Relevance to This Domain

A clinical-interpretation caveat for [[Growth Hormone-IGF-1 Axis]]'s use as a biomarker, relevant to any of this domain's sources using IGF-1 levels to gauge GH therapy effects in pubertal populations.
