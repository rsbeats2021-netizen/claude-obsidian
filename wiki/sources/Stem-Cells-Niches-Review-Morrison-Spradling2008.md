# Stem Cells and Niches: General Review (Morrison & Spradling 2008)

A foundational, general (not growth-plate-specific) review of stem-cell niche biology — the mechanisms by which tissue microenvironments maintain stem-cell populations throughout life. Provides the broader conceptual framework this domain's growth-plate-specific epSSC niche literature ([[Epiphyseal Skeletal Stem Cells and Hedgehog Signaling]]) operates within.

## Relevance to This Domain

General stem-cell-niche-biology background for [[Epiphyseal Skeletal Stem Cells and Hedgehog Signaling]]'s growth-plate-specific niche findings.
