# Claims ledger — aas-jaw-growth

Source: Gebhardt A, Pancherz H. "The effect of anabolic steroids on mandibular
growth." Am J Orthod Dentofacial Orthop 2003;123:435-40.
PDF: `.raw/Studies/Androgen-Testosterone-DHT/The Effect of Anabolic Steroids on Mandibular Growth - Gebhardt et al - 1990.pdf`

## slide 2
CLAIM: 70 day study, juvenile and adult Wistar-Kyoto rats, cephalometry at 300%
QUOTE: "Juvenile (n 16) and adult (n 16) inbred female Wistar-Kyoto rats were compared... Lateral headfilms taken before and after the 70-day study period were analyzed." / "digitized, scanned, and enlarged to 300% to increase the precision of the measurements."
SOURCE: Gebhardt & Pancherz 2003, abstract + methods

## slide 3
CLAIM: juvenile length +27.2% vs +24.5%; height +41.2% vs +32.0%
QUOTE: "Mandibular length (variable 2) increased by an average of 27.2% (15.8 mm) in the juvenile E animals and by an average of 24.5% (14.0 mm) in the C animals." / "Mandibular height (variable 3) increased by an average of 10.2 mm (41.2%) in the juvenile E animals and by an average of 8.1 mm (32.0%) in the C animals."
SOURCE: Gebhardt & Pancherz 2003, results

## slide 4
CLAIM: adult length +4.7% (3.6mm) vs +0.3% (0.2mm)
QUOTE: "The adult E and C animals showed an increase in mandibular length of 4.7% (3.6 mm) and 0.3% (0.2 mm), respectively."
SOURCE: Gebhardt & Pancherz 2003, results

## slide 5
CLAIM: significant effect in both juvenile and adult animals
QUOTE: "It was found that the anabolic steroid (Deca-Durabolin) had a significant effect on mandibular growth in both juvenile and adult rats."
SOURCE: Gebhardt & Pancherz 2003, abstract conclusion

## slide 6
CLAIM: adult IGF-1 higher in treated; serum rise 383% vs 255%
QUOTE: "The IGF-I blood serum levels were similar in the juvenile E and C rats but higher in the adult E animals than in the adult C animals." / "This level was increased by the anabolic steroids (E group, 383.1%; C group, 254.7%)"
SOURCE: Gebhardt & Pancherz 2003, abstract + results

## slide 7
CLAIM: both cephalometric distances increased more in treated, both age groups
QUOTE: "In the juvenile animals during the examination period, the 2 cephalometric distances (variables 2 and 3) increased more in the E group than in the C group" / "Also in the adult animals during the study period, the 2 distances increased more in the E group than in the C group"
SOURCE: Gebhardt & Pancherz 2003, discussion

## CORRECTION LOG
The previous version of this deck said the study showed mandibular growth was
"altered, not increased" and "not improved". That was wrong. The paper reports
increases in both dimensions, in both age groups, and concludes a significant
effect. The error came from writing the deck off the vault's one-line summary
("altered mandibular growth") instead of the PDF. Posted at 20k+ views, and
viewers correctly read it as denying that AAS change craniofacial structure.
