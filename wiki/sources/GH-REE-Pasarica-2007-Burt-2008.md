# GH Raises REE by Roughly 130-175 kcal/day, Dose-Dependently

Two trials bracket the growth hormone answer, one in non-deficient men at supraphysiological dose, one as a dose-response in deficiency.

## Pasarica 2007 — supraphysiological GH, non-deficient men

Double-blind, placebo-controlled, 6 months, 30 non-diabetic middle-aged men with central adiposity (BMI > 27, waist > 102 cm) and low IGF-1, followed by 6 months blinded follow-up.

| Outcome | Change |
|---|---|
| **Resting energy expenditure** | **+172.5 ± 41.6 kcal/24 h** |
| Lean body mass | +2.5 ± 0.6 kg |
| Visceral adiposity | −8.8% |
| Fasting insulin, glucose, insulin resistance | **all increased** |

After withdrawal the fat and visceral-fat effects vanished, while weight stayed above baseline. The authors' verdict: supraphysiological GH is *not* an effective treatment here.

## Burt 2008 — dose-response in GH deficiency

16 GH-deficient adults randomised to 3 µg/kg/day (n=6) or 6 µg/kg/day (n=10) for 12 weeks:

| Dose | REE change | Fat mass | Lean mass |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 µg/kg/day | −28 ± 41 kcal/d | ns | ns |
| 6 µg/kg/day | **+132 ± 36 kcal/d** (P = 0.01) | −1.4 kg (P = 0.005) | +2.2 kg (P = 0.01) |

Leucine oxidation fell maximally in the higher-dose group, and the 2-week change in leucine oxidation predicted the later lean-mass gain.

## Comparison with the androgens

GH's REE effect is **larger per kg of lean mass gained** than testosterone's: Pasarica got +172 kcal/day on only +2.5 kg lean, which is ~69 kcal per kg lean added — far above muscle's 13 kcal/kg/day. GH is therefore doing something beyond adding tissue: driving lipolysis and fat oxidation, which is itself metabolically costly, and the futile cycling of free fatty acid release and re-esterification burns energy.

This makes GH the one drug in this set with a genuine **direct** thermogenic contribution, in contrast to the androgens ([[Santosa-2010-Sex-Steroid-Withdrawal-REE]], [[Strawford-1999-Nandrolone-REE-Nitrogen-Balance]]).

> [!gap] Both trials used medical doses. Neither approaches the 4-10 IU/day used in bodybuilding, where insulin resistance becomes the dominant clinical issue. No REE measurement exists at those doses.

> [!gap] Two further trials in the corpus (Wright 2024 in post-COVID patients; Malpani 2025 in short-stature boys) found **no** significant REE change on GH. The REE effect is not universal — it appears at adequate dose in subjects with fat to mobilise.
