# GH / IGF-1 axis

> GHRH drives pulsatile GH from the pituitary; GH drives hepatic IGF-1; IGF-1 acts at the growth plate and feeds back to suppress its own upstream signal.

## Upstream inputs
- **GHRH** from the hypothalamus — stimulatory, pulsatile
- **Somatostatin** — inhibitory; the interplay of the two sets pulse shape, not just amplitude
- **Ghrelin** — secretagogue at GHS-R
- **Slow-wave sleep** — the largest single pulse of the 24h cycle is tied to the first N3 block · [[gh-pulse-front-loaded]]

## The cascade
1. **Hypothalamus → GHRH.** Release is episodic. The axis is built around pulses; a flat signal is a different input.
2. **Pituitary somatotrophs → GH.** GH enters circulation in bursts.
3. **GH receptor dimerisation.** GH binds a receptor pair; the pair apposes and activates **JAK2**.
4. **JAK2 → STAT5b.** Phosphorylated STAT5b translocates and transcribes **IGF-1**.
5. **Hepatic IGF-1 → circulation.** Most circulating IGF-1 is liver-derived.
6. **IGF-1 at the growth plate.** Acts on proliferative and hypertrophic chondrocytes.
7. **Negative feedback.** Circulating IGF-1 suppresses pituitary GH release. The axis regulates itself, which is why it resists being pushed.

## Downstream effects
Longitudinal bone growth · protein synthesis · lipolysis · insulin antagonism at high exposure.

## Cross-talk
- **Sex steroids** — oestrogen amplifies the axis at puberty and separately drives plate closure · [[Epiphyseal Fusion Mechanisms]]
- **Thyroid** — permissive; hypothyroidism blunts growth independent of GH
- **Nutrition** — IGF-1 falls in energy deficit even with normal GH. A common reason a "normal GH" result misleads.
- **SOCS2** — induced by GH signalling, terminates it. Continuous exposure causes desensitisation; pulsatility preserves sensitivity.

## Where it is measured
| Analyte | What it actually reflects | Caution |
|---|---|---|
| Random serum GH | almost nothing | pulsatile, so a single draw is noise |
| IGF-1 | integrated GH exposure over days | falls with undernutrition regardless of GH |
| IGFBP-3 | GH-dependent carrier | age-referenced |
| Stimulation test | pituitary reserve | the actual diagnostic |

**The vault's own note:** serum IGF-1 tells you what the liver did, not what reached the plate. The plate is avascular · [[plate-hypoxia-implies-intervention]]

## Known modulators
| Modulator | Effect on axis | Tier | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Slow-wave sleep | raises the largest pulse | B | free, and the largest lever · [[gh-pulse-front-loaded]] |
| Energy deficit | lowers IGF-1 | B | decouples GH from IGF-1 |
| Exogenous GH, non-deficient | no final-height gain in trials | B | Kawai 1997, Bierich 1992, Kaplowitz 1995 |
| Continuous vs pulsatile GH | continuous desensitises via SOCS2 | C | pattern matters, not only dose |
