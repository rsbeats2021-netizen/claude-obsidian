# Nandrolone Produces Large Nitrogen Retention With Zero REE Change (Strawford et al. 1999)

The only controlled human trial that measures resting energy expenditure on nandrolone, and it is a metabolic-ward study with directly measured nitrogen balance — a tightly controlled design.

## Numbers

| Arm | Dose | Nitrogen balance | REE |
|---|---|---|---|
| Placebo | — | −11 g N/week | unchanged |
| Low | 65 mg/week | +33 to 52 g N / 14 days | **unchanged** |
| High | 200 mg/week | +33 to 52 g N / 14 days | **unchanged** |

Nitrogen retention equates to **0.5-0.9 kg lean tissue gained per week**. De novo lipogenesis fell (borderline, P < 0.06). Endogenous testosterone and gonadotropins were suppressed, as expected.

## The dissociation this establishes

Over 21 days, nandrolone drove lean tissue accrual at a rate of roughly 0.7 kg/week while resting energy expenditure did not budge. Mechanistically this is the same conclusion as [[Santosa-2010-Sex-Steroid-Withdrawal-REE]] reached from the withdrawal side, arrived at from the administration side with a different molecule.

It also means the REE rise in [[Welle-1992-Testosterone-BMR-Lean-Mass]] is a **lagging** consequence of accumulated tissue, not a concurrent pharmacological effect. Over 21 days there is not yet enough new tissue to show up in REE; over 3 months there is.

> [!gap] Three weeks is too short for the lean-mass-driven REE rise to appear even if the mechanism is real. This study does not show nandrolone never raises REE — it shows nandrolone does not raise it *directly or quickly*. Also n=4 in the low-dose arm.

> [!gap] No study in the corpus measures REE on nandrolone at bodybuilding doses (600-1,000+ mg/week) or for longer than 12 weeks.

## Relevance to This Domain

Directly answers the nandrolone part of the question: **no measurable resting-metabolic-rate increase**, with the caveat on duration. Any TDEE gain is downstream of the lean mass.
