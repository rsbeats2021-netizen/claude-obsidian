Your IGF-1 is higher. Three studies. Same dose, different pattern.

[cover card]

Continuous infusion at 12 mU per day gave no net gain in body weight at all, while the same dose given in a pulsatile manner produced significant body growth, P < 0.05. Analysis of variance confirmed pulsatile was significantly more effective than continuous.

[the first study]

A pulsatile pattern was more effective than continuous at stimulating longitudinal bone growth, P < 0.01. In skeletal muscle, pulsatile GH at 3-hour intervals induced a 3 to 5 fold increase in IGF-1 mRNA, while continuous treatment exerted no effect whatsoever.

[the second study]

Here is the part that explains the mechanism. In that same study there was no significant difference between pulsatile and continuous GH on hepatic IGF-1 mRNA.

[but the plate does]

They wrote it themselves, and it is the cleanest sentence in this whole series: receptor, GH-BP, and IGF-I are stimulated more by continuous GH delivery, whereas growth is enhanced more by pulsatility. What this means is that serum IGF-1 is not a scoreboard for growth.

[their own summary]

Lundberg 2015 randomised children to the same daily GH dose given once daily or split into two injections, 67 µg/kg once versus 33 µg/kg twice. That is a real human randomised result and it has to be dealt with rather than ignored.

[what it actually tested]

Three things survive. Delivery pattern changed growth independently of total dose, repeatedly, in multiple labs.

[takeaway card]

Stop reading IGF-1 as your growth number.
