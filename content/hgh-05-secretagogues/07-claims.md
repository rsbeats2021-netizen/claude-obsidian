# Claims — hgh-05-secretagogues
## Bowers et al. 2004, JCEM — 30-day continuous sc GHRP-2, healthy older adults
`.raw/.../Sustained Elevation of Pulsatile GH Secretion and IGF-I, IGFBP-3, IGFBP-5 - Bowers et al - 2004.pdf`
QUOTE: "The 30-d delivery of GHRP-2 (1; n = 17 subjects): 1) stimulated pulsatile,
rhythmic, and entropic GH secretion by more than 3-fold on d 1 and more than
1.8-fold on d 14 and 30 (each P < 0.001 vs. baseline)"
QUOTE: "Acute administration of a high dose of GHRP down-regulates homologous
responsiveness of the somatotropic axis in all mammalian species studied (20-24)."
→ Day 1 >3x, day 14 and 30 >1.8x. Still significant, roughly 40% smaller.
NOTE kept honest on the slide: the paper's own summary is that 30 days "sustains a
physiologically activated somatotropic axis" — it did not stop working. The deck
reports both the sustain and the decline.
