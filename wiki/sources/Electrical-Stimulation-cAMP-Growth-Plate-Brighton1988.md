# Electrical Stimulation Increases cAMP in Growth-Plate Chondrocytes (Brighton & Townsend 1988)

Shows capacitively-coupled electrical stimulation increases both cAMP production and chondrocyte proliferation in bovine growth-plate tissue — a biophysical/electrical route to the same cAMP pathway [[PTHrP-HDAC4-MEF2-Chondrocyte-Hypertrophy-Kozhemyakina2009|Kozhemyakina et al. 2009]] and other sources trigger hormonally (via PTHrP or forskolin).

## Relevance to This Domain

Adds an electrical-stimulation route to [[PTHrP-Ihh Feedback Loop]]'s cAMP-signaling mechanisms, distinct from but converging on the same second-messenger pathway as hormonal triggers.
