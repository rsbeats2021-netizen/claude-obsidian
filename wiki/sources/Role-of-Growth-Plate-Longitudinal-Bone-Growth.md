# The Role of the Growth Plate in Longitudinal Bone Growth (Pines & Hurwitz review)

A general review of growth-plate physiology, covering the three-zone structure of the plate (resting, proliferative, hypertrophic zones), the GH-IGF-I axis as the principal systemic growth driver, local second-messenger signaling (cAMP/cGMP), the role of vitamin D metabolites in matrix calcification, and gonadal steroids (chiefly estrogen) as the trigger for eventual growth-plate closure.

Functions in this vault mainly as background/context connective tissue for the more specific molecular and hormonal papers in this domain rather than presenting a single novel claim of its own.
