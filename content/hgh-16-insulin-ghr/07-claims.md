# Claims — hgh-16-insulin-ghr

All from the "Insulin, IGFBP-1, and hepatic IGF-1 regulation" section of
`wiki/chats/Research Compendium.md`, which is the most densely cited subsection in
the file (19 hard references).

QUOTE: "Portal insulin stimulates hepatic GH-receptor (GHR) protein synthesis and
surface translocation in a concentration-dependent, biphasic manner, with peak
surface-GHR response at 10 nmol/L insulin in human hepatoma (HuH7) cells; without
adequate portal insulin exposure the liver cannot fully respond to GH."

QUOTE: "In 17 T1D adults, 8 hours off basal insulin caused IGFBP-1 to rise 32→205
ng/mL (6.4-fold), IGFBP-3 to rise 16%, total IGF-1 to fall 19%, and free IGF-1 to
fall from 0.72 ng/mL to below the 0.08 ng/mL detection limit in 15 of 17 patients;
two hours after a single 0.2 U/kg dose of regular or lispro insulin, free IGF-1
rebounded to 0.54 ng/mL."

QUOTE: "Switching T1D patients from subcutaneous to continuous intraperitoneal
insulin delivery dropped IGFBP-1 by −100.7 µg/L (95% CI −143.0 to −58.3, p<0.01)
with no significant change in IGF-1 or HbA1c-driven correlation (r=−0.3, p=0.33
between ΔHbA1c and ΔIGFBP-1) — hepatic insulin exposure route, not glycemic
control, is the controlling variable."

QUOTE: "all four preparations produced parallel decreases in IGFBP-1 despite
marked differences in peripheral insulin concentration and plasma glucose, leading
the authors to conclude glycemic response cannot predict IGF-system response"

QUOTE: "Obese subjects respond to exogenous GH with higher IGF-1 increases than
lean subjects, attributed to increased hepatic insulin exposure; Cushing's
syndrome shows a supranormal IGF-1 response to exogenous GH by the same mechanism."

QUOTE: "Oral estradiol raised IGFBP-1 by 170% and lowered IGF-1 by 43% in patients
with hypopituitarism on GH treatment, while transdermal estradiol did neither — a
first-pass hepatic effect specific to the oral route"

QUOTE: "IGFBP-3, in ternary complex with the acid-labile subunit (ALS), holds
roughly 75–80% of circulating IGF-1 with a complex half-life of about 12 hours"

QUOTE: "In 149 short small-for-gestational-age children randomized to GH doses of
0.033, 0.067, or 0.100 mg/kg/day, after 1 year IGF-1 SDS rose, insulin rose, and
IGFBP-1 declined significantly (p<0.05)"
