# Claims — hgh-03-pulsatility

The question: does the PATTERN of GH matter, separately from the dose?

## Clark et al. 1985, J Endocrinol (hypophysectomised rat)
`.raw/Studies/Growth-Hormone-IGF1-Axis/Growth Induced by Pulsatile or Continuous Infusion of Human Growth Hormone - Clark et al - 1985.pdf`

QUOTE: "Continuous infusions of GH at 12 mu./day gave no net gain in body weight,
whereas the same dose given in a pulsatile manner produced significant (P<0.05)
body growth."
QUOTE: "Analysis of variance showed that pulsatile infusion was significantly more
effective than continuous infusion (F(1,17) = 4.37, P<0.05)"
QUOTE: "infusions of human or bovine GH at two doses (12 or 36 mu./day, eight
pulses/day, 5-min duration, every 3 h)"
→ The pulse is 5 minutes, 8x a day. This matters for the human caveat below.

LIMIT, quoted: at the 3x higher dose "pulsatile infusions achieved a higher mean
body weight gain, this difference was not statistically significant." The
pattern effect shrinks as dose rises.

## Isgaard et al. 1988, Endocrinology (hypophysectomised rat)
`.raw/Studies/Growth-Hormone-IGF1-Axis/Pulsatile Intravenous GH Infusion to Hypophysectomized Rats Increases - Isgaard et al - 1988.pdf`

QUOTE: "a pulsatile pattern of GH infusion was more effective than continuous
infusion in stimulating longitudinal bone growth (P < 0.01)"
QUOTE: "in the skeletal muscle pulsatile treatment with GH at 3-h intervals
induced a 3- to 5-fold increase in the levels of IGF-I mRNA ... whereas
continuous treatment with GH exerted no effect"
QUOTE: "In rib growth plate continuous infusion of 1.5 U/kg-day GH enhanced IGF-I
mRNA, although this treatment was much less effective than pulsatile infusion
(P < 0.05)"
QUOTE: "There was no significant difference between the effects of pulsatile and
continuous GH on hepatic IGF-I mRNA in the present study."
→ The liver does not care about the pattern. The growth plate and muscle do.

## Bick et al. 1992, Endocrinology (hypophysectomised rat) — the key result
`.raw/Studies/Growth-Hormone-IGF1-Axis/Roles of Pulsatility and Continuity of GH Administration in the Regulation - Bick et al - 1992.pdf`

QUOTE: "Serum IGF-I concentrations were higher after continuous GH treatment than
5 or 90 min after a 5-min GH infusion"
QUOTE: "Despite the higher serum IGF-I levels after continuous GH treatment than
after pulsatile injections of GH every 3 h, the latter treatment induced a greater
body weight gain than the same dose of GH infused continuously"
QUOTE: "Receptor, GH-BP, and IGF-I are stimulated more by continuous GH delivery,
whereas growth is enhanced more by pulsatility."
→ Same dose. Continuous produced the HIGHER serum IGF-1 and the LESS growth.

## THE HUMAN CAVEAT — Lundberg et al. 2015, BMC Endocr Disord
`.raw/Studies/Growth-Hormone-IGF1-Axis/Growth Hormone (GH) Dose-Dependent IGF-I Response Relates to Pubertal - Lundberg et al - 2015.pdf`

They randomised the same daily dose given once vs split in two:
QUOTE: "to receive 67 μg/kg/day (GH67) given once (GH67x1; n = 30) or divided into
two daily injection (GH33x2; n = 36)"
QUOTE: "There were no differences in gain in heightSDS between the two high-dose
groups with GH67 given once or divided into twice daily GH33"

→ Splitting a subcutaneous injection in two is NOT the same intervention as an
8-pulse, 5-minute-per-pulse intravenous profile. A subcutaneous depot already
releases slowly. This is the honest limit of the rat data and the deck ends on it
rather than on a protocol.

## Scope of the evidence
- It does not claim a human dosing schedule is proven superior. One human trial
  tested a 2-way split and found nothing.

- The rat work is hypophysectomised animals, i.e. no endogenous GH at all.
