# ERRγ Regulation by IL-1 in Osteoarthritic Chondrocytes (Bonnelye et al. 2011)

Introduces a genuinely distinct nuclear receptor to this domain's estrogen-receptor-family catalog: ERRγ (estrogen receptor-related receptor gamma), an orphan nuclear receptor that — despite its name — doesn't bind estrogen at all, but is structurally related to classical ERs. Already known to be expressed in articular chondrocytes and dysregulated in inflammatory arthritis models, this paper shows IL-1 dysregulates ERRγ via prostaglandin E2- and cAMP-dependent pathways specifically in osteoarthritic chondrocytes.

> [!key-insight] This is the sixth distinct estrogen-receptor-family mechanism now cataloged in this domain (alongside classical ERα/ERβ genomic signaling, GPER-1, ligand-independent ERα mechanotransduction, ligand-independent ERα DNA-damage/senescence suppression, and ElBaradie's rapid membrane ERα/ERβ signaling) — and the most structurally distinct, since ERRγ is an orphan receptor with no known endogenous estrogenic ligand at all, making its inclusion in the "estrogen receptor" family almost purely nominal/structural.

## Relevance to This Domain

Extends [[Three Estrogen Receptor Mechanisms in the Growth Plate]] and [[Estrogen Receptor (ER)]] with a structurally distinct orphan-receptor family member (ERRγ), inflammation-regulated via cAMP/PGE2 in osteoarthritic (not growth-plate) chondrocytes.
