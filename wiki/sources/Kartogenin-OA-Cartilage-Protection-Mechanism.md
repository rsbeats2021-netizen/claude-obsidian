# Kartogenin Protects Against Cartilage Degradation in Post-Traumatic OA (2021)

Supplies the actual research substance behind a compound this domain previously only encountered as a product safety-data-sheet (Cayman Chemical's Kartogenin SDS, left un-ingested earlier this session as non-scientific commercial content): Kartogenin (KGN), already known to promote MSC chondrogenic differentiation, is shown here to protect against cartilage degradation in a post-traumatic osteoarthritis mouse model, with interleukin-exposed chondrocyte culture used to probe the underlying mechanism.

## Relevance to This Domain

The primary research paper behind Kartogenin's cartilage-protective mechanism, complementing [[Kartogenin-Mode-of-Action-4ABP-PA|the pharmacological mode-of-action study]] (same batch) that investigates how KGN is metabolized and whether its breakdown products retain activity.
