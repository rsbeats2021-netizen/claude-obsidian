# PEPITEM: A Novel Endogenous Bone-Anabolic Peptide Acting via OPG (Lewis, Frost, et al.)

Identifies PEPITEM, an endogenous peptide, as a direct anabolic osteogenic signal: it acts directly on osteoblasts to promote new, stronger bone formation, and stimulates an inhibitory paracrine loop via OPG (the same osteoprotegerin pathway already central to this domain's [[ERKO-BERKO-DERKO-OPG-RANKL-Male-Mice-Windahl2001|ERα/OPG/RANKL literature]]) to limit bone resorption — halting disease-induced bone loss in vivo. A genuinely novel, non-steroid-hormone endogenous bone-anabolic mechanism operating through the same OPG/RANKL axis this domain already tracks for estrogen.
