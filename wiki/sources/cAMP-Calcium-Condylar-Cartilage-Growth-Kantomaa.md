# cAMP/Ca++ Signaling in Mandibular Condylar Growth (Kantomaa & Hall)

Extends this domain's cartilage-growth-mechanism literature to the craniofacial skeleton (mandibular condylar cartilage), reviewing the historically contested question of whether this cartilage responds actively to mechanical/functional loading via cAMP/calcium second-messenger signaling — the same general second-messenger pathway (cAMP, via forskolin) already central to [[PTHrP-HDAC4-MEF2-Chondrocyte-Hypertrophy-Kozhemyakina2009|Kozhemyakina et al. 2009]]'s growth-plate hypertrophy mechanism, but applied here to a craniofacial rather than long-bone context.

## Relevance to This Domain

Extends [[Sex-Hormones-Cartilaginous-Tissues-Scoping-Review-Hutchinson2025|Hutchinson & Séguin 2025]]'s call for more research on non-growth-plate cartilaginous tissues, with a specific mechanistic (cAMP/calcium) angle on craniofacial cartilage growth.
