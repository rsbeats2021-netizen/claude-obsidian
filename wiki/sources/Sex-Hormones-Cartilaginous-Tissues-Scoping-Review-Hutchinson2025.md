# Sex Hormones in Cartilaginous Tissues: Scoping Review (Hutchinson & Séguin 2025)

A systematic scoping review mapping the sex-hormone literature across three cartilaginous joint tissues largely outside this domain's usual growth-plate focus: the intervertebral disc, temporomandibular joint, and articular cartilage. Starting from ~3900 initially identified articles, the review critically examines how well (or poorly) characterized sex-hormone action is in each tissue, motivated by increasing off-label clinical use of sex hormones for musculoskeletal conditions despite this evidence gap.

> [!gap] A scoping review (mapping the evidence landscape) rather than a primary mechanistic study; specific findings by tissue were not extracted in full given the review's breadth.

## Relevance to This Domain

Extends [[Estrogen vs Androgen in Male Bone Health]] to three cartilaginous tissue types (IVD, TMJ, articular cartilage) this domain's growth-plate-centric sources don't directly address, and confirms (per the review's own framing) that this is a genuinely under-studied area across the field, not just a gap in this vault's ingested corpus.
