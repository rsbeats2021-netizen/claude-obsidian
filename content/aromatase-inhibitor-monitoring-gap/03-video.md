The main drug used. Not because it's safe. Because nobody checked.

[cover card]

Letrozole and anastrozole block testosterone converting into estrogen. Less estrogen means slower bone maturation, and in boys that means more adult height.

[do work for height]

In postmenopausal breast cancer, aromatase inhibitors are standard therapy, often for years. That population has been studied hard for adverse effects.

[are used in oncology]

Joint and muscle pain. Altered retinal function.

[the side effects show up]

Paediatric height studies of these drugs generally don't screen for the sensory or cognitive outcomes oncology treats as standard safety monitoring.

[that should bother you]

A single line in the literature says letrozole doesn't impair cognitive development in boys. That's one citation, not a monitoring programme.

[and it's thin]

Effective for height in the right clinical setting, under supervision, and carrying a documented adverse effect profile that the height literature never went looking for.

[takeaway card]

No reported harm isn't evidence of no harm when nobody measured.
