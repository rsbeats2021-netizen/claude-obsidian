# Cell Cycle Kinetics of Proliferative-Zone Chondrocytes at Different Growth Rates (Wilsman et al. 1996)

A foundational quantitative-biology paper directly measuring proliferative-zone chondrocyte cell-cycle kinetics across growth plates elongating at different rates (i.e., comparing fast-growing to slow-growing plates within and across bones) — establishing the basic cell-cycle-time framework that separates how much of a growth-plate's elongation rate is attributable to cell-cycle speed versus other cell-kinetic parameters (cell size, hypertrophic expansion). A classic Wisconsin-Cornell (Wilsman/Farnum) methodology paper this domain's other chondrocyte-kinetics sources implicitly build on.

## Relevance to This Domain

Foundational quantitative cell-kinetics framework for [[Molecular Regulators of Growth Plate Chondrocyte Maturation]] and [[Epiphyseal Skeletal Stem Cells and Hedgehog Signaling]] — establishes the cell-cycle-time methodology this domain's other chondrocyte-proliferation findings are implicitly measured against.
