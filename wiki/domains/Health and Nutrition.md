# Health and Nutrition

Domain covering nutrition science, natural food compounds, and their effects on human metabolic health. Seeded from honey research: diabetes/glycemic effects, bone metabolism, composition/bioactivity, and microbiome.

## Open Questions

> [!gap] Only preclinical/small human trials so far — no large-scale RCTs establishing honey as a clinical antidiabetic or antiosteoporotic intervention.

> [!contradiction] [[Honey-Hydroxyapatite-Bone-Healing|Bigham-Sadegh et al. 2018]] found topical honey at a bone-defect site was the *worst* performer of all tested treatments, while systemic honey supplementation consistently helped bone metabolism elsewhere in [[Honey and Bone Metabolism]] — route of administration matters and shouldn't be conflated.

> [!gap] Most of the bone-metabolism animal literature traces to one Malaysian research group (Ooi/Tavafzadeh/Mosavat, Universiti Sains Malaysia) using Tualang honey — corroborating but not independently replicated across labs.
