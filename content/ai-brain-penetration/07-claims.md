Verified against the PubMed record, 3 Oct 2026.
QUOTE: "The brain-to-plasma ratio (Kp,brain, mL/g brain) of anastrozole was
0.0299 ± 0.0068, which was lower than that of letrozole (0.383 ± 0.048) and

→ letrozole / anastrozole = 0.383 / 0.0299 = 12.8-fold.
QUOTE: "In situ mouse brain perfusion also demonstrated that the uptake clearance

QUOTE: "Anastrozole and vorozole were transported by P-glycoprotein"
QUOTE: "The Kp,brain of anastrozole and vorozole were increased by 12- and

QUOTE (conclusion): "active efflux mediated by P-gp at the blood-brain barrier
limits the effect of anastrozole in the central nervous system, whereas vorozole

using microdialysis, was 0.118 ± 0.037 mL/g brain."

## Species discipline

which the two drugs differ, not a human cognitive outcome.
