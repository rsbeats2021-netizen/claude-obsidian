# Research brief — What testosterone actually becomes

## Core claim
The fraction of testosterone converted to estradiol versus DHT varies with both
dose and age, measured directly in men. The same protocol produces different
hormonal states in different people.

## Key points (tier-tagged)
- **Tier 1** — Direct human measurement of how testosterone dose and patient
  age affect the fraction converted to estradiol (via aromatase) versus DHT
  (via 5α-reductase) (Lakshman 2010).
- **Tier 1** — Conversion fractions are not fixed; raising dose does not scale
  all three hormones proportionally.
- **Tier 1** — Age independently affects the conversion pattern.
- **Tier 1** — Relevance here: estradiol is the dominant driver of growth plate
  closure, so the aromatised fraction is precisely the part that spends
  skeletal maturation.

## Why this deck is useful rather than protocol-adjacent
It argues that dose numbers don't transfer between people — which undercuts
protocol-sharing rather than enabling it. Slide 8 makes the takeaway
methodological: ask what a compound becomes in a given person, not what it is.

## Deliberately not included
No doses, no conversion ratios quoted as targets, no recommendations. The deck
describes why such numbers are unreliable, and giving specific ones would
contradict its own argument.
