# Does AAS Use Downregulate Skeletal Muscle Androgen Receptors? A Narrative Review

Directly tests a popular hypothesis in both clinical and AAS-enthusiast communities: that the well-known plateau effect in muscle hypertrophy under long-term anabolic-androgenic steroid regimens is caused by AAS-induced downregulation of skeletal muscle androgen receptors. Reviewing seminal and recent evidence, the paper concludes this hypothesis is not clearly supported — complicating a widely assumed mechanism for AAS's diminishing muscle-building returns over time.

## Relevance to This Domain

Extends [[Androgen Receptor (AR)]] and [[Testosterone]]'s anabolic-androgenic-steroid literature to skeletal muscle (rather than bone/growth-plate), directly challenging a popular but evidently under-supported AR-downregulation hypothesis for the AAS hypertrophy plateau.
