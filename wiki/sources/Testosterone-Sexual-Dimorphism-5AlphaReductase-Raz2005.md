# Testosterone's Sex-Dimorphic Chondrocyte Effect Requires 5α-Reductase Conversion to DHT (Raz et al. 2005)

From the same Boyan/Schwartz/Ornoy research lineage as [[Estrogen-IP3-Calcium-Growth-Plate-Sex-Specific-Ekstein2005|Ekstein et al. 2005]] and [[Membrane-Effects-Sex-Hormones-Growth-Plate-Chondrocytes-Thesis|ElBaradie's thesis]] already in this domain, this paper establishes the androgen-side counterpart to that lab's estrogen sex-dimorphism findings: testosterone's sex-specific effect on prehypertrophic/hypertrophic growth-plate chondrocytes requires local metabolism to DHT via 5α-reductase type 1 — the enzyme itself, not just testosterone, is required for the sex-differential response to manifest.

## Relevance to This Domain

Extends this domain's [[5-Alpha-Reductase]] and [[Dihydrotestosterone (DHT)]] vocabulary threads with a sex-dimorphism angle, and completes the androgen side of the Boyan/Schwartz/Ornoy lab's parallel estrogen ([[Estrogen-IP3-Calcium-Growth-Plate-Sex-Specific-Ekstein2005|Ekstein 2005]]) and androgen sex-dimorphism research program.
