# DHT and Estradiol in TMJ Osteoarthritis: Sex-Specific Mechanisms (Tomura et al.)

Extends this domain's sex-hormone/sex-dimorphism literature to a novel anatomical site: the temporomandibular joint (TMJ, the jaw joint). Using mechanical stress, aromatase inhibitors, orchiectomy, and direct estradiol treatment in male mice, this study directly tests how DHT and estradiol modulate TMJ osteoarthritis pathogenesis, revealing sex-specific differences — a craniofacial-joint complement to this domain's otherwise mostly long-bone-growth-plate sex-dimorphism cluster (Raz 2005, Nasatzky 1993).

## Relevance to This Domain
