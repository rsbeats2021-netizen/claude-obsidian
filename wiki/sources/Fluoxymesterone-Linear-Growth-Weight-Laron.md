# Fluoxymesterone for Growth Retardation and Underweight (Laron)

Zvi Laron (a major figure in pediatric growth-hormone research) tested fluoxymesterone — the same anabolic androgen already covered in this domain's [[Growth-Hormone-Androgen-Synergy-Hypopituitary-Dwarfs|1974 GH+androgen synergy trial]] — alone in growth-retarded, underweight children, establishing a specific dose threshold (0.25 mg/kg/day) above which virilization occurred, with 0.1-0.15 mg/kg/day producing beneficial weight/growth effects without it.

## Relevance to This Domain

Adds a specific dose-response threshold for fluoxymesterone's growth-benefit-vs-virilization tradeoff to [[Testosterone]]'s anabolic-androgen catalog, and directly complements [[Growth-Hormone-Androgen-Synergy-Hypopituitary-Dwarfs|the 1974 GH+fluoxymesterone trial]] already in this domain (same drug, single-agent data).
