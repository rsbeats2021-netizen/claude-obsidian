# PGE2 Induces Chondrocyte Apoptosis via cAMP (Miwa et al. 2000)

Establishes that prostaglandin E2 (PGE2), a key mediator of joint inflammation, induces apoptosis in bovine articular chondrocytes through a cAMP-dependent pathway — an inflammation-driven chondrocyte-death mechanism relevant to osteoarthritis progression, and an interesting counterpoint to [[Induction-Increased-cAMP-Chondroprotective-Karsdal2007|Karsdal et al. 2007]] (same batch), where raising cAMP was found to be *chondroprotective* against a different degradation pathway (MMP-mediated). Together these two papers suggest cAMP signaling's net effect on chondrocyte survival may depend heavily on which upstream trigger (PGE2 vs. calcitonin) and downstream degradation pathway (apoptosis vs. MMP-mediated matrix breakdown) is in play.

## Relevance to This Domain

An inflammation-driven chondrocyte-apoptosis mechanism complementing [[Induction-Increased-cAMP-Chondroprotective-Karsdal2007|Karsdal et al. 2007]]'s chondroprotective cAMP finding — together suggesting cAMP's effect on chondrocytes is context-dependent rather than uniformly protective or destructive.
