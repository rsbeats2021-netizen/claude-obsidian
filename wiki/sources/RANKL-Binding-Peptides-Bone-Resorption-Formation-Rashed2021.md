# RANKL-Binding Peptides and Bone Remodeling (Rashed et al. 2021)

Extends the RANKL/OPG bone-remodeling mechanism already central to [[AR-Osteoblast-Stage-Dependent-Action-Russell2014|Russell et al. 2014]]'s AR-osteoblast finding in this domain, testing peptides that directly bind RANKL to modulate bone resorption/formation balance — a potential therapeutic strategy targeting the same pathway AR naturally regulates.

## Relevance to This Domain

Directly extends the RANKL/OPG mechanism central to [[AR-Osteoblast-Stage-Dependent-Action-Russell2014|Russell et al. 2014]] and [[Androgen Receptor (AR)]]'s bone-resorption-inhibition pathway.
