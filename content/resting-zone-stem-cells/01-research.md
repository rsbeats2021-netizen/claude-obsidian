# Research brief — The cells you actually run out of

## Core claim / hook angle
Final height is decided by a thin layer of PTHrP+ stem cells at the top of the
growth plate. Everything below them is the assembly line they feed.

## Misconception it corrects
People picture the growth plate as one undifferentiated slab of cartilage that
"closes". It's a hierarchy with a finite reserve population at the top.

## Key points (tier-tagged)
- **Tier 1** — Schrier 2006: resting-zone proliferation collapses from >95% at
  birth to <10% by 17 weeks in rabbits; cell *number* declines too. Depletion
  is both qualitative and quantitative.
- **Tier 1** — Chu 2025: single-cell RNA-seq of human pubertal growth plate
  biopsies confirms two resting-zone stem subpopulations in humans, not just mice.
- **Tier 2** — Trompet 2024: Hedgehog-agonist bead in one rat femur produced
  progressive leg-length gain vs untreated contralateral leg.
- **Tier 2** — Hallett 2021: these cells require a low-Wnt niche; forcing
  canonical Wnt impairs column formation.
- **Tier 2** — Chu 2025: GH acts directly on human epSSCs via JAK/STAT and
  autocrine TGF-beta, independent of the liver IGF-1 axis.
- **Tier 3 / untested** — Whether normal fusion IS epSSC clonal exhaustion has
  never been directly tested. Best available model, still inference.
