Your growth plate. And it splits testosterone down two paths with opposite effects.

[cover card]

A human chondrocyte cell line both produced 17 beta estradiol and responded to it, changing proliferation and apoptosis. That's a complete local loop: the tissue makes the hormone and reads it.

[responds to what it makes]

Oxandrolone in paediatric growth disorders produced growth velocity of 4.5 to 9.6 cm per year and 2.3 to 4.6 cm of additional adult height across 45 studies. That is not what a compound that closes plates does.

[non aromatisable differs]

Skeletal maturation still advanced 3 to 7 months, and adverse effects were dose dependent, clustering above 0.06 mg per kg per day. Virilisation appeared in 12.5 to 15.2% at those higher doses.

[found the cost]

How much testosterone becomes estradiol versus DHT at the plate decides whether you are growing or closing. That split shifts with dose and age, and it happens locally, where no blood test can see it.

[takeaway card]

Ask what it becomes, not how much you took.
