# H19 Gene Silencing in Rhabdomyosarcoma (Lynch et al. 2002)

This paper studies the epigenetic mechanism silencing the imprinted growth-regulatory gene H19 in rhabdomyosarcoma (a pediatric muscle-tissue cancer) and Wilms' tumor (a pediatric kidney cancer), establishing that DNA methylation — not histone deacetylation — is the primary silencing mechanism, since HDAC inhibitors could not reactivate the gene despite achieving maximal histone hyperacetylation, while a methylation inhibitor could.

> [!contradiction] Flagged as topically unrelated to this domain: this is a cancer epigenetics/gene-silencing mechanism paper. While H19 controls the growth factor IGF2 in cis, the paper's subject is tumor-suppressor-gene methylation biology in pediatric cancer, not growth-plate physiology or steroid hormone signaling — no substantive overlap with this domain beyond the incidental mention of a growth-factor-adjacent gene name.
