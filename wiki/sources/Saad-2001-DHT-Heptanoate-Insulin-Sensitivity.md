# Saad et al. 2001 — 50 mg DHT-hp q2wk in Delayed Puberty

*J Clin Endocrinol Metab* 86(10):4881–6. PMID 11600557. DOI 10.1210/jcem.86.10.7913. Full text obtained 2026-10-17. Bruce Keenan is a co-author, which is why this is the same DHT heptanoate preparation as the Texas studies.

## Dosing — the lowest human DHT-hp dose published

> "**DHT heptanoate** was administered under an investigational new drug from the Food and Drug Administration (**IND 29,139**). Each participant received **50 mg of DHT im every 2 wk for a total of 4 months**. The DHT dose was similar to the T dose used in our previous study and comparable to the monthly DHT dose used previously in delayed puberty."

**50 mg = 36.1 mg DHT-equivalent per injection**; 8 injections over 4 months = 400 mg ester total. Note the authors treat 50 mg q2wk as *equivalent* to the ~100–200 mg monthly regimens of [[Keenan-1993-DHT-Heptanoate-Pubertal-Growth]] and [[Keenan-1996-DHT-Heptanoate-GH-Binding-Protein]], which it is not on a cumulative basis — 400 mg over 4 months against 500 mg over 2 months. It is roughly half the monthly exposure, delivered twice as often.

## Subjects

Ten adolescent boys with constitutional delay of growth and puberty, **all prepubertal at Tanner stage I**, otherwise healthy. Ages ranged from 13.5 to 15.2 y in the per-patient table; height SD scores −1.3 to −2.8; most had a positive family history of CDGP.

## Results after 4 months

| | Pre | Post | P |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Plasma DHT** | 0.36 ± 0.09 nmol/L | **10.8 ± 2.5 nmol/L** (≈313 ng/dL) | 0.03 |
| Plasma T | 1.5 ± 0.4 nmol/L | 3.5 ± 0.9 | 0.04 |
| Serum estradiol | <18 pmol/L | **<18 pmol/L in all subjects** | — |
| IGF-1 | 0.27 ± 0.02 nmol/L | 0.31 ± 0.03 | NS |
| Mean nocturnal GH | 3.2 ± 0.6 µg/L | 3.0 ± 0.4 | NS |
| % body fat | 23.6 ± 2.8 | **22.0 ± 2.8** | 0.01 |
| HDL | 1.3 ± 0.1 mmol/L | **1.1 ± 0.1** | 0.02 |
| LDL / VLDL | 2.8 / 0.5 | 2.8 / 0.6 | NS |
| Insulin-stimulated glucose metabolism | 57.2 ± 3.9 | 58.3 ± 3.9 µmol/kg·min | NS |
| Total body lipolysis, proteolysis | — | unchanged | NS |

Height, weight and fat-free mass increased; percentage body fat fell; secondary sexual characteristics advanced to roughly Tanner II. In two subjects testosterone rose into the Tanner II range.

Methods: hyperinsulinemic (40 mU/m²·min)–euglycemic clamp, 3 h; lipolysis and proteolysis by [²H₅]glycerol and [1-¹³C]leucine; body composition by DXA; nocturnal GH from 22 samples every 20 min, 2400–0700 h.

## Why this matters

Three things this adds that the higher-dose studies cannot:

1. **A dose–response anchor.** 50 mg q2wk → 313 ng/dL; 100–200 mg monthly → 703–848 ng/dL; 200 mg q2–3wk → 284 ng/dL at 2 wk. Blood DHT is roughly proportional to monthly exposure, not to injection frequency.
2. **Clean androgen isolation at a low dose.** Estradiol stayed under assay sensitivity in all ten, and GH and IGF-1 did not move — so the body-composition changes are androgen-receptor effects with the estrogen and GH arms silent.
3. **The regulatory record.** FDA IND 29,139 is the only identifier tying this preparation to a formal investigational programme; nothing in the literature records what became of it.

The HDL fall is the one adverse signal, consistent with the general non-aromatisable-androgen effect on lipids.
