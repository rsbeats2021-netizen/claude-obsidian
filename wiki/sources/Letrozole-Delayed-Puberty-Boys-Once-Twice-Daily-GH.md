# Once vs. Twice Daily GH Injections in Idiopathic Short Stature (Phillip et al. 1997)

A clinical dosing-schedule comparison extending this domain's basic-science GH-pulsatility literature ([[Pulsatile-GH-IGF1-mRNA-Isgaard1988|Isgaard et al. 1988]], [[GH-Pulsatility-Hepatic-Receptors-IGFBP-Bick1992|Bick et al. 1992]], [[GH-Secretory-Pattern-Peak-Trough-Gevers1996|Gevers et al. 1996]]) into a practical clinical question: does splitting the daily GH dose into two injections (mimicking more physiological pulsatility) improve growth outcomes over once-daily dosing in idiopathic short stature?

## Relevance to This Domain

A clinical-translation data point for [[Growth Hormone-IGF-1 Axis]]'s pulsatility literature, testing whether the basic-science pulsatility findings translate into an actionable dosing-schedule recommendation.
