# GH Pulsatility and Hepatic GH Receptor/IGFBP Regulation (Bick et al. 1992)

From the same Isaksson/Jansson research group as [[Pulsatile-GH-IGF1-mRNA-Isgaard1988|Isgaard et al. 1988]] already in this domain, extending the pulsatile-vs-continuous GH administration question to hepatic GH receptor regulation and circulating GH-binding protein levels, building a fuller picture of how GH secretion pattern shapes downstream axis components beyond IGF-I itself.
