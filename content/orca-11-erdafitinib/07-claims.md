# Claims — orca-11-erdafitinib

## THE HUMAN CASE — Majlessipour et al. 2024
`.raw/Studies/FGFR3-Achondroplasia/Skeletal Overgrowth in a Pre-Pubescent Child Treated with Pan-FGFR - Majlessipour et al - 2024.pdf`
QUOTE: "we report an unforeseen outcome in a pre-pubescent child with an
FGFR1-mutated glioma who was successfully treated with FDA-approved erdafitinib,
a pan-FGFR inhibitor approved for treatment of Bladder tumors."
QUOTE: "While on treatment with erdafitinib, the patient experienced **rapid
skeletal and long bone overgrowth resulting in kyphoscoliosis**, reminiscent of
patients with congenital loss-of-function FGFR3 mutations."
QUOTE: "Here, we present the first detailed report of a serious adverse effect
involving skeletal and long bone overgrowth"
QUOTE: "The pattern of overgrowth and skeletal deformity in our patient is
strikingly reminiscent of patients with congenital loss-of-function FGFR3
mutations"
→ This is simultaneously the proof that it works on human long bone and the proof
that the result was a deformity. Both halves are the same sentence.

QUOTE (adult toxicity): "In adults, the most common toxicities associated with
erdafitinib and other FGFR inhibitors include hyperphosphatemia, skin, and nail
toxicities, as well as hand and foot syndrome (redness, swelling, peeling, or
tenderness on the hands or feet)"

QUOTE (the IGF-1 interaction): "insulin-like growth factor 1 (IGF-1) ... can
activate the PI3K/AKT pathway in erdafitinib-treated cells but not the MAPK
[pathway]" and "The IGF-I-activated PI3K/AKT signaling rescued normal fibroblasts
from the cytotoxic effects of erdafitinib by promoting cell survival."

## THE SELECTIVITY PROBLEM — compendium
- "Erdafitinib's clinical toxicity profile overall is largely attributed to FGFR1
  inhibition, which occurs before FGFR3 is even fully engaged given its higher
  potency at FGFR1 than FGFR3."
- "Erdafitinib is a CYP3A4/2C9 substrate (not inhibitor) and inhibits
  P-glycoprotein and OCT2"
- "Whether erdafitinib or other FGFR-pathway agents are functionally active on an
  individual's growth plates can only be confirmed by serial bone-age X-ray; no
  single serum marker provides direct proof"
- "Vosoritide/erdafitinib combination specifically has no human evidence base
  outside pediatric genetic disease ... erdafitinib is an oncology drug with a
  serious side-effect profile."

## THE SELECTIVE ALTERNATIVE — Starrett et al. 2025
`.raw/Studies/Chondrocyte-Cartilage-Bone/TYRA-300, an FGFR3-Selective Inhibitor, Promotes Bone Growth in Two - Starrett et al - 2025.pdf`
QUOTE: "TYRA-300, a potent and selective FGFR3 inhibitor, was evaluated in 3
genetic contexts: wild-type mice, the Fgfr3Y367C/+ mouse model of ACH, and the
Fgfr3N534K/+ mouse model of HCH."
QUOTE: "**In each model, TYRA-300 treatment increased nasoanal length and tibia
and femur length.**" → including wild-type.
QUOTE: "Histologic analysis of the growth plate in Fgfr3Y367C/+ mice revealed that
TYRA-300 mechanistically increased both proliferation and differentiation of
chondrocytes."
→ Mouse, not human. But it isolates FGFR3 from the FGFR1 toxicity that drives
erdafitinib's profile.

## Scope of the evidence
Erdafitinib's approved indication is urothelial carcinoma; the case
report is a single pre-pubertal child on oncology treatment.
