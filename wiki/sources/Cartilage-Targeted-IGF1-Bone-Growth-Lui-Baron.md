# Cartilage-Targeted IGF-1 to Promote Bone Growth (Lui, Baron et al.)

From Jeffrey Baron's NICHD group (the same institutional lineage as [[Estrogen-Irreversible-Resting-Zone-Depletion-Nilsson2014|Nilsson et al. 2014]] and [[Resting-Zone-Chondrocytes-Stem-Cell-Review-Lui2020|Lui 2020]] already central to this domain's epSSC cluster): an engineered cartilage-targeted IGF-1 fusion protein designed to concentrate IGF-1's growth-promoting action specifically at the growth plate, rather than acting systemically like conventional GH/IGF-1 therapy. Directly motivated by GH therapy's well-known limitation — poor efficacy in non-GH-deficient severe short stature such as chondrodysplasias — and the goal of avoiding systemic off-target IGF-1 effects.

> [!key-insight] This represents a genuinely novel therapeutic strategy this domain hasn't previously covered: rather than manipulating systemic hormone levels (GH, aromatase inhibitors, anabolic androgens), this approach re-engineers the *delivery* of an already-known growth factor to act locally where it's needed — directly relevant to [[Epiphyseal Skeletal Stem Cells and Hedgehog Signaling]]'s growth-plate-specific mechanistic focus.

## Relevance to This Domain

A novel targeted-drug-delivery approach for [[Growth Hormone-IGF-1 Axis]], directly motivated by the GH-therapy efficacy gap in chondrodysplasias already implicit in this domain's FGFR3/achondroplasia literature.
