# Because the plate is hypoxic, you can manipulate it with breathing

**Verdict: unsupported.** The mechanism is real and well evidenced. The intervention does not follow from it.

## Evidence for the mechanism (solid)
- HIF-1a is required for chondrocyte survival — Schipani 2001, tier C · [[growth-plate-hypoxia]]
- Chondrocytes store their own oxygen via Hedy — Zhang 2023 *Nature*, tier C · [[growth-plate-hypoxia]]
- P50 27.85 mmHg vs 58.2 for RBCs, a storage profile · [[growth-plate-hypoxia]]

## Evidence against the leap
- The plate is hypoxic **because it is avascular**. That is a diffusion-distance fact, not a function of arterial oxygen content.
- Hedy is described as a **buffer**. A buffer stabilises a variable; it does not scale with ventilation.
- Every experiment is **embryonic knockout in mice**. No study tests a breathing intervention in a growing adolescent.

## Who asserts it
Tier X, from the corpus: `_sox9` (149,300 views) frames hypoxia as beneficial and suggests breath-holding.
`rpo3212` (304,900 views) builds a named hypoxic/hyperoxic training protocol on the ATP version.
Combined reach on this claim: **568,100 views**. See [[growth-plate-hypoxia]] in `wiki/creators/`.

## What would settle it
A controlled trial of an intermittent-hypoxia protocol in adolescents with open plates, with final adult height as the endpoint. Nothing short of that.
