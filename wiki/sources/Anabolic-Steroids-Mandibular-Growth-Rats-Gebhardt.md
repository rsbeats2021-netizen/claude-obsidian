# Nandrolone Alters Mandibular Growth in Rats (Gebhardt & Pancherz)

A second craniofacial data point for this domain's anabolic-androgenic-steroid literature: nandrolone (Deca-Durabolin) administration altered mandibular growth in both juvenile and adult female rats, assessed via radiographic cephalometry and immunoradiology, directly complementing [[Nandrolone-Craniofacial-Growth-Rats-Fischman|the cranial-vault/skull-length nandrolone finding from an earlier batch]] — together establishing nandrolone's craniofacial growth effects across multiple specific bone sites (skull vault and mandible), not just long-bone growth plates.
