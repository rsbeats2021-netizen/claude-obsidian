# Molecuro Research Compound Catalogue

Every product listed by Molecuro (`https://molecuro.com`) as of **2026-10-21**: **479 products**, two shop sections, 15 compound classes. Grouped here by class, then by physical form.

> [!warning] Vendor figures, not evidence
> Every field below is the vendor's own listing copy: class, form, price, molecular formula. Nothing here has been checked against a certificate of analysis, an independent assay, or any regulatory record, and no row says anything about purity, identity, efficacy, or legality in any jurisdiction. Molecuro labels all 479 items *for research use only, not for human consumption*. Treat the catalogue as a commercial primary document about what is sold, not as a source of fact about what any compound does.

## Method

The ten catalogue listing pages were printed to PDF on 2026-10-21 (held outside the vault in `~/Downloads/molecuro/`) and reconciled against the live listing pages the same day. The print lost **69 entries** at page breaks and omitted the whole *Essential and herbal oils* class (49 products); both are restored here, so this page is a superset of the PDFs. Counts per class match the vendor's own stated product counts (72, 96, 187, 49, and 75 across the OTC classes).

Each row resolves at `https://molecuro.com/products/<slug>`, and the slug column carries the slug. Formula and molar mass appear only where the listing states them (205 of 479 rows); extracts, blends, oils and most OTC items carry none. Prices are the entry price for the smallest pack plus the vendor's stated unit price, whose basis differs by product (per mg, per g, per kg, per capsule, per 100 capsules, per softgel) and is reproduced as given. No price was converted or recomputed.

Form is the vendor's label. Where a listing names two forms (`Lyophilised vial, powder`) the first is the primary presentation: the row is filed once under that form, and its Forms cell names both. So the per-class form counts under each heading, which count every form a product is available in, add up to more than the class total.
