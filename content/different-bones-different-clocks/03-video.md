Your hand bones. Which is a problem, because bone age is read from a hand X-ray.

[cover card]

A single figure describing how far along your skeleton is. One value, whole body, read from a radiograph of the hand and wrist.

[is treated as one number]

Growth plate senescence — declining proliferation, hypertrophy and cell depletion — occurs earlier in metacarpals and phalanges than in femurs and tibias.

[is site-specific timing]

The bones read for bone age are the ones furthest along. The bones that determine your height are the ones with more time left.

[backwards from what you want]

MRI of 958 people aged 14 to 21 found fusion timing varied by skeletal site within the same individual — wrist, knee and ankle did not close together.

[agrees]

A single bone age number is a useful clinical estimate and a poor description of reality. Different bones are on different schedules, and the one you measure isn't the one you care about.

[takeaway card]

Useful estimate. Not a physical fact.
