# Sex-Specific Membrane ER Regulation Underlies Differential Estradiol Response (ElBaradie et al. 2013)

The published journal article behind Chapter 3 of [[Membrane-Effects-Sex-Hormones-Growth-Plate-Chondrocytes-Thesis|ElBaradie's 2012 thesis]], supplying the mechanistic detail: the male/female difference in rapid estradiol-induced PKC signaling traces specifically to differential regulation of *plasma-membrane-associated* estrogen receptors themselves (which receptors reach the membrane, and how much), not just downstream signaling differences.

## Relevance to This Domain

Completes the mechanistic picture for [[Membrane-Effects-Sex-Hormones-Growth-Plate-Chondrocytes-Thesis|ElBaradie 2012's]] rapid-membrane-signaling finding already in [[Three Estrogen Receptor Mechanisms in the Growth Plate]] — the sex-dependence originates at the level of membrane receptor localization itself.
