# Skeletal stage predicts percent of final height reached

**Verdict: supported**, within the limits of the cohorts it came from.

## Evidence for
- Sanders 2017, *Scientific Reports*: two longitudinal cohorts followed to final adult height (Brush n=54, Berkeley n=135). Stage 2 = 85%, stage 3 = 90% at peak velocity, stages 4-5 = 96%. · [[skeletal-maturity]]
- Pattern is sex-independent once normalised to final height and standardised by peak growth age.

## Limits
- Cohorts are mid-century American and modest in size. Generalisation beyond them is an assumption.
- Older prediction tables (Bayley-Pinneau 1952) over-predict when bone age is advanced and under-predict when delayed. Treat any single output as a range.
