[on reading this]

Aromatase deficiency and estrogen receptor mutation both produced dramatic, unmistakable skeletal phenotypes — unfused plates, continued growth into adulthood.

[to the estrogen cases]

If DHT were the dominant driver of height that some claims suggest, lifelong absence of it should produce something obvious. The literature doesn't describe one.

[takeaway card]
