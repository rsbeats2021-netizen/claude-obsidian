# A Review of Fulvestrant in Breast Cancer (Nathan & Schmid 2017)

A clinical-oncology review of fulvestrant — the same selective ER degrader (SERD) already covered in this domain via [[Fulvestrant-Precocious-Puberty-McCune-Albright|Sims et al. 2012]]'s pediatric use for McCune-Albright precocious puberty — but here entirely in its dominant clinical context: metastatic ER-positive breast cancer treatment. Covers dosing optimization (500mg beats the original 250mg regimen), combination therapy with CDK4/6 inhibitors and PI3K/AKT/mTOR pathway drugs, and use after aromatase-inhibitor resistance via ESR1 mutation.

> [!contradiction] Flagged as topically unrelated to this domain's growth-plate/bone focus despite sharing the same drug and receptor (fulvestrant, ER) as [[Fulvestrant-Precocious-Puberty-McCune-Albright|Sims et al. 2012]] — this review is entirely about oncology efficacy/dosing/resistance-mechanism questions, not growth or bone outcomes. Ingested for searchability and because it usefully confirms fulvestrant's SERD mechanism (complete ER degradation, distinct from tamoxifen's mixed SERM agonism) referenced but not detailed in the Sims paper.

## Relevance to This Domain

Confirms and details the mechanistic distinction (SERD vs. SERM) underlying fulvestrant's use in [[Fulvestrant-Precocious-Puberty-McCune-Albright|Sims et al. 2012]], but the oncology dosing/combination-therapy content itself has no bearing on growth-plate biology.
