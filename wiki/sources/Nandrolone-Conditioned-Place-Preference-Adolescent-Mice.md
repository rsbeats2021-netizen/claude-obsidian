# Nandrolone Is Rewarding/Addictive in Adolescent Mice via D1 Dopamine Receptor (CPP Study)

Extends this domain's anabolic-androgenic-steroid risk literature — already covering [[AAS-Neurotoxicity-Rat-Cortical-Cultures-Zelleroth|neurotoxicity]] and reproductive/hepatic/renal harms — to addiction potential specifically during adolescence: nandrolone induces conditioned place preference (a standard behavioral measure of drug reward) in adolescent male mice, associated with altered D1 dopamine receptor expression in the nucleus accumbens (a core reward-circuit region). Directly relevant given that AAS misuse typically begins during adolescence.

## Relevance to This Domain
