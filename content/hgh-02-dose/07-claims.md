# Claims — hgh-02-dose

## Mauras et al. 2000, JCEM — randomised, GH-deficient adolescents
`.raw/Studies/Growth-Hormone-IGF1-Axis/High Dose Recombinant Human Growth Hormone Treatment of GH-Deficient - Mauras et al - 2000.pdf`

**Design**
QUOTE: "All patients were randomly assigned to continued treatment with GH ... at
either 0.3 mg/kg·week (standard dose) or 0.7 mg/kg·week (high dose), sc, daily."

**Main result**
QUOTE: "subjects in the high dose group were significantly taller at near-adult
height than subjects in the standard dose group by an average of 4.6 cm
(n = 75; P < 0.001; 95% confidence interval, 2.6–6.5 cm)."

QUOTE: "For subjects who received at least 4 yr of rhGH treatment, the difference
between dose groups at that time point was 5.7 cm (by ANCOVA, P = 0.024; n = 20)."

**Growth rate**
QUOTE: "The mean month 0–12 growth rate was 9.8 cm/yr in the high dose group
(n = 44) compared with 8.2 cm/yr in the standard dose group (n = 43; P = 0.001
between groups)."
QUOTE: "The mean prestudy growth rate was 8.5 cm/yr in both groups"

**Bone age did NOT accelerate** — the key safety finding
QUOTE: "An interesting and important finding of these studies is the normal pace
of bone maturation, with bone ages advancing approximately 1 yr/chronological yr
in both the standard and high dose groups."
QUOTE: "did not increase the rate of skeletal maturation"

**IGF-1 ran high in both groups**
QUOTE: "At any given point in time, approximately 28% of the patients in the high
dose group and approximately 9% in the standard dose group had high IGF-I
concentrations."

**Insulin**
QUOTE: "a greater increase in fasting insulin (P = 0.011) and C peptide
(P = 0.019) in the high dose"

## Ranke 2021, Front Endocrinol — the independent summary
`.raw/Studies/Growth-Hormone-IGF1-Axis/Short and Long-Term Effects of Growth Hormone in Children and Adolescents - Ranke - 2021.pdf`
QUOTE: "Mauras showed that a doubling of the prepubertal GH dose during puberty,

## Population discipline
Every number here is from GH-DEFICIENT adolescents receiving replacement. The
deck states this. It is not evidence for what happens in someone with a normal
GH axis, and the deck says that too.
