[bone age is one number]

Actually the growth plate responds to locally produced IGF-1. That's why cartilage targeted delivery exists as a research programme at all.

[more igf-1 means more height]

Actually suppress growth temporarily and plates grow faster afterwards, because slowing the division rate conserves proliferative capacity.

[lost growth is lost forever]
