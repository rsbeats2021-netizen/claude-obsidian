# Trenbolone Induces Muscle Hypertrophy via Satellite Cell Activation (Thompson et al. 1989)

Extends this domain's anabolic-androgenic-steroid muscle-mechanism literature (already covering nandrolone's effects via IGF-I/IGFBP signaling and cell-cycle regulation) to trenbolone, a distinct and particularly potent anabolic steroid: satellite-cell-mediated mechanisms drove ~60% increased muscle DNA content in treated female rats, alongside reduced body lipid and increased protein deposition.
