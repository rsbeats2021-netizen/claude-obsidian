---

The IGF-1 ceiling. It is far lower than you think.

[cover card]

The standing recommendation is that IGF-1 levels should not exceed 2 SDS, because of concerns about increasing the potential risk of adverse events. SDS means standard deviation score: how far above the mean you sit, for your age and sex.

[the published limit]

The 2020 Swedish national guidelines give the whole structure in one sentence: a standard GH dose of 33 µg per kg per day adjusted for IGF-1, with an upper limit below 2 SDS. And in the first two years of treatment, an IGF-1 up to 3 SDS can be short-term tolerated if necessary.

[how it works in practice]

In that cohort, samples at or above 2 SDS came from a median GH dose of 0.042 mg/kg/day against 0.038 for samples below it. But look at the ranges rather than the medians: 0.02 to 0.10 against 0.01 to 0.10.

[the overlap]

In the same samples, the high IGF-1 group had lower estradiol in girls, 13 against 102 pmol/L, and lower testosterone in boys, 0.35 against 6.9 nmol/L. Both at P < 0.001.

[why your number is noisy]

The ceiling clinicians use is 2 SDS, briefly 3 in the first two years, and it exists for safety rather than for effect. There is no established IGF-1 target for height outcomes at all.

[takeaway card]

2 SDS is a brake. There is no target to chase.
