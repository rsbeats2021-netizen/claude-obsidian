# Chondrocytes in the Resting Zone Are Maintained in a Wnt-Inhibitory Environment (Hallett et al. 2021)

From the same Noriaki Ono lab as [[Hedgehog-Resting-Zone-Chondrocytes-Osteoblast-Transformation|Orikasa et al. 2023]] (Ono is a co-author on both), this eLife paper identifies a second, entirely distinct signaling requirement for maintaining PTHrP+ resting-zone skeletal stem cells — not Hedgehog activation, but Wnt *inhibition*. Using a sophisticated quadruple-transgenic pulse-chase system ("Col2-Q"), the authors isolated slow-cycling label-retaining chondrocytes (LRCs) from the resting zone and found their transcriptome is dominated by Wnt-signaling inhibitors, while their more rapidly dividing proliferating-zone descendants are enriched for Wnt activators and pre-hypertrophic/hypertrophic markers (Ihh, Col10a1, Mef2c, Dlx5, Sp7).

Functionally, when the authors forced canonical Wnt/β-catenin activation specifically within PTHrP+ resting chondrocytes (via Apc haploinsufficiency), the cells' ability to form and expand into columnar chondrocytes was impaired — proving the Wnt-inhibitory environment is not merely correlated with stemness but functionally required to maintain it.

> [!key-insight] This paper and [[Hedgehog-Resting-Zone-Chondrocytes-Osteoblast-Transformation|Orikasa et al. 2023]] together establish that the resting-zone PTHrP+ stem-cell niche is governed by at least two independent signaling requirements: Hedgehog activation drives proliferation/fate-switching (Orikasa, [[Skeletal-Stem-Cells-Growth-Plate-Hedgehog-Signaling|Trompet et al. 2024]]), while Wnt inhibition is separately required just to maintain the stem cells in their quiescent, undifferentiated state at all. Losing either could plausibly deplete the resting-zone pool by a different route — this paper doesn't test whether the two pathways interact directly in the same cells.

> [!gap] Mouse genetic model; the Apc-haploinsufficiency phenotype was described by the authors themselves as "modest," and how canonical Wnt inhibition interacts with the non-canonical Wnt/planar-cell-polarity pathway (also active in resting-zone chondrocytes, per the same paper's discussion) is not resolved.

## Relevance to This Domain

A major addition to [[Epiphyseal Skeletal Stem Cells and Hedgehog Signaling]], introducing Wnt-pathway inhibition as a second required signaling axis (alongside Hedgehog) for resting-zone PTHrP+ stem-cell maintenance — and to [[PTHrP-Ihh Feedback Loop]], since Ihh itself is one of the most strongly non-LRC-enriched genes identified (54-fold), directly confirming where in the growth plate Ihh production actually originates relative to the PTHrP+ population.
