# Research brief — Insulin, local aromatase, and obesity

## Core claim
Obesity-associated hyperinsulinemia may raise aromatase expression locally
inside growth plate cartilage — a short mechanistic chain from metabolic health
to skeletal maturation that this niche doesn't discuss.

## Key points (tier-tagged)
- **Tier 2** — High-fat-diet-induced obesity/hyperinsulinemia rat model in
  juvenile animals; investigates whether insulin regulates aromatase expression
  locally within growth plate cartilage.
- **Tier 1** — Aromatase converts androgens to estrogen; estrogen is the
  dominant driver of growth plate closure. Raising local aromatase raises local
  estrogen at the closure switch.
- **Tier 2** — Proposed chain: obesity → insulin → local aromatase → local
  estrogen → accelerated skeletal maturation.
- **Tier 2** — Offers a mechanism for the common observation that heavier
  children are often taller early without being taller finally.

## Honest limits — slide 7
Rat model, diet-induced. The study investigates the link rather than settling
it. No human growth plate data, no final height outcomes.

## Why it ranks above more exotic topics
Metabolic health is modifiable, extremely common, and affects vastly more
people than any compound in this library. Slide 8 makes that the point:
boring levers move more people than interesting ones.
