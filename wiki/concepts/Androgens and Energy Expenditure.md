# Androgens and Energy Expenditure

Androgens raise total daily energy expenditure **indirectly, by adding metabolically active tissue**, and show no reproducible direct thermogenic effect on existing tissue. Three independent lines of evidence converge on this.

## The three convergent findings

**1. Regression removes the effect.** Testosterone enanthate 3 mg/kg/week for 3 months raised BMR 10% and lean body mass 10%; adjusting BMR for the lean-mass gain left **no residual increase in healthy men** (Source: [[Welle-1992-Testosterone-BMR-Lean-Mass]]).

**2. Withdrawal does nothing.** Three weeks of complete testosterone withdrawal, or repletion, in 54 men with endogenous production clamped off changed neither REE nor respiratory exchange ratio (Source: [[Santosa-2010-Sex-Steroid-Withdrawal-REE]]). Three weeks is long enough for a direct effect, short enough that tissue mass has not moved.

**3. Anabolism without thermogenesis.** Nandrolone decanoate at 65 or 200 mg/week drove nitrogen retention equal to 0.5-0.9 kg lean tissue per week over 21 days with **REE unchanged** (Source: [[Strawford-1999-Nandrolone-REE-Nitrogen-Balance]]).

## Why the literature looks contradictory

The sign of the REE response depends on the subject's **baseline catabolic state**, not on the drug:

| Baseline state | Androgen effect on REE | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Hypercatabolic (severe burns) | **Decreases** | [[Oxandrolone-Burns-REE-Herndon-Porro]] |
| Catabolic (AIDS wasting, 3 wk) | Unchanged | [[Strawford-1999-Nandrolone-REE-Nitrogen-Balance]] |
| Hypogonadal, immobilised | Increases ~112 kcal/d | [[Bauman-2011-Testosterone-SCI-Resting-Energy-Expenditure]] |
| Normal, 3+ months | Increases 7-10%, via lean mass | [[Welle-1992-Testosterone-BMR-Lean-Mass]] |
| Energy-restricted (contest prep) | **Decreases** | [[Almeida-2023-Bodybuilder-Peak-Week-RMR]] |
| Ruminant on poor feed (trenbolone) | **Decreases ~10%** | [[Hunter-Vercoe-1987-Trenbolone-Fasting-Metabolic-Rate-Steers]] |

In a hypercatabolic subject the androgen's dominant action is to *suppress the catabolic drive* — less protein breakdown, less futile cycling, lower cardiac work — which lowers REE. In a metabolically normal subject there is no catabolic drive to suppress, so only the tissue-accrual term remains and REE rises slowly over months.

## The arithmetic that follows

Because the route is tissue, TDEE gain is computable from lean-mass gain:

- Skeletal muscle at rest: **~13 kcal/kg/day**
- Whole fat-free mass (includes organs): ~20-22 kcal/kg/day

Androgen-driven FFM gain is muscle plus intracellular water and glycogen, and water and glycogen are metabolically near-inert. The muscle coefficient is the defensible one. Using the dose-response in [[Bhasin-2001-Testosterone-Dose-Response-Fat-Free-Mass]]:

| Testosterone dose | FFM gain (20 wk) | Derived REE gain |
|---|---|---|
| 125 mg/week | +3.4 kg | ≈ +45 kcal/day |
| 300 mg/week | +5.2 kg | ≈ +68 kcal/day |
| 600 mg/week | +7.9 kg | ≈ +103 kcal/day |

Measured values run somewhat higher (+112 kcal/d measured in SCI men, +7% of BMR in healthy men), so **+50 to +180 kcal/day across the 125-600 mg/week range** is the honest band.

## The indirect term that dwarfs the direct one

The largest real-world TDEE effect of androgens is **not metabolic at all**: it is the extra training volume and spontaneous activity that recovery capacity permits. A lifter who adds two sessions a week and 3,000 daily steps because he recovers faster has added several hundred kcal/day of activity expenditure — more than the entire measured resting effect. This term is absent from every trial in the corpus, because trials standardise or prohibit training.

> [!gap] No study measures total daily energy expenditure by doubly labelled water on any anabolic androgen at any dose. Every number above is resting expenditure or derived from lean mass.

## What is unmeasured

- **Trenbolone, methyltrienolone (M-tren), and every other 19-nor or designer androgen in humans**: zero energy-expenditure data. Zero pharmacokinetic data for M-tren in humans at all; it exists in the literature as the radioligand R1881 for androgen-receptor binding assays, not as a studied drug.
- **Oxymetholone (Anadrol)**: the HIV-wasting RCTs measured weight, body cell mass and appetite, not REE. Reported appetite *increase* on oxymetholone is energy intake, not expenditure.
- Any dose above 600 mg/week testosterone equivalent.
