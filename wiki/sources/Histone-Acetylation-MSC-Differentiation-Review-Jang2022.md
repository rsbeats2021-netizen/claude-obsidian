# Histone Acetylation in MSC Differentiation: Review (Jang et al. 2022)

Situates [[PTHrP-HDAC4-MEF2-Chondrocyte-Hypertrophy-Kozhemyakina2009|Kozhemyakina et al. 2009]]'s HDAC4-mediated PTHrP mechanism (already central to [[PTHrP-Ihh Feedback Loop]]) within the broader histone-acetylation/epigenetic-regulation literature governing MSC lineage differentiation generally — HDAC4 dephosphorylation/nuclear translocation is one specific instance of a much broader epigenetic-control principle this review surveys.

## Relevance to This Domain

Broader epigenetic-mechanism context for [[PTHrP-Ihh Feedback Loop]]'s HDAC4-mediated chondrocyte hypertrophy repression, situating it within general histone-acetylation-based stem-cell fate control.
