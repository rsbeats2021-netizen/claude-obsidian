# Research brief — People who make no DHT

## Core claim
5α-reductase type 2 deficiency is a lifelong natural knockout of DHT. It is the
cleanest available test of DHT's contribution to human height — and the answer
is much less dramatic than the preclinical literature implies.

## Key points (tier-tagged)
- **Tier 1** — In 5α-reductase type 2 deficiency, testosterone cannot convert
  to DHT. Testosterone is normal; DHT effectively absent from birth.
- **Tier 1** — Children were treated with DHT or testosterone enanthate, but
  the treatment goal was genital development. Height was recorded alongside,
  **not** targeted.
- **Tier 2** — Because height wasn't the endpoint, the data cannot cleanly
  separate DHT's specific height contribution from testosterone's or from
  treatment timing.
- **Tier 2 — argument from absence** — Aromatase deficiency and ERα mutation
  both produce dramatic, unmistakable skeletal phenotypes. No comparably
  dramatic height phenotype is reported for lifelong DHT absence.

## The reasoning to be careful with
Slide 7 makes an argument from absence of a reported phenotype. That is
legitimate but weak, and the slide says so explicitly. Do not strengthen that
wording in editing — the inference does not support more than it currently claims.
