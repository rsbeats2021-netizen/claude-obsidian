Your growth plate. AND SPLITS IT TWO WAYS

[cover card]

a target for hormones made elsewhere. Growth plate cartilage carries the enzymes to rebuild them on site.

[it is not just]

Aromatase converts testosterone into estradiol. Researchers detected aromatase transcripts by RT-PCR directly in human growth plate tissue.

[path one]

responds to what it makes. A human chondrocyte cell line produced 17 beta estradiol and reacted to it.

[and the plate]

acts on estrogen receptors in your chondrocytes. Chondrocytes are the cartilage cells that divide to lengthen bone.

[that estradiol]

Oxandrolone across 45 studies in paediatric growth disorders. Growth velocity of 4.5 to 9.6 cm per year.

[which explains]

found the tradeoff. Adverse effects were dose dependent, clustering above 0.06 mg per kg per day.

[section: the cost]

How much testosterone becomes estradiol versus DHT at the plate decides whether you grow or close. That split shifts with dose and with age.

[takeaway card]

Ask what it becomes. Not how much you took.
