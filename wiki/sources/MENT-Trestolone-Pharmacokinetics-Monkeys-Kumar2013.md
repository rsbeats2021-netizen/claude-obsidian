# MENT (Trestolone) Pharmacokinetics: Original Rationale (Kumar et al. 2013)

An earlier (2013) Population Council paper explaining the original therapeutic rationale behind trestolone (MENT), already covered in this domain via [[Trestolone-Human-Metabolism-Doping-Analysis|Piper et al. 2025]]'s anti-doping detection work: because MENT resists 5α-reduction to a DHT-like metabolite, it was expected to avoid DHT's amplified prostate-stimulatory effects, making it a candidate for safer long-term androgen replacement therapy. This paper establishes MENT acetate (the intended clinical ester form) delivers equivalent bioavailability to the parent compound in monkeys.

> [!gap] Animal pharmacokinetics only; no efficacy or safety data on MENT's actual clinical performance as an androgen replacement is included here.

## Relevance to This Domain

Supplies the original developmental rationale for [[Trestolone-Human-Metabolism-Doping-Analysis|Piper et al. 2025]]'s more recent anti-doping metabolism work — together the two papers span trestolone's history from intended therapeutic candidate (this paper) to a substance now requiring dedicated doping-detection methods (Piper et al.), extending [[Testosterone]]'s catalog of pharmacologically distinct androgens.
