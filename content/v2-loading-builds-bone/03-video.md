Mechanical force. THE MECHANISM NOBODY EXPLAINS

[cover card]

contain cells called osteocytes. They sit inside the bone matrix and do one job: detect mechanical stress.

[your bones]

Osteocytes sense the strain. They secrete nitric oxide and PGE2.

[when you load bone]

is a brake on bone formation. It blocks the Wnt pathway, which is the main anabolic signal for bone.

[sclerostin]

increases the number of cells that produce bone. It also suppresses bone resorption at the same time.

[and more wnt]

Robling and colleagues, Journal of Biological Chemistry, 2008. Mechanical stimulation of bone in vivo reduced osteocyte Sost and sclerostin expression.

[this was measured]

Chondrocytes sense load through a three tier cascade. Ion channels at the membrane.

[cartilage does it too]

is estrogen receptor alpha. It works as a mechanotransducer with no estrogen bound to it at all.

[the receptor doing it]

maintains ER alpha expression. That keeps COL2 and SOX9 running, which are the genes for cartilage identity.

[section: the dose response]

Loading is not binary. It has a dose response curve.

[takeaway card]

The variable is magnitude. Not whether you load.
