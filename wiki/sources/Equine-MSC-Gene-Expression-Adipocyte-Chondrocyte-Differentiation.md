# Gene Expression During Equine MSC Differentiation into Adipocytes and Chondrocytes (Ropka-Molik et al. 2026)

A veterinary-science transcriptomics paper tracking equine (horse) bone-marrow-derived MSCs as they differentiate into two different lineages — adipocytes and chondrocytes — via NGS transcript sequencing. The chondrogenic differentiation pathway showed markedly fewer differentially expressed genes (803) than the adipogenic pathway (2325), suggesting chondrogenesis is a comparatively smaller, more targeted transcriptional shift from the MSC baseline state. Sox9 featured prominently among the chondrogenesis-associated genes, consistent with its role everywhere else in this domain.

> [!gap] A veterinary/equine species paper; while Sox9's chondrogenic role appears conserved (as expected, given its evolutionary conservation across mammals), the specific numeric gene-expression-change figures and the adipocyte-vs-chondrocyte comparison are equine-specific findings not yet cross-validated against the domain's rodent/human sources on the same question.

## Relevance to This Domain

A cross-species (equine) data point for [[Sox9]] and [[Molecular Regulators of Growth Plate Chondrocyte Maturation]], reinforcing Sox9's role as the default chondrogenic transcriptional marker across yet another species and cell-source context (bone-marrow MSCs rather than native growth-plate chondrocytes).
