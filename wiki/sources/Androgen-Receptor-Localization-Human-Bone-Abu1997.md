# Androgen Receptor Localization in Human Bone Tissue (Abu et al.)

Complements [[Androgen-Receptors-Human-Osteoblasts-Colvard1989|Colvard et al. 1989]]'s cell-culture discovery of AR in osteoblast-like cells (already in this domain) with direct in situ localization of androgen receptors within human bone tissue itself, confirming the cell-culture finding translates to actual bone.

## Relevance to This Domain

Confirms [[Androgen-Receptors-Human-Osteoblasts-Colvard1989|Colvard et al. 1989]]'s cell-culture AR discovery with direct human bone-tissue localization, strengthening [[Androgen Receptor (AR)]]'s evidentiary foundation.
