# PTH Resistance in Growth Cartilage of Uremic Rats (Kreusser et al. 1982)

Establishes that chronic kidney failure (uremia) produces measurable PTH resistance directly at the growth-plate level: PTH-stimulated cAMP response in proximal tibial growth cartilage was significantly blunted in uremic rats compared to controls, and this resistance was not corrected by 1,25(OH)2D3 (active vitamin D) pretreatment — implicating a growth-plate-intrinsic PTH-signaling defect in the growth failure seen in pediatric renal disease (renal osteodystrophy).

## Relevance to This Domain

Extends [[PTHrP-Ihh Feedback Loop]] with a disease-state (renal failure) angle on PTH signaling specifically at the growth-plate level, distinct from this domain's otherwise developmental/hormonal focus.
