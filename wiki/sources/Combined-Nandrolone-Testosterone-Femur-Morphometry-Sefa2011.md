# Combined Nandrolone + Testosterone Effects on Femur Morphometry (Sefa et al. 2011)

From the same Selçuk University research group behind [[Trenbolone-Extremity-Bones-Running-Rats|Sarı & Lök 2019]]'s trenbolone/bone study already in this domain, this short communication tests combined nandrolone and testosterone administration's effect on femur morphometry — a two-drug anabolic-androgen combination this domain hadn't directly tested before (versus its usual single-agent focus).

> [!gap] Short communication; limited methodological/statistical detail extracted.

## Relevance to This Domain

Adds a combination-therapy (rather than single-agent) data point to [[Testosterone]]'s anabolic-androgen-and-bone catalog, from the same research tradition as [[Trenbolone-Extremity-Bones-Running-Rats|Sarı & Lök 2019]].
