# Tissue-Specific AR Knockout Mice: Bone, Muscle, Immune, and Neural Roles (Chang et al. 2013)

A comprehensive review synthesizing tissue/cell-type-specific androgen receptor knockout (ARKO) mouse studies, demonstrating that AR's physiological roles are highly cell-type-specific: bone mineralization, muscle growth, immune cell maturation, and hypothalamic/neural function each depend on AR in ways that couldn't be distinguished using conventional whole-body castration or androgen-insensitivity-syndrome models. Directly relevant to this domain's bone-specific AR literature by situating it within the broader multi-organ ARKO research program.
