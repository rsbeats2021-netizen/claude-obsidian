# Claims — orca-13-abaloparatide

## THE PULSATILITY RESULT — Fischer et al. 2015
`.raw/Studies/Chondrocyte-Cartilage-Bone/Intermittent PTHrP(1-34) Exposure Augments Chondrogenesis and Reduces - Fischer et al - 2015.pdf`
QUOTE: "Human MSCs subjected to chondrogenic induction in high-density culture
received PTHrP(1–34), forskolin, dbcAMP, or PTHrP(7–34) either constantly or via
**6-h pulses (three times weekly)**"
QUOTE: "While **constant application of PTHrP(1–34) suppressed chondrogenesis** of
MSCs, **pulsed application significantly increased** collagen type 2 (COL2A1) gene
expression and the collagen type II, proteoglycan, and DNA content of pellets
after 6 weeks."
QUOTE: "constant application of PTHrP in chondrogenic medium suppressed
chondrogenesis instead of selectively inhibiting hypertrophic features only"
QUOTE: "Whereas **constant application acted catabolically on bone, an intermittent
application (eg, once daily) provided anabolic effects**, resulting in a net
increase in bone mass"
→ Same inversion as GH: constant suppresses, pulsed builds, at the same molecule.

## MECHANISM — compendium
- "PTHrP strongly increases phosphorylation of SOX9 at serine 181 (a PKA consensus
  site) and increases SOX9-dependent activity at chondrocyte-specific COL2A1
  enhancers, meaning a PTHrP analog such as abaloparatide would be expected to
  increase SOX9 transcriptional activity via PTH1R–PKA signaling"
- "vosoritide (hypertrophic zone) and abaloparatide (proliferative zone)" — the two
  act on different zones of the plate.

## INTERACTIONS — compendium
- "Trenbolone is strongly anti-chondrogenic; its progestogenic activity suppresses
  the PTHrP axis, which would counteract a PTHrP/PTH1R agonist (e.g.,
  abaloparatide) used elsewhere in a stack"
- "Because abaloparatide and forskolin both converge on cAMP as their final
  signaling node, combining them at the same time point would occlude at the PKA
  ceiling and is unlikely to be more than marginally additive"
- Compendium also notes abaloparatide "increases bone turnover markers".

## LIMIT
Fischer is human MSCs in high-density pellet culture, a chondrogenic induction
model, not a growth plate in a living animal. Abaloparatide's licensed indication
is osteoporosis. There is no growth-plate or height trial.
