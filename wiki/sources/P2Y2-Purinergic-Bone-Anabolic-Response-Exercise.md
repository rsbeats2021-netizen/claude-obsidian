# P2Y2 Inhibition and Bone Anabolic Response to Exercise (Chougule et al. 2025)

A bone-mechanobiology paper aimed at improving exercise-induced bone formation in aging populations (osteoporosis prevention), via pharmacological inhibition of the P2Y2 purinergic receptor — a completely different receptor system from any steroid hormone pathway covered in this domain, though thematically adjacent to [[ERalpha-Mechanotransduction-Chondrocytes-Review|Wang et al. 2023]]'s ERα-mediated mechanotransduction in that both concern how bone/cartilage cells convert mechanical load into cellular signals.

> [!contradiction] Flagged as topically unrelated to this domain's steroid-hormone focus: P2Y2 is a purinergic (ATP/nucleotide-sensing) receptor, entirely independent of estrogen, androgen, or any hormone-receptor system covered elsewhere in this vault. Included for its thematic (not mechanistic) resonance with the domain's mechanotransduction thread.

## Relevance to This Domain

None mechanistically, but worth noting alongside [[ERalpha-Mechanotransduction-Chondrocytes-Review|Wang et al. 2023]] as a second, independent example of "mechanical load converted to bone/cartilage cell signaling" — a reminder that mechanotransduction in skeletal tissue runs through multiple, non-overlapping receptor systems (ERα in cartilage per Wang et al.; P2Y2 in bone per this paper), not a single unified pathway.
