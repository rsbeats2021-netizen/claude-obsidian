# DHT-Binding Sites in Human Fetal Epiphyseal Chondrocytes (Carrascosa et al. 1990)

A direct follow-up to [[Androgen-Metabolism-Human-Fetal-Epiphyseal-Cartilage-Audi1984|Audi et al. 1984]] from the same Barcelona research group, identifying specific DHT-binding sites (functional androgen receptors) in cultured human fetal epiphyseal chondrocytes and characterizing the biological effects of androgen binding directly.

## Relevance to This Domain

Completes the human-fetal-cartilage androgen-receptor evidence pair with [[Androgen-Metabolism-Human-Fetal-Epiphyseal-Cartilage-Audi1984|Audi et al. 1984]], extending [[Dihydrotestosterone (DHT)]]'s human-tissue receptor-binding evidence base.
