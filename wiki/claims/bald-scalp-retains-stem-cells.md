# Bald scalp retains its stem cells

**Verdict: supported.** The reversal implication is a separate, weaker claim.

## Evidence for
- Garza 2011, *J Clin Invest*: paired bald and haired biopsies from the same men. KRT15hi stem cells maintained; CD200hi ITGA6hi and CD34hi progenitors markedly diminished. · [[hair-follicle-miniaturisation]]

## The separate claim, and its weaker tier
"Therefore AGA is reversible" is **tier D**. Retained stem cells establish plausibility. No product has been shown to restart the conversion step in humans. Keep the two claims visibly apart on any slide.
