# Metabolic Effects of Real-World Hormonal Doping Patterns in Bodybuilders

Studies real-world metabolic effects of combined AAS, GH, and insulin abuse patterns in recreational bodybuilders, in the context of anti-doping detection technology (the Athlete Biological Passport, which monitors biological variables over time to indirectly detect doping since insulin abuse itself cannot be directly tested for). Provides ecologically valid data on how actual hormone-abuse patterns (often stacking multiple hormone classes) manifest metabolically, complementing this domain's otherwise single-compound, controlled-experiment anabolic-steroid literature.

## Relevance to This Domain

Real-world, multi-hormone-class abuse-pattern data for [[Testosterone]] and [[Growth Hormone-IGF-1 Axis]]'s anabolic-steroid/GH-misuse risk literature, complementing this domain's otherwise single-compound controlled studies.
