# Research brief — Growth plate reactivation

## Core claim
Vertebral body tethering reactivated vertebral growth plate function in an
animal model, suggesting spinal growth plate dormancy may be more reversible
than the standard "closure is permanent" framing implies.

## Key points (tier-tagged)
- **Tier 2** — Vertebral body tethering (a scoliosis growth-modulation
  technique) reactivated vertebral growth plate function in an animal model
  (Latalski 2022).
- **Tier 2** — Implication: closure may be a *state* rather than an *event*.
  States can in principle change; events cannot.
- **Tier 2** — Constraint: vertebral growth plates differ from long bone plates
  in structure, loading and growth pattern. Does not transfer by default.
- **Tier 2** — Constraint: the intervention was **mechanical load**, not a
  compound.

## Why the framing constraints are strict
This is the single most misuse-prone finding in the library. "Growth plates can
be reactivated" is exactly the claim someone will attach to a supplement. Two
slides exist purely to prevent that: slide 6 (it was the spine) and slide 7
(load did this, not a drug). Slide 8 closes with "a crack in a certainty is not
an opportunity."

None of those may be cut for length. If the deck runs long, cut slide 5.
