# cAMP Regulates ECM Gene Expression in Primary Rat Chondrocytes (Rockel et al. 2009)

Extends this domain's growing cAMP-chondrocyte-signaling literature — already covering [[Induction-Increased-cAMP-Chondroprotective-Karsdal2007|Karsdal et al. 2007]]'s protective effect against MMP-mediated degradation and [[PGE2-Apoptosis-Bovine-Articular-Chondrocytes-cAMP-Miwa2000|Miwa et al. 2000]]'s apoptosis-inducing effect — with a third angle: cAMP directly regulates extracellular matrix gene expression and metabolism in primary rat chondrocytes, showing cAMP's chondrocyte effects extend beyond survival/death decisions to the cell's core matrix-production function.

## Relevance to This Domain

A third mechanistic angle (direct ECM gene regulation) for this domain's cAMP-chondrocyte-signaling cluster, alongside [[Induction-Increased-cAMP-Chondroprotective-Karsdal2007|Karsdal et al. 2007]] and [[PGE2-Apoptosis-Bovine-Articular-Chondrocytes-cAMP-Miwa2000|Miwa et al. 2000]].
