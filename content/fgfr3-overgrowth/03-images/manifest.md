## What these show

Growth curves showing dose-dependent response: 14 mg/kg > 12 mg/kg > vehicle,
for nasoanal and tail length across weeks 3–8. The dose-ordering is the useful
part — it's what makes the effect look causal rather than incidental.

Bone-length panels (C–D) give tibia and femur measured by callipers at 8 weeks,
per dose group. Cleaner single-number comparison if the curves feel busy.

⚠️ Both are **mouse** data. Slide 5 copy should not imply human growth-velocity
results, and the deck's slide 8 framing (these treat a genetic dysplasia in
patients who have it) applies to these figures too.
