# Growth Plate Physiology: A Modern Synthesis Review (Lui et al. 2014)

From the same NICHD (Jeffrey Baron) research lineage as several already-central sources in this domain, this review synthesizes growth-plate physiology as understood through gene-expression-network approaches (microdissection combined with microarray profiling) and genome-wide association studies linking specific genes to human height variation — a modern, systems-level complement to this domain's mostly single-mechanism-focused primary literature.

## Relevance to This Domain

A modern systems-level review situating [[Molecular Regulators of Growth Plate Chondrocyte Maturation]] and [[Epiphyseal Skeletal Stem Cells and Hedgehog Signaling]]'s individual mechanistic findings within the broader gene-network and GWAS-informed understanding of growth-plate biology.
