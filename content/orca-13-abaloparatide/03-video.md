Abaloparatide. Constant use suppresses the thing you want.

[cover card]

Worth placing these two next to each other, because they are often discussed as interchangeable chondrogenic agents and they are not. Vosoritide acts mainly on the hypertrophic zone, the final swelling stage.

[the division]

Human MSCs under chondrogenic induction received PTHrP either constantly or as 6-hour pulses, three times weekly, for six weeks. The pulsed arm significantly increased COL2A1 gene expression, and the collagen type II, proteoglycan and DNA content of the pellets.

[fischer 2015]

Not reduced it. And the paper notes it suppressed chondrogenesis rather than selectively inhibiting hypertrophy, which is what you would have hoped a constant signal would do.

[the same study]

This is the identical inversion that runs through the growth hormone evidence. Constant exposure produces the wrong answer and pulses produce the right one, at the same total molecule.

[why it matters]

If this sits in a stack alongside trenbolone, the two are working against each other at the same receptor pathway. Trenbolone is strongly anti-chondrogenic and its progestogenic activity suppresses the PTHrP axis, which is the exact axis a PTH1R agonist works through.

[the stack conflict]

A licensed drug with a clean mechanism onto the master cartilage transcription factor, acting on the proliferative zone, with a cartilage result that depends entirely on being pulsed rather than continuous. Its evidence is a pellet culture, its licence is for a different disease, and in a stack it is directly antagonised by trenbolone and redundant with forskolin.

[takeaway card]

Real mechanism. Schedule-dependent. Easy to cancel out.
