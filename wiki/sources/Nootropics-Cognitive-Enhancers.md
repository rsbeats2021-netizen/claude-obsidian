**Type**: Review article (Nutrients, 2022)

A general review of cognitive-enhancer/"smart drug" classes, dosages, and side effects.

## Key Findings

- **Nootropic classes covered**: racetams (e.g. piracetam), stimulants (modafinil, methylphenidate, amphetamines), cholinergic compounds, and natural/herbal compounds (e.g. ginseng/ginsenosides, caffeine, Ginkgo biloba).
- **Evidence quality gradient**: strongest evidence exists for clinical populations (ADHD stimulant therapy, cholinesterase inhibitors in dementia); evidence for enhancing cognition in healthy individuals is weaker, more mixed, and more prone to publication bias and small-sample-size issues.
- **Side effects/risks vary by class**: from minimal (moderate caffeine use) to dependency and cardiovascular risk (stimulant misuse), underscoring that "smart drug" is not a uniform risk category.
