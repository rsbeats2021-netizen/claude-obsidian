## What these show

`slide-05-sag-femur-pair.png` is the strongest asset in this deck: a photograph
of two femurs side by side, DMSO (vehicle) vs SAG (Hedgehog agonist), against a
ruler, with the treated bone visibly longer. It is the "same animal, one leg
longer" claim as a photograph rather than a diagram — no chart-reading required,
which is exactly what works on a 3-second scroll.

`slide-05-sag-length-charts.png` is the quantified version (femur, tibia and
whole-leg length at 1, 2 and 6 months). Use it only if the photo needs backing
up; it is denser and slower to read.
