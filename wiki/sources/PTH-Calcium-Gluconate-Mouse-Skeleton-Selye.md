# PTH and Calcium Gluconate Effects on Mouse Skeletal Tissue (Historical)

An early (1940s) foundational paper documenting the historically inconsistent reported effects of parathyroid hormone on the mammalian skeleton — cartilaginous overgrowth, stunting, or no effect, depending on species, age, dose, and calcium/phosphorus intake — establishing the experimental-variability problem that later, more precise PTHrP-pathway research in this domain (Kozhemyakina, Okubo, the PTHrP/Ihh loop literature generally) eventually resolved with more controlled genetic and molecular tools.

## Relevance to This Domain

Historical foundational context for [[PTHrP-Ihh Feedback Loop]] — illustrates how much more precisely this domain's modern PTHrP-pathway sources (molecular/genetic tools) resolve the same basic question this 1940s paper found so inconsistent using whole-animal hormone administration alone.
