# Sex-Specific Estradiol Calcium Signaling in Growth-Plate Chondrocytes (Ekstein et al. 2005)

The foundational 2005 paper (same Boyan/Schwartz lab lineage as [[Membrane-Effects-Sex-Hormones-Growth-Plate-Chondrocytes-Thesis|ElBaradie's later thesis]]) first establishing that growth-plate chondrocytes show sex-specific rapid calcium/IP3 signaling responses to estradiol via a capacitative calcium entry mechanism — the precursor finding ElBaradie's thesis work (2012/2013) directly extended to characterize which specific membrane estrogen receptors drive this response and how DHT produces an analogous sex-dependent effect.

## Relevance to This Domain

The foundational paper underlying [[Membrane-Effects-Sex-Hormones-Growth-Plate-Chondrocytes-Thesis|ElBaradie's thesis]] work already in [[Three Estrogen Receptor Mechanisms in the Growth Plate]] — establishes the rapid calcium-signaling phenomenon a decade before its receptor-level mechanism was characterized.
