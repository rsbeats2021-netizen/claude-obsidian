# Pulsatile GH Infusion Outperforms Continuous Infusion in Hypophysectomized Rats (Clark et al. 1985)

A foundational, classic (1985) demonstration underlying this domain's GH-pulsatility thread ([[Pulsatile-GH-IGF1-mRNA-Isgaard1988|Isgaard 1988]], [[GH-Pulsatility-Hepatic-Receptors-IGFBP-Bick1992|Bick 1992]]): pulsatile i.v. GH infusion (8 pulses/day) produced greater body-weight gain than continuous infusion at the same total daily dose in hypophysectomized rats, directly establishing that GH's normal pulsatile secretion pattern functionally matters for growth, not merely reflecting incidental neuroendocrine noise. Also found direct i.v. GH delivery was threefold more effective than subcutaneous delivery — an early, practically relevant dosing-route finding.

## Relevance to This Domain

One of the earliest and most direct experimental foundations for [[Growth Hormone-IGF-1 Axis]]'s pulsatility thread, predating and likely informing the mechanistic work of Isgaard, Bick, and Gevers already central to this domain.
