# The Original GPER-1/GPR30 Discovery Paper (Revankar et al., Science 2005)

The foundational discovery paper — this domain's entire GPER-1 thread ([[GPER1-Chondrocyte-Proliferation-PTHrP-Ihh|Chou et al. 2025]]'s growth-plate proliferation findings, [[GPR30-GPER1-Deficiency-Bone-Mass-Male-Mice-Ford|Ford et al.]]'s knockout-mouse bone-mass confirmation, and the "Mechanism 2" entry in [[Three Estrogen Receptor Mechanisms in the Growth Plate]]) traces back to this 2005 Science paper, which first established that GPR30 (later renamed GPER-1) is a genuine transmembrane estrogen receptor mediating rapid, non-genomic cell signaling — distinct in structure and mechanism from the classical nuclear ERα/ERβ receptors this domain otherwise centers on.

> [!key-insight] Finding this foundational paper closes an important citation gap: every GPER-1 source already in this domain implicitly rests on this 2005 discovery establishing the receptor's basic biology, but until now the domain only had the receptor's much later (2024-2025) growth-plate-specific applications, not the original characterization work.
