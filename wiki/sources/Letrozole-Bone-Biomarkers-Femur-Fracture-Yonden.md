# Letrozole Effects on Bone Biomarkers and Femur Fracture Resistance (Yonden et al.)

Extends [[Aromatase]]'s letrozole-and-bone literature with whole-animal biomechanical/fracture-resistance data in female rats, alongside biomarker changes — a female-rat complement to [[Letrozole-Bone-Laying-Hens|Letrozole in Laying Hens]]'s avian bone-strength finding, testing whether the mammalian pattern (aromatase inhibition affecting bone quality) holds similarly.

## Relevance to This Domain

Adds female-rat bone-biomechanics data to [[Aromatase]]'s letrozole thread, complementing the avian (laying hen) and human clinical literature already in this domain with a rodent whole-bone fracture-resistance outcome.
