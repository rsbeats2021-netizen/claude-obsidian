# Anabolic-Androgen Effects Converge Across Every Craniofacial Site Studied

Four independent rat studies, each examining a different craniofacial structure, collectively show that anabolic-androgenic steroids and sex hormones alter craniofacial growth wherever they've been looked for in this domain's corpus — not an isolated finding at one anatomical site, but a pattern that generalizes across the skull.

---

## Site 1: Cranial Vault / Skull Length

**[[Nandrolone-Craniofacial-Growth-Rats-Fischman|Fischman et al.]]** found chronic nandrolone phenylpropionate in female rats increased body weight ~20% alongside measurable increases in total skull length and altered cephalometric measurements.

## Site 2: Mandible

**[[Anabolic-Steroids-Mandibular-Growth-Rats-Gebhardt|Gebhardt & Pancherz]]** found nandrolone (Deca-Durabolin) altered mandibular growth in both juvenile and adult female rats, assessed via radiographic cephalometry — establishing the jaw specifically, not just the overall skull, as steroid-responsive.

## Site 3: Full Craniofacial Complex, with a Dose-Dependent Shape Effect

**[[Anabolic-Steroids-Craniofacial-Growth-Rat-BarrettHarris1993|Barrett & Harris 1993]]** used a rigorous three-arm dose comparison (control, low-dose, high-dose mimicking actual human abuse levels) across 20 craniofacial measures, finding low doses proportionately enlarge the face (3-5% increase across most dimensions) while abuser-level high doses specifically distort facial shape — a maxillomandibular anteroposterior discrepancy from maxillary excess, not just a bigger normal face.

## Site 4: Temporomandibular Joint (TMJ)

**[[DHT-Estradiol-TMJ-Osteoarthritis-Sex-Specific-Tomura|Tomura et al.]]** extends sex-hormone effects to the jaw joint itself: DHT and estradiol modulate TMJ osteoarthritis development in sex-specific ways, tested via mechanical stress, aromatase inhibitors, and orchiectomy in mice. **[[Idiopathic-Condylar-Resorption-Estrogen-Occlusal-Factors|A related rat study]]** combines estrogen manipulation with mechanical occlusal interference to study idiopathic condylar resorption at the same joint — showing hormonal and mechanical factors may interact at this specific craniofacial site.

## Why This Matters

> [!key-insight] Because these four studies independently converge on craniofacial-skeletal responsiveness to sex steroids across four different specific sites (vault, mandible, whole face, jaw joint), the finding is unlikely to be a single-lab or single-compound artifact. This has direct clinical relevance for adolescent anabolic-steroid misuse: the craniofacial skeleton, not just long bones, may show discernible, potentially non-reversible changes — a risk dimension not emphasized in most public-health messaging about AAS misuse, which typically focuses on cardiovascular, hepatic, and reproductive harms.

> [!gap] None of these four studies were designed to compare against each other, and none tests whether the same dose-dependent proportionate-vs-distorted-shape pattern [[Anabolic-Steroids-Craniofacial-Growth-Rat-BarrettHarris1993|Barrett & Harris]] found for the whole face also holds at the mandible or TMJ specifically — this synthesis is this wiki's own connection, not a claim made by any single source.
