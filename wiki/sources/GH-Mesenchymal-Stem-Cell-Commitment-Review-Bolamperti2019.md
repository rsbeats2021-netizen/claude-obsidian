# GH's Role in MSC Lineage Commitment: Review (Bolamperti et al. 2019)

A review synthesizing GH's role in directing mesenchymal stem cell fate — toward osteoblast, chondrocyte, or adipocyte lineages — extending beyond GH's better-known prepubertal-growth role. Connects to [[Equine-MSC-Gene-Expression-Adipocyte-Chondrocyte-Differentiation|Ropka-Molik et al. 2026]]'s transcriptomic data on the same adipocyte-vs-chondrocyte MSC fate decision, adding GH as a specific upstream regulatory input to that fate choice.
