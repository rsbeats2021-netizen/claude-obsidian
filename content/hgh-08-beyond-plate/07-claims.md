# Claims — hgh-08-beyond-plate
## Cool et al. 2005
`.raw/.../Role of Growth Hormone Receptor Signaling in Osteogenesis from Murine Bone - Cool et al - 2005.pdf`
QUOTE: "GH replacement therapy stimulates bone turnover and improves bone mass
during childhood and adolescence, and decreases fracture incidence by 3- or 4-fold"
QUOTE: "GH receptor-deleted mice (GHR-/-) have reduced femoral width and low
cortical bone mass associated with a very low mineral apposition rate at 4 weeks
of age."
QUOTE: "The reduced osteoblast surface area in these mutants is consistent with
impaired osteoblast proliferation"
QUOTE: "in rats GH induces subperiosteal bone formation associated with increased
mechanical strength"
QUOTE: "the reduced cortical bone formation in GHR-/- mice can be restored with
IGF-1 treatment"
QUOTE: "These bone anabolic actions of GH evidently do not require activation of
STAT5a or b, since combined deletion of these transcription factors is without
effect on cortical bone thickness or mineral apposition rate"
## Svensson 2000 corroboration
Bone mineral content up, volumetric BMD unchanged → bigger bones, not denser.
## Note
The fracture-incidence figure is cited by Cool et al. to ref [51]; it is reported
on the slide as what GH replacement does in children and adolescents, which is the
population the sentence describes.
