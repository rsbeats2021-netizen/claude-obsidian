# Aromatase Inhibitors for Short Stature: Cochrane Review (McGrath & O'Grady 2015)

The highest-evidence-quality source on aromatase-inhibitor-for-height in this domain's entire corpus (a Cochrane systematic review with GRADE quality assessment), and its conclusion meaningfully tempers the AI-optimism running through [[Aromatase-Inhibitors-Near-Final-Height-Males|Bouliari et al. 2026]] and [[Aromatase-Inhibitors-in-Men-Effects-Therapeutic-Options|de Ronde & de Jong 2011]]. After searching from inception through 2014, the reviewers found only 4 qualifying RCTs totaling 207 participants — a small evidence base for a domain-central intervention.

The critical distinction the review draws: short-term growth outcomes (predicted adult height, growth velocity) reliably improved across all 4 trials. But *actual final adult height* — the outcome that actually matters clinically — was reported by only one trial, under non-randomized extension conditions, and showed no meaningful benefit. The authors' conclusion is blunt: "There was no evidence to support an increase in final adult height, based on limited data."

> [!contradiction] This directly complicates the domain's generally favorable framing of AI-for-height. [[Aromatase-Inhibitors-Near-Final-Height-Males|Bouliari et al. 2026]] is titled around near-final height gains, but this earlier, more rigorously evidence-graded Cochrane review found the opposite conclusion using the same category of intervention — the discrepancy may reflect differences in study population, trial design, or simply that more/better trial data accumulated between 2015 (this review) and 2026 (Bouliari et al.), but as of this Cochrane review's publication, final-height benefit was explicitly *not* established, only short-term growth-velocity benefit.

> [!gap] This review found a real safety signal — 45% of letrozole-treated prepubertal boys developing mild vertebral morphological abnormalities — that is not mentioned in this domain's other AI-for-height sources currently ingested. Whether more recent trials (post-2014, potentially including whatever evidence underlies Bouliari et al. 2026) have addressed or resolved this specific safety concern is not established by any source currently in this vault.

## Relevance to This Domain

A crucial evidence-quality check on [[Aromatase]] and the domain's AI-for-height narrative: the rigorously-reviewed RCT evidence (as of 2015) supports short-term growth benefit but explicitly does NOT support final-height benefit, and flags a vertebral-morphology safety signal absent from this domain's other AI sources. Should be read as a caution against over-interpreting [[Aromatase-Inhibitors-Near-Final-Height-Males|Bouliari et al. 2026]]'s more optimistic framing without checking whether it addresses this review's specific evidentiary gaps.
