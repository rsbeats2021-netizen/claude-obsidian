# Tamoxifen for Height Potential in Short Pubertal Boys (Kreher et al. 2005)

A third distinct pharmacological strategy for the same clinical goal covered elsewhere in this domain (delaying growth-plate closure to improve height): tamoxifen, a SERM with mixed ER agonist/antagonist activity, tested in short pubertal boys. This sits alongside [[Fulvestrant-Precocious-Puberty-McCune-Albright|fulvestrant's]] pure ER antagonism and [[Aromatase-Inhibitors-Near-Final-Height-Males|letrozole's]] synthesis-blockade approach — three different points of intervention in the same estrogen pathway, all tried for height-related clinical goals in this domain's corpus.

## Relevance to This Domain

Completes a three-way comparison of estrogen-pathway interventions for height in [[Estrogen Receptor (ER)]] and [[Aromatase]]: SERM (tamoxifen, mixed agonism), pure antagonist (fulvestrant), and synthesis blocker (letrozole) — all targeting the same underlying "less estrogen signaling reaching the growth plate" goal via different mechanisms.
