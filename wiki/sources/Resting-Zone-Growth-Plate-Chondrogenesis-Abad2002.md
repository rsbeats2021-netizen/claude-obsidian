# The Foundational Resting-Zone Stem-Cell Paper (Abad et al. 2002)

This is the primary source this domain's entire [[Epiphyseal Skeletal Stem Cells and Hedgehog Signaling]] cluster ultimately traces back to — the paper that first demonstrated, via surgical auto-transplantation experiments in rabbits, that resting-zone chondrocytes function as a genuine stem-like cell source generating the columnar proliferative-zone chondrocytes below them. Both [[Growth-Plate-Senescence-Epiphyseal-Fusion-Review|Nilsson & Baron 2004]] and [[Depletion-Resting-Zone-Chondrocytes-Growth-Plate-Senescence|Schrier et al. 2006]] — already central to this domain — cite this paper as their foundational premise, but this vault had never directly ingested the primary source itself until now.

> [!key-insight] Reading this paper directly closes a citation loop this domain had been operating on secondhand: every later epSSC paper (Trompet, Orikasa, Hallett, Chu) implicitly rests on this 2002 auto-transplantation result as its evidentiary foundation.

## Relevance to This Domain

The true foundational paper for [[Epiphyseal Skeletal Stem Cells and Hedgehog Signaling]] — everything else in that cluster builds on this result.
