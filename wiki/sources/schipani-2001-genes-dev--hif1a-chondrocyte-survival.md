# HIF-1α is essential for chondrocyte survival

**Design.** Conditional HIF-1α deletion in mouse chondrocytes. Tier C.

## Key claims
- The growth plate is **avascular**; cells at its centre sit furthest from any oxygen supply.
- Deleting HIF-1α caused **massive cell death in the interior** of the growth plate — the death is central, not uniform, which is the signature of a diffusion limit.
- The hypoxia response is therefore **required** for the tissue to survive its own architecture, not incidental to it.

## Why it matters here
This is the paper that made people look for how cartilage normally copes, which is the line that leads to [[10.1038-s41586-023-06611-6--haemoglobin-body-chondrocyte]].
