## Notes
- Slide 5 is the whole deck. Two checklists, one full and one empty, makes the
  point faster than any sentence. Build that one properly.
- Slide 4's payload is that the brain expresses aromatase and makes its own
  estrogen — most viewers won't know that, and it's what makes the oncology
  side effects make sense rather than sound arbitrary.
- Tone check: this deck must read as "nobody measured", never as "this drug
  harms children". The evidence supports the first and not the second.
