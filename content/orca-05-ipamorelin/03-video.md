Ipamorelin. The cleanest of the ghrelin-receptor agonists.

[cover card]

This is what distinguishes it inside its own class. Ipamorelin is the only GHRP-class agonist that does not drive cortisol or prolactin elevation.

[the selectivity]

The GHS receptor internalises with repeated agonism, and the compounds are not equal. The desensitisation ranking is hexarelin > GHRP-2 > GHRP-6 ≈ ipamorelin, with ipamorelin at the favourable end.

[the ranking]

GHS-R1a desensitises and internalises with repeated agonism across the whole class, and 24 hours of continuous GHRP infusion attenuated the bolus response while 14 days of GHRH did not. The human shape of this is the GHRP-2 data: more than 3-fold on day 1 falling to more than 1.8-fold by day 14 and 30.

[the class problem]

The pituitary responds to instantaneous receptor occupancy from the dose in front of it, not to your integrated daily total. At saturating doses you are paying for receptors that are already full.

[why big doses waste]

The best-behaved compound in its class: selective, no cortisol or prolactin liability, slowest desensitisation, and a real synergy with the GHRH arm for mechanistic reasons rather than marketing ones. It fades, it is gated by somatostatin, it cannot exceed what your own pituitary can release, and no trial has tested it against a height endpoint.

[takeaway card]

Cleanest in its class. Still inside your own ceiling.
