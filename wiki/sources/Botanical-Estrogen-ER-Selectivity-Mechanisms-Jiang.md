# Mechanisms Enforcing ER Selectivity of Botanical Estrogens (Jiang et al.)

From the Katzenellenbogen lab (major contributors to ER structural biology), this paper characterizes the molecular basis for why different botanical estrogens (phytoestrogens) show different receptor-subtype (ERα vs. ERβ) selectivity — directly relevant background for understanding why compounds like S-equol ([[S-Equol-Estrogen-Receptor-Anti-Androgen|Setchell et al. 2005]]) and genistein ([[Genistein-Estradiol-ATDC5-Chondrogenesis-TGFbeta|Takeda et al. 2025]]) show their specific, sometimes divergent, receptor-engagement patterns already documented in this domain.

## Relevance to This Domain

Mechanistic background for why this domain's various phytoestrogens (S-equol, genistein, liquiritigenin) show different ER-subtype selectivity profiles.
