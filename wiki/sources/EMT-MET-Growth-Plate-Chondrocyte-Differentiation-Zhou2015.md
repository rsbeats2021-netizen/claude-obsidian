# EMT/MET-Like Transitions in Growth-Plate Chondrocyte Differentiation (Zhou et al. 2015)

Applies the EMT/MET (epithelial-mesenchymal / mesenchymal-epithelial transition) cellular-state-change framework — more commonly used in cancer metastasis and embryonic development — to growth-plate chondrocyte differentiation during endochondral ossification, a conceptually novel lens for this domain's chondrocyte-maturation literature.
