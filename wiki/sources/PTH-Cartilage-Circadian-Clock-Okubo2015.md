# PTH Resets the Cartilage Circadian Clock (Okubo et al. 2015)

A novel connection for [[PTHrP-Ihh Feedback Loop]]: PTH signaling resets the circadian clock specifically within growth-plate cartilage in organ-cultured murine femurs, adding a chronobiology dimension to PTH/PTHrP signaling this domain hadn't previously covered.

> [!gap] Read via abstract; the functional consequence of PTH-driven circadian resetting for actual growth-plate output (proliferation rate, hypertrophy timing) wasn't extracted in full.

## Relevance to This Domain

Adds a circadian-rhythm dimension to [[PTHrP-Ihh Feedback Loop]] not previously covered — whether growth-plate chondrocyte maturation has time-of-day-dependent dynamics is a genuinely new question this paper raises for the domain.
