The height drug worked. A quarter of the patients also showed a suppressed adrenal response.

[cover card]

24 adolescent males with limited growth potential from advancing skeletal maturation. Twelve months of letrozole.

[people quote for efficacy]

Bone age progression relative to chronological age fell from 1.51 to 0.68. Predicted adult height went from minus 1.41 standard deviations to minus 0.64.

[was real]

Low-dose ACTH stimulation testing. Roughly one in four patients showed subnormal responsiveness, and the authors concluded letrozole causes mild adrenal suppression.

[adrenal function]

Aromatase inhibitors are not surgical. The adrenal gland participates in steroid synthesis, so a drug that blocks a step in that chemistry does not only act where you aimed it.

[is the mechanism]

It's the best kind of study — it reported an inconvenient finding alongside a favourable one. That's what makes it worth citing rather than the numbers alone.
