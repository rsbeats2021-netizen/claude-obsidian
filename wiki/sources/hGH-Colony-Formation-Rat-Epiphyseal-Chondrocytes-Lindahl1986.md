# hGH Directly Potentiates Rat Epiphyseal Chondrocyte Colony Formation (Lindahl et al. 1986)

A classic 1986 in vitro demonstration that hGH acts directly on isolated growth-plate chondrocytes: chondrocytes from rat proximal tibial epiphyseal plates, cultured in suspension, formed larger colonies when treated with hGH — direct cellular evidence that GH's growth-promoting action isn't mediated solely through systemic liver-derived IGF-I, predating the later theoretical work of [[Somatotropic-Axis-GH-IGF-Related-Independent-Roles-Butler2001|Butler & Le Roith 2001]] that formalized this "GH acts locally too" understanding.

## Relevance to This Domain

Early direct-action evidence for [[Growth Hormone-IGF-1 Axis]]'s local (non-systemic) GH-action thread, complementing [[GH-Stimulates-Human-Osteoblast-Proliferation-Kassem1993|Kassem et al. 1993]]'s parallel finding in human osteoblasts.
