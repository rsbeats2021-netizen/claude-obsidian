# Intranasal Insulin and Postoperative Delirium in Esophageal Cancer Surgery (Zhang et al.)

A perioperative-neurology study testing whether intranasal long-acting insulin pretreatment reduces postoperative delirium via NLRP3/caspase-1/IL-1β inflammasome signaling in elderly esophageal cancer surgery patients.
