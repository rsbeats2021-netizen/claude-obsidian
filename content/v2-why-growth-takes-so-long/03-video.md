Want to know why. NOT SURFACE LEVEL

[cover card]

Your growth plate is avascular. That means it has no blood vessels running through it.

[your growth plate]

Chondrocytes are the cells in your growth plate that divide to make bone longer. Oxygen based production makes 32.

[so the cells]

Height growth is the slowest anabolic process in your body. Muscle can grow in weeks.

[this is why]

Anything that lowers oxygen delivery to cartilage lowers the ceiling on growth rate. Anything that raises local energy availability raises it.

[section: why it matters]

At the top of the plate sits a thin layer of stem cells called the resting zone. Every one of their divisions produces the columns that lengthen bone.

[section: what runs out]

In rabbits over 95% of resting zone cells were dividing at birth. By 17 weeks that dropped under 10%.

[and they empty fast]

Delaying fusion is the wrong target. Preserving proliferative capacity is the real one.

[takeaway card]

Chasing a date instead of the thing that sets the date.
