2026-10-02: **autoresearch — hormonal effects on TDEE.** Researched how much anabolic androgens, thyroid hormone and GH actually raise daily energy expenditure, per compound. 12 pages: 10 sources, [[Androgens and Energy Expenditure]], [[Research Hormonal Effects on TDEE]], plus the claim page [[trenbolone-raises-metabolic-rate]] (verdict: unsupported). **Central result**: androgens show no reproducible *direct* thermogenic effect — [[Santosa-2010-Sex-Steroid-Withdrawal-REE]] found zero REE change on 3-week testosterone withdrawal/repletion, [[Strawford-1999-Nandrolone-REE-Nitrogen-Balance]] found zero REE change on nandrolone despite 0.5-0.9 kg/week lean accrual, and [[Welle-1992-Testosterone-BMR-Lean-Mass]] found the +7-10% BMR rise disappears after regression-adjusting for lean mass. So the androgen TDEE effect is the resting cost of added tissue (~13 kcal/kg/day muscle): roughly **+45/+68/+103 kcal/day at 125/300/600 mg/week** testosterone, measured values up to +180. Thyroid hormone is the only big lever (**+58% over predicted REE** in frank thyrotoxicosis) and its dose-response is flat within the reference range. GH sits between (**+132 to +172 kcal/day**), with a genuine direct lipolytic component. **Sign of the effect flips with baseline state**: oxandrolone *lowers* REE in hypercatabolic burn patients, trenbolone *lowers* steer fasting metabolic rate ~10%, and supraphysiological doses did not stop RMR falling in contest-prep bodybuilders. **Unmeasured**: no doubly-labelled-water TDEE for any androgen; no REE data for oxymetholone; no human data at all for trenbolone or M-tren; nothing above 600 mg/week. **Next**: the activity-expenditure term (extra training volume permitted by faster recovery) is plausibly larger than the whole resting effect and appears in no trial — worth its own page if the user wants the practical calculator.

## Key Lessons from This Release Cycle

1. Cross-phase audits are essential. Individual phase reviews miss drift between phases.
2. Opt-in feature detection (`[ -x script ] && [ -f state ]`) preserves default plugin behavior for adopters and non-adopters alike.
3. PostToolUse hook matcher is `Write|Edit`, so Bash writes don't fire it. Scripts that mutate tracked state must be Bash-only to avoid side-effect commits.
4. Seed-vault self-consistency matters: if the spec says post-rollout pages need addresses, the concept page itself has to have one.
5. Codex adversarial review rounds stop when the punch list is empty, not when the author feels done.
