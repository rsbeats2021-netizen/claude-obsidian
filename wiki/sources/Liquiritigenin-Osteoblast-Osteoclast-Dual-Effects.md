# Liquiritigenin's Dual Bone-Favorable Effect (Uchino et al.)

A licorice-derived flavonoid with phytoestrogen-like structure, liquiritigenin simultaneously promotes osteoblast differentiation and inhibits osteoclast differentiation — a genuinely dual bone-favorable mechanism, contrasting with [[Genistein-Estradiol-ATDC5-Chondrogenesis-TGFbeta|Takeda et al. 2025]]'s genistein finding (which purely inhibited chondrogenic ECM production at higher doses).

> [!gap] Read via abstract; cell-culture study, in vivo bone-density confirmation not established here.

## Relevance to This Domain

Adds a second phytoestrogen-like compound to this domain's diet-derived bone-active molecule catalog, with a notably more favorable (dual pro-osteoblast/anti-osteoclast) profile than genistein's purely inhibitory chondrogenesis effect.
