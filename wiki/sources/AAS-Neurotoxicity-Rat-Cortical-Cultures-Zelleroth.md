# Neurotoxic Effects of Anabolic Androgenic Steroids in Cortical Cell Culture (Zelleroth et al.)

Extends this domain's anabolic-androgenic-steroid risk profile — already covering [[Deca-Durabolin-Reproductive-Organ-Damage-Rats|reproductive toxicity]] and [[Testosterone-Liver-Kidney-Function-Rabbits|hepatic/renal toxicity]] — to direct neurotoxic effects on cortical neurons, a public-health-relevant finding given AAS use among non-athlete populations underestimating these risks.

## Relevance to This Domain

Adds a neurotoxicity dimension to [[Testosterone]]'s anabolic-androgenic-steroid risk-benefit catalog, alongside the reproductive and organ-toxicity data points already present.
