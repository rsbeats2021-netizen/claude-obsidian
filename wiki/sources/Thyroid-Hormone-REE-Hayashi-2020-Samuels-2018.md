# Thyroid Hormone and REE: Flat Within Range, +58% in Thyrotoxicosis

Three trials together define the shape of the thyroid dose-response curve, which is the most important and least appreciated fact in this area.

## Hayashi 2020 — the upper end

Twenty newly diagnosed Graves' patients, REE by metabolic analyser, compared against Harris-Benedict predicted (BEE) and against 19 healthy volunteers:

| Timepoint | REE / predicted BEE |
|---|---|
| At onset (thyrotoxic) | **1.58 ± 0.28** |
| 1 month treatment | 1.34 ± 0.34 |
| 3 months | 1.06 ± 0.19 |
| 6 months | 1.01 ± 0.16 |

Frank thyrotoxicosis therefore runs REE about **58% above predicted** — on a 1,800 kcal predicted BEE that is **roughly +1,050 kcal/day**. Body weight, muscle mass and lean body mass all *increased* as treatment normalised thyroid function, and post-treatment REE sat *below* the healthy volunteers', which is why these patients gain weight after treatment.

## Samuels 2018 — the flat middle

138 levothyroxine-treated hypothyroid patients randomised double-blind to unchanged, higher or lower LT4 dose, targeting TSH of 0.34-2.50, 2.51-5.60 or 5.61-12.0 mU/L, with doses adjusted every 6 weeks for 6 months. Final mean doses 1.50, 1.32 and 0.78 µg/kg; final TSH 1.85, 3.93 and 9.49 mU/L.

**No substantial metabolic differences between arms** in total, resting or physical-activity energy expenditure, thermic effect of food, substrate oxidation, dietary intake or body composition. (A within-subject correlation between falling thyroid status and falling REE was observed, but the between-arm effect was not material.)

Notably, subjects **preferred the dose they perceived as higher** despite no measurable metabolic benefit.

## Chen 2022 — no acute effect

12 healthy volunteers, randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover: oral liothyronine 0.7 µg/kg (T3 Cmax 421 ng/dL, Tmax 120 min), equimolar levothyroxine, or placebo. **No change** in energy expenditure, heart rate, blood pressure, haemodynamics or echocardiography.

## The shape of the curve

Flat from mildly hypothyroid to high-normal. Flat for a single acute dose. Steep only once circulating hormone is frankly supraphysiological and sustained. This means:

- Small T3 doses "to boost metabolism" sit on the flat part of the curve and do essentially nothing measurable.
- Large sustained doses do work, dramatically, by pushing into iatrogenic thyrotoxicosis — which is the same state that carries atrial fibrillation and bone-loss risk.
- There is no dose that gives a large metabolic effect without the thyrotoxic state, because the metabolic effect *is* the thyrotoxic state.

> [!gap] No controlled trial administers supraphysiological T3 to healthy euthyroid adults and measures REE, because deliberately inducing thyrotoxicosis is not ethically approvable. The +58% figure comes from disease, not dosing, and the dose equivalent must be inferred from circulating hormone concentrations.

## Relevance to This Domain

Thyroid hormone is by a wide margin the largest TDEE lever of the three drug classes, and the only one where the effect is directly on existing tissue rather than mediated by added tissue. It is also the only one where the mechanism of the benefit is inseparable from the mechanism of the harm.
