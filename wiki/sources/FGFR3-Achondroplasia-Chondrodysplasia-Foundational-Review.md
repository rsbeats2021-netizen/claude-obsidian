# FGFR3 Mutation Mechanisms in Achondroplasia and Related Chondrodysplasias (Foundational Review)

A foundational review detailing the precise molecular mechanisms by which FGFR3 gain-of-function mutations cause achondroplasia and its more severe related syndromes (hypochondroplasia, SADDAN, thanatophoric dysplasia): a combination of receptor stabilization, enhanced dimerization, and enhanced tyrosine kinase activity, all converging on excessive FGFR3 signaling in chondrocytes and mature osteoblasts. This mechanistic detail is the direct foundation for this domain's FGFR3-targeted drug-development thread.
