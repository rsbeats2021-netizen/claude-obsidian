# Sex Steroids Modulate Maximal GH-Secretagogue-Driven Pulsatile Secretion (Veldhuis et al. 2004)

Directly connects sex-steroid physiology to this domain's GH-pulsatility mechanism thread: both gender and systemic estradiol/testosterone concentrations independently contribute to the maximal achievable drive of pulsatile GH secretion, tested via GH-secretagogue infusion in healthy middle-aged and older adults — showing sex-steroid status modulates not just baseline GH levels but the ceiling of pharmacologically-stimulated GH pulse amplitude.
