Growth hormone never lengthens a bone itself. It works through two routes: the
liver making IGF-1, and GH acting directly on the growth plate. Your blood test
only sees one of them.

Hunziker 1994, Peng 1995, Lundberg 2015. Comment SOURCES for the PDFs.
