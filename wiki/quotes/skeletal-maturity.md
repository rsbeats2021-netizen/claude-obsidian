# Quotes — skeletal maturity and growth remaining

---

> At Sanders stage 2, when "the epiphyses are as wide as the metaphyses", the child "has entered their adolescent growth spurt at 85% of adult height, 2 years before the peak growth."

- **Source:** Sanders JO et al., *Scientific Reports* 2017
- **Tier:** B — two longitudinal cohorts to final adult height (Brush n=54, Berkeley n=135)
- **Shows:** a readable hand radiograph converts to a percentage of final height
- **Figure:** [[fig-2017-sanders-stage-curve]]
- **Used in:** [[deck-how-much-is-left]] slides 05, 08

---

> "The peak is reached at Sanders stage 3 when all the digits are capping, at 90% of final height."

- **Source:** Sanders JO et al., *Scientific Reports* 2017 · **Tier:** B
- **Used in:** [[deck-how-much-is-left]] slide 06

---

> Growth "slows markedly at 1.5 years after the peak at 96% of final height when the distal phalanges begin to fuse."

- **Source:** Sanders JO et al., *Scientific Reports* 2017 · **Tier:** B
- **Used in:** [[deck-how-much-is-left]] slide 07

---

> "The growth patterns of both boys and girls become identical during the adolescent growth spurt" once normalised to final height and standardised by peak growth age.

- **Source:** Sanders JO et al., *Scientific Reports* 2017 · **Tier:** B
- **Shows:** the staging is sex-independent, which is why a hand film alone is readable
- **Used in:** [[deck-how-much-is-left]] slide 09
