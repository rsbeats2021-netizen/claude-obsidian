Growth hormone and IGF-1. They act on different stages of the same cell lineage.

[cover card]

Growth hormone hits the liver, the liver makes IGF-1, IGF-1 does the growing. Clean, popular, and incomplete.

[says gh works via igf-1]

Hypophysectomised rats — no endogenous pituitary hormone at all — then infused either GH or IGF-1 separately. No confounds, one variable at a time.

[removed the pituitary]

GH primarily pulls resting-zone chondrocytes into the proliferative pool. It decides how many cells enter the production line.

[recruits]

IGF-1 acts on cells already proliferating, driving their expansion. It decides how much each recruited cell produces.

[amplifies]

Recruiting more cells and expanding existing ones are different operations. Maximising one does not substitute for the other, and neither alone reproduces normal growth.

[they aren't interchangeable]

GH acts directly on human growth plate stem cells through JAK/STAT and local TGF-beta, independent of the liver IGF-1 axis. Same conclusion, thirty years later.
