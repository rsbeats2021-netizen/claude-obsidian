# Cartilage Peptides for Chondrocyte Proliferation and Geroprotection (Myakisheva et al. 2023)

Another paper from the Khavinson-tradition St. Petersburg Institute of Bioregulation and Gerontology research group — co-authored by Linkova, the same author whose [[Peptide-Regulation-Chondrogenic-Stem-Cell-Differentiation|2023 review]] was the one genuinely on-topic find from that cluster (independently corroborating Sox9's dual-role finding). This paper is a primary experimental study, not a review: a bovine-cartilage-derived polypeptide complex (PPKH) and its component tripeptide AED increased chondrocyte proliferation in cultured young and old rat intervertebral-disc cartilage, more effectively in old animals — framed as both chondroprotective and geroprotective, with Phase II clinical trials underway for knee osteoarthritis.

> [!contradiction] Flagged as topically unrelated to this domain: unlike Linkova's 2023 review (which explicitly corroborated this vault's Sox9/Runx2 findings), this paper contains no mention of Sox9, Runx2, estrogen, androgen, or growth-plate biology — it's a geroprotection/osteoarthritis peptide-pharmacology study, adjacent to but distinct from this domain's steroid-hormone focus.
