# Coutts et al. 1997 — 250 mg DHT-hp in Healthy Men

*Clin Chem* 43(11):2091–8. PMID 9365393. DOI 10.1093/clinchem/43.11.2091. Full text paywalled; **vehicle and concentration not stated anywhere accessible**.

The only published dose of a DHT ester in **healthy adult men**: **250 mg IM, single dose, n = 6**. That is **180.3 mg DHT-equivalent**; at the patent's 190 mg/mL sesame-oil strength it would be ~1.32 mL (inference, not reported).

Markers tested against the discrimination limits proposed for DHT doping confirmation:
- primary: urinary **DHT/epitestosterone**
- secondary: **5α-androstane-3α,17β-diol/EpiT**, **LH**, **5β-androstane-3α,17β-diol**

Results: **all four markers greatly exceeded the published discrimination limits within 24 h**, remained above them for **10–14 days**, and **all ratios returned to basal values by day 28**.

The informative discrepancy: unlike after percutaneous DHT, **5β-Adiol excretion fell** — the authors attribute this to greater suppression of testicular steroidogenesis by the IM route. Otherwise results agreed with percutaneous administration, "probably augmented by the larger dose and the different route of delivery."

Day-28 clearance on a single 250 mg dose matches the 4–6 week return-to-baseline from [[Keenan-1987-DHT-Heptanoate-Pharmacokinetics]].
