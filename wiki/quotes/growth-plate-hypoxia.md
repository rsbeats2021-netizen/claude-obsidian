# Quotes — growth plate hypoxia

Verbatim only. Verified against the papers this session.

---

> "Massive cell death occurred in the inner zones of developing growth plates."

- **Source:** Zhang F et al., *Nature* 2023 · [[10.1038-s41586-023-06611-6--haemoglobin-body-chondrocyte]]
- **Tier:** C — chondrocyte-specific haemoglobin knockout, mouse
- **Shows:** the growth plate cannot survive its own architecture without a local oxygen store
- **Figure:** [[fig-2023-zhang-knockout]]
- **Used in:** [[deck-no-blood-supply]] slide 08

---

> "Hedy functions as a localized oxygen reservoir, plays a crucial role in enabling chondrocyte adaptation to hypoxia and ensuring cartilage survival."

- **Source:** Zhang F et al., *Nature* 2023 · [[10.1038-s41586-023-06611-6--haemoglobin-body-chondrocyte]]
- **Tier:** C
- **Shows:** cartilage manufactures and stores its own haemoglobin, outside red blood cells
- **Figure:** [[fig-2023-zhang-hedy]]
- **Used in:** [[deck-no-blood-supply]] slide 05

---

> P50 for haemoglobin-containing chondrocytes measured **27.85 mmHg** versus **58.2 mmHg** for red blood cells.

- **Source:** Zhang F et al., *Nature* 2023 · [[10.1038-s41586-023-06611-6--haemoglobin-body-chondrocyte]]
- **Tier:** C
- **Shows:** a left-shifted dissociation curve, meaning storage rather than delivery
- **Figure:** [[fig-2023-zhang-p50]]
- **Used in:** [[deck-no-blood-supply]] slide 07
- **Note:** the single most quotable number in this topic. Nobody in the corpus has it.

---

> "HIF-1α is essential for chondrocyte growth arrest and survival."

- **Source:** Schipani E et al., *Genes & Development* 2001
- **Tier:** C — conditional knockout, mouse
- **Shows:** the hypoxia response is required, not incidental
- **Figure:** [[fig-2001-schipani-central-death]]
- **Used in:** [[deck-no-blood-supply]] slides 03-04
