# Testosterone Dose and Age Determine Conversion to Estradiol vs. DHT (Lakshman et al. 2010)

A foundational human pharmacokinetic study directly measuring how both injected testosterone dose and patient age affect the proportion converted to estradiol (via aromatase) versus DHT (via 5α-reductase) in men. This supplies a quantitative dose-response and age-response framework for interpreting this domain's testosterone/aromatization/DHT literature — since many of this domain's findings depend on assuming a roughly fixed conversion ratio, when in fact the ratio itself shifts with both dose and age.

## Relevance to This Domain

A foundational quantitative pharmacokinetic reference for [[Testosterone]], [[Aromatase]], and [[5-Alpha-Reductase]] — establishes that the testosterone-to-estradiol-vs-DHT conversion ratio is not fixed but varies with dose and age, a parameter this domain's other mechanism papers generally hold constant.
