## Notes
- Slide 4 is the whole deck. $35k/inch is the most memorable number in the
  topic and it's the screenshot people will share.
- Slide 7's empty chart is a real device — visualising absence of evidence is
  more honest and more striking than asserting it in text.
- Slide 9 (the correction) is a positioning asset, not an embarrassment. A
  channel built on rigour gains more from a visible correction than from
  looking consistent.
- Figure catalogue is thin (2 starred, both GH-IGF-1 axis overviews). This deck
  is mostly numbers, so redraws are the right call anyway.
