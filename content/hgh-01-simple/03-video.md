How HGH. There are two routes. Your blood test sees one.

[cover card]

So lets start with the thing most people get wrong about growth hormone. GH is made in your pituitary gland, which sits underneath your brain.

[near your legs]

GH also travels directly to the growth plate itself and acts on the cells there, without going anywhere near your liver first. Lundberg 2015 states it plainly: GH has effects on longitudinal bone growth both directly at the growth plate and locally mediated through IGF-1.

[straight to the plate]

Hunziker 1994 measured exactly how each one acts. Untreated those cells took 50 days per division.

[gh versus igf-1]

Here is why that matters more than it sounds. Not GH stretching a bone, but GH making the cell factory at the top of the plate run faster than it otherwise would.

[is the factory]

It works mainly by speeding up the reserve cells at the top, which feed everything below. And the dose response bends rather than climbing forever.

[takeaway card]
