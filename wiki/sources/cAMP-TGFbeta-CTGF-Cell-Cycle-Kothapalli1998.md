# cAMP Inhibits TGF-β-Stimulated CTGF via a Cell-Cycle Restriction Point (Kothapalli et al. 1998)

Connects two signaling pathways already separately relevant to this domain: cAMP (central to [[PTHrP-HDAC4-MEF2-Chondrocyte-Hypertrophy-Kozhemyakina2009|Kozhemyakina et al. 2009]]'s PTHrP mechanism) and TGF-β (central to [[Genistein-Estradiol-ATDC5-Chondrogenesis-TGFbeta|Takeda et al. 2025]]'s genistein-chondrogenesis-inhibition mechanism) — cAMP directly suppresses TGF-β-stimulated CTGF expression, identifying a specific cell-cycle checkpoint where these two pathways intersect.

## Relevance to This Domain

A direct mechanistic bridge between [[PTHrP-Ihh Feedback Loop]]'s cAMP signaling and [[Genistein-Estradiol-ATDC5-Chondrogenesis-TGFbeta|Takeda et al. 2025]]'s TGF-β mechanism, both otherwise treated as separate pathways in this domain.
