# Androgen Treatment, Bone Age, and Height in 5α-Reductase Type 2 Deficiency

A clinical human-genetics natural experiment relevant to this domain's androgen/bone-age literature: children with 5α-reductase type 2 deficiency (unable to convert testosterone to DHT) who received androgen treatment (DHT or testosterone enanthate) to promote genital development showed below-average height relative to bone age, despite above-average absolute height — particularly pronounced in the treated group. Directly parallels this domain's other anabolic-androgen findings (Stirling 1994, fluoxymesterone 1982) that androgen treatment tends to advance skeletal (bone-age) maturation disproportionately relative to the height gained.

## Relevance to This Domain

A genetic-deficiency clinical data point for [[5-Alpha-Reductase]] and [[Dihydrotestosterone (DHT)]], reinforcing this domain's recurring finding that androgen treatment often advances bone age faster than it adds height.
