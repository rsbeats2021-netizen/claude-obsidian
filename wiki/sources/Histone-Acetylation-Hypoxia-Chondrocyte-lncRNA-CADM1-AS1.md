# Hypoxia-Driven Epigenetic Regulation of Chondrocyte Homeostasis via lncRNA CADM1-AS1

Connects hypoxia — already one of this domain's four competing theories for growth-plate fusion via [[Epiphyseal Fusion Mechanisms]] — to a specific epigenetic mechanism: significant histone acetylation changes occur in chondrocytes under hypoxic conditions, regulated through a key enhancer element controlling the long non-coding RNA CADM1-AS1, essential for cartilage homeostasis. Conducted exclusively in male mice.

> [!gap] Conducted exclusively in male mice; whether this hypoxia-epigenetics mechanism shows the sex-dependent patterns seen elsewhere in this domain's chondrocyte literature (Nasatzky, Ekstein, Raz) is untested.

## Relevance to This Domain

A specific epigenetic mechanism (histone acetylation, lncRNA CADM1-AS1) connecting hypoxia to chondrocyte gene regulation, relevant to [[Epiphyseal Fusion Mechanisms]]'s hypoxia theory and complementing [[Hypoxia-MSC-Proliferation-Grayson2007|Grayson et al. 2007]]'s more general hypoxia/proliferation finding.
