# Claims — gh-thyroid-drain

## Martins & Abucham 2008, Arq Bras Endocrinol Metabol 52(5):889-900 (PMID 18797597)
Verified against the PubMed record, 3 Oct 2026. Review.
QUOTE: "In growth hormone deficient patients, somatotrophic replacement increases
the conversion rate of the inactive form of the thyroid hormone (T4) to its active
form (T3), whereas the same replacement induces the conversion of cortisol, which
is hormonally active, in cortisone, its inactive form."
→ Two separate axes moved by GH: thyroid activation, cortisol inactivation.

## Compendium — the mechanism and the trap
- "GH therapy in adults alters thyroid function via upregulation of type 2
  iodothyronine deiodinase (DIO2); following rhGH therapy, median serum free T3
  significantly increased alongside a rise in the free T3/free T4 ratio"
- "this effect is dose-dependent, with highly significant GH dose-dependent
  increases in total and free T3 and a reduction in rT3 as GH dose rises"
- "Following GH replacement, mean free T4 declined significantly
  (−1.09 ± 1.99 pmol/L), reverse T3 fell, and free T3 rose significantly"
- "GH accelerates T4-to-T3 flux, consuming T4 faster, so the T4 substrate pool
  shrinks even as T3 bioavailability from available T4 increases"
- "GH raises free T3 significantly without changing T4 or TSH much, so a person on
  exogenous GH has higher effective T3 activity than TSH/T4 alone would suggest"
- "In adult hypopituitary patients, GH replacement has been reported to unmask
  central hypothyroidism biochemically in 36–47% of apparently euthyroid patients,
  necessitating thyroxine replacement, via GH suppressing TRH sensitivity at the
  hypothalamic level and blunting TSH pulsatility"

## Population discipline
The 36-47% unmasking figure is in **hypopituitary adults on GH replacement**, a
population that already has pituitary disease. The slide states that population
explicitly rather than generalising it.

## Connects to
The HGH series: another case where the standard blood marker (TSH) fails to report
what the axis is doing.
