# Neurosteroid (Allopregnanolone/Pregnanolone) Steroidogenesis Review

A review of the enzymatic synthesis pathway producing allopregnanolone and pregnanolone — GABAergic neurosteroids derived from progesterone — and their implicated roles across a wide range of neuropsychiatric conditions (seizure disorders, PTSD, depression, Parkinson's disease, Alzheimer's disease, neurotrauma, stroke).
