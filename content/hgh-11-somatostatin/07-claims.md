# Claims — hgh-11-somatostatin

## Spoudeas et al. 1992, JCEM 75(2):453-8 (PMID 1353504) — human
Verified against the PubMed record, 2 Oct 2026. Six adult males, GHRH stimuli on
a background infusion of saline, somatostatin, or oral pyridostigmine.
QUOTE: "We conclude that the GH response to GHRH is dependent on SS tone"
QUOTE: "SS attenuates the rate of GH release without changing the duration of
secretion and appears important in terminating GH secretion."
QUOTE: "Deconvolution analysis of serum GH values demonstrated that the pituitary
responded to two GHRH stimuli 90 min apart without attenuation of the second
response." (on saline background)
QUOTE: "The higher dose of SS-(1-14) significantly blunted the first GH response;
second GH responses were further attenuated by both SS-(1-14) doses."
QUOTE: "Peak stimulated serum TSH was attenuated by both SS-(1-14) doses, but
pyridostigmine had no effect." → the thyrotroph is not tonically inhibited; this
is specific to GH.

## Compendium, from the vault
`wiki/chats/Research Compendium.md`
- "Hypophysiotropic somatostatin neurons in the PeVN suppress GHRH neurons (ARC)
  via K+ channel activation ... producing the slow population oscillation that
  generates GH pulsatility — somatostatin sets both the floor and the rhythm of
  GH secretion"
- "GH pulse amplitude is set by GHRH surging while somatostatin is in a trough;
  pulse frequency and the inter-pulse nadir are set by somatostatin, so a drug
  raising tonic somatostatin flattens the pulse profile rather than simply
  lowering mean GH" — marked [^unsourced] in the compendium. The slide states the
  amplitude/rhythm split, which Spoudeas supports directly; it does NOT assert the
  drug claim.
- "Roughly 70% of daily GH output in men occurs during early sleep; the
  sleep-onset pulse is caused by a GHRH surge coinciding with a circadian period
  of relative somatostatin disinhibition, about 70% of sleep GH pulses coincide
  with slow-wave sleep"
- "hypophysial portal sampling is not possible in living humans, so every
  statement about human somatostatin tone is inferred from pharmacological
  probes" → stated on its own slide.
