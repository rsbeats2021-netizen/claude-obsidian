# Claims — hgh-04-exogenous

## 1. Isaksson, Jansson & Gause 1982, Science 216:1237-9 (PMID 7079756)
Verified against the PubMed record, 2 Oct 2026.
QUOTE (abstract, verbatim): "Local administration of human growth hormone in vivo
to the cartilage growth plate of the proximal tibia of hypophysectomized rats
resulted in accelerated longitudinal bone growth. This finding suggests that
growth hormone directly stimulates the cells in the growth plate, and does not
support the theory that the increase in the plasma concentration of somatomedin
that follows growth hormone administration is the cause of this stimulation."
→ One leg injected, the other is the control, inside the same animal with one
shared bloodstream. Confirmed independently: Russell & Spencer 1985, "Local
injections of human or rat growth hormone ... stimulate unilateral tibial
epiphyseal growth in hypophysectomized rats."

## 2. Loveridge et al. 1995, J Endocrinol 146:55-62 (rat)

QUOTE: "Circulating total IGF-I concentrations were unchanged in the rats given
the GH antiserum compared with pair-fed non-immune-treated animals (24 h:
9.9 ± 0.5 vs 10.4 ± 0.9 nmol/l; 4 days: 10.3 ± 1.0 vs 10.7 ± 0.9 nmol/l; 10 days:
10.4 ± 1.4 vs 11.6 ± 0.8 nmol/l)"
QUOTE: "By 4 days, the total number of chondrocytes synthesising DNA and the width

although the labelling index was unchanged."
QUOTE: "the germinal layer has the highest concentration of GH receptors ...
suggesting that the role of GH may be to commit the germinal chondrocytes into a
proliferative pool"
→ Block GH, the plate shrinks, serum IGF-1 does not move.

## 3. Lui et al. 2010 — cartilage-targeted IGF-1 (mouse)
`.raw/.../Cartilage-Targeted IGF-1 Treatment to Promote Longitudinal Bone Growth - Lui et al - 2010.pdf`
QUOTE: "non-targeted IGF-1 fusion protein did not show any significant effect on
growth, suggesting the effects of IGF1-c22 and -c26 fusion proteins are due to
their ability to target the growth plate to attain a higher local concentration
and/or longer retention time at the cartilage"
→ Same molecule. Only the delivery address differs. One works, one does not.

## 4. Hunziker et al. 1994, J Clin Invest (hypophysectomised rat)
`.raw/.../Differential Effects of Insulin-Like Growth Factor I and Growth Hormone on - Hunziker et al - 1994.pdf`
QUOTE: "Stem cell cycle time was shortened from 50 to 15 and 8 d after treatment
with IGF-I and GH, respectively." (normal rats: 6 d)
QUOTE: "Proliferating cell cycle time decreased from 11 to 4.5 and 3 d, and
duration of the hypertrophic phase decreased from 6 to 4 and 2.8 d."
QUOTE: "Mean terminal cell height in the NaCl group (19.5 µm) was significantly
less than that in either the IGF-I (27.3 µm), GH (26.5 µm), or normal rat
(29.8 µm) groups"
QUOTE: "Mean cell volume in the NaCl group (6,370 µm3) was significantly smaller
than that in either the IGF-I (9,890), GH (11,140), or normal rat (10,246) groups"
QUOTE: "Under physiological conditions, cell hypertrophy has been shown to be the
most efficient cellular process contributing to bone elongation."
QUOTE (productivity): GH "during the stem and proliferating phases, this was in
the order of five- to sevenfold, and during hypertrophy three- to fourfold.
IGF-I was less effective; it induced an approximately fourfold increase ... and a
twofold one during hypertrophy."
QUOTE (the inversion, reported by Loveridge citing this work): both act on
chondrocyte development "with GH being the more effective even though it induced
a lower level of circulating IGF-I than seen in the normal or IGF-I treated"

## 5. Isgaard et al. 1988, Endocrinology — pattern changes LOCAL IGF-1 only
QUOTE: "in the skeletal muscle pulsatile treatment with GH at 3-h intervals
induced a 3- to 5-fold increase in the levels of IGF-I mRNA ... whereas continuous
treatment with GH exerted no effect"
QUOTE: "There was no significant difference between the effects of pulsatile and
continuous GH on hepatic IGF-I mRNA in the present study."

## 6. BONE AGE — Nwosu et al. 2021, J Endocr Soc 5(5):bvab036 (PMID 33860132)
Verified against the PubMed record, 2 Oct 2026. 71 children, treated 2-14 y
(mean 5.5 y).
QUOTE: "Bone age minus chronological age (BA-CA) did not differ significantly
between baseline and the end of rhGH therapy (-1.05 ± 1.42 vs -0.69 ± 1.63,
P = 0.09)."
QUOTE: "Piecewise regression, however, showed a quantifiable catch-up phenomenon
in BA of 1.5 months per year of rhGH therapy in the first 6.5 years"
QUOTE: "Long-term rhGH therapy demonstrated an initial catch-up phenomenon in
skeletal maturation in the first 6.5 years that plateaued thereafter with no
overall significant advancement in bone age."
Supporting, Mauras et al. 2000, JCEM: "bone ages advancing approximately
1 yr/chronological yr in both the standard and high dose groups" and high dose
"did not increase the rate of skeletal maturation".
COUNTERPOINT, kept in the deck: Ly et al. 2020 notes "supraphysiological doses
have been shown to accelerate bone maturation". The reassuring data is at
therapeutic doses.

## 7. TOTAL GAINS — Maghnie et al. 2022, JCEM 107(12):3287-3301 (PMID 36102184)
Full KIGS cohort. Verified against the PubMed record, 2 Oct 2026.
QUOTE: "The full KIGS cohort (N = 83 803 [58% male])"
QUOTE: "Median gains in NAH-SDS were 1.79 (IGHD), 1.37 (ISS), and 1.34 (SGA) for
boys, and 2.07 (IGHD), 1.62 (ISS), 1.07 (TS), and 1.57 (SGA) for girls."
QUOTE: "Median first-year delta height-SD score (Prader) in prepubertal patients
was 0.66 (IGHD), 0.55 (ISS), 0.58 (TS), and 0.71 (SGA)."
SAFETY, quoted: "SAEs occurred in 3.7% of patients and death in 0.4%. The most
common SAEs were recurrence of craniopharyngioma (n = 151), neoplasm (n = 99),
and cancer (n = 91); and scoliosis (n = 91)."
→ CONFOUND, stated on the slide: 10% of this cohort had organic GHD, i.e. brain
tumours and their treatment. Craniopharyngioma recurrence is the underlying
disease, not demonstrated to be a drug effect.

## 8. DOSE CEILING — Mauras et al. 2000, JCEM
QUOTE: "significantly taller at near-adult height ... by an average of 4.6 cm

## Scope of the evidence
- No SDS-to-centimetre conversion is put on a slide as if it were measured. KIGS
  reports SDS; the deck reports SDS.
- Every human figure is from children with a growth disorder, on prescribed
  replacement. Not enhancement in a normal axis. Stated on the slide.
- Isaksson, Loveridge, Hunziker, Isgaard and Lui are rodent. Stated.
