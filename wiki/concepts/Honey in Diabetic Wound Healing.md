# Honey in Diabetic Wound Healing

Topical use of honey to manage diabetic wounds/ulcers, which heal slowly due to peripheral arterial disease and neuropathy reducing blood flow to affected tissue.

---

## Mechanism

- **Antimicrobial**: acidic pH, hydrogen peroxide, and nitric-oxide metabolites lower infection at the wound site.
- **Antioxidant / anti-inflammatory**: reduces reactive oxygen species and inflammation, aiding the healing process.
- **Tissue growth stimulation**: assimilable sugars, vitamins, amino acids, and phenolics increase oxygen and nutrient delivery to the wound area.

Medical-grade honeys with standardized antibacterial activity exist commercially (e.g., Apiban, Woundcare 18+, Medihoney); Manuka honey specifically has evidence for improving cell proliferation at wound sites.

---

## Evidence Strength

> [!gap] Cited studies show "successful honey treatments" reducing wound size, pain, and healing time, but the review does not report controlled-trial-level detail (sample sizes, randomization) for the wound-healing claims specifically — weaker evidentiary basis than the glycemic-effect claims in [[Honey Hypoglycemic Mechanism]].
