# Oxandrolone DECREASES REE in Burn Patients While Increasing Lean Mass

The clearest demonstration that "anabolic" and "raises metabolic rate" are independent properties.

Severe burns produce a hypercatabolic state: elevated catecholamines and corticosteroids, suppressed testosterone, REE far above predicted, growth arrest for up to 2 years. Oxandrolone was given for 12 months (0.1 mg/kg twice daily) in a placebo-controlled trial (Porro 2012, n=222) and in a 612-patient randomised trial with propranolol arms (Herndon 2016).

## Result

Oxandrolone **substantially decreased resting energy expenditure** and rate-pressure product, **increased** IGF-1, lean body mass, bone mineral content, muscle strength, height and weight. Propranolol separately decreased REE and increased peripheral lean mass; the combination added benefit.

## The mechanism this reveals

In a hypercatabolic state, the androgen's effect is to **attenuate the catabolic drive** — less protein breakdown, less futile cycling, lower cardiac work — which lowers REE even as tissue accrues. In a normal-metabolic-state adult ([[Welle-1992-Testosterone-BMR-Lean-Mass]]) there is no catabolic drive to attenuate, so only the tissue-accrual term is left and REE rises.

**The direction of the REE response therefore depends on the patient's baseline metabolic state, not on the drug.** This explains apparently contradictory literature without needing to discount any of it.

> [!gap] Paediatric burn patients on a specific oral 17-alpha-alkylated androgen. Generalising the *magnitude* to healthy adults is unjustified; the *principle* (baseline state determines sign) is what transfers.

## Relevance to This Domain

Resolves the apparent contradiction between the testosterone studies (REE up), the nandrolone study (REE flat) and the trenbolone steer study (REE down). Baseline catabolic state is the hidden variable.
