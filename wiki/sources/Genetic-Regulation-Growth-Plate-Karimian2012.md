# Genetic Regulation of the Growth Plate: Review (Karimian, Chagin, Sävendahl 2012)

A 2012 review from the same Karolinska/Andrei Chagin research lineage central to this domain's epSSC cluster (via [[Skeletal-Stem-Cells-Growth-Plate-Hedgehog-Signaling|Trompet et al. 2024]] and related papers), synthesizing growth-plate genetic regulation as understood before that lab's later stem-cell-specific discoveries — useful background tracing how this research program's focus evolved over the following decade-plus.

## Relevance to This Domain

Background/historical context for the Chagin lab's later epSSC work in [[Epiphyseal Skeletal Stem Cells and Hedgehog Signaling]], and a synthesizing reference for [[Molecular Regulators of Growth Plate Chondrocyte Maturation]].
