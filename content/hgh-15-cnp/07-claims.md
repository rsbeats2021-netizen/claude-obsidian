# Claims — hgh-15-cnp

## Olney et al. 2006, JCEM 91(4):1229-32 (PMID 16384845) — human
Verified against the PubMed record, 3 Oct 2026. 39 members of one family.
QUOTE: "Height z-scores of these carriers were -1.8 +/- 1.1 (mean +/- sd), which
was significantly less than the 23 noncarrier family members (-0.4 +/- 0.8,
P < 0.0005) and the general population (P < 0.0005)."
QUOTE: "there was no difference in body proportion between carriers and
noncarriers"
QUOTE: "approximately one in 30 individuals with idiopathic short stature are
carriers of NPR2 mutations" (their estimate, assuming 1 in 700 carry a mutation)
QUOTE: "The proband with AMDM had low IGF-I levels and evidence of GH resistance,
as well as very high plasma levels of CNP and its amino-terminal propeptide."
→ A receptor defect in the CNP pathway produced GH resistance. The two pathways
are not independent. This is on a slide.
QUOTE: "Loss-of-function mutations affecting the CNP receptor natriuretic peptide
receptor-B (gene NPR2) cause the autosomal recessive skeletal dysplasia,
acromesomelic dysplasia, Maroteaux type (AMDM)."

## Yasoda / Nakao group 2015, Sci Rep 5:10554 (PMID 26014585) — mouse
QUOTE: "Bones were severely shorter in cartilage-specific CNP or GC-B knockout
mice and the extent was almost the same as that in respective systemic knockout
mice."
QUOTE: "Hypertrophic chondrocyte layer of the growth plate was drastically reduced
and proliferative chondrocyte layer, along with the proliferation of chondrocytes
there, was moderately reduced in either cartilage-specific knockout mice."
→ Deleting CNP only in cartilage is almost as bad as deleting it everywhere. The
CNP that matters is made locally in the plate, exactly as with IGF-1.

## Savarirayan et al. 2020, Lancet 396:684-692 (PMID 32891212) — human phase 3
QUOTE: "The adjusted mean difference in annualised growth velocity between
patients in the vosoritide group and placebo group was 1·57 cm/year in favour of
vosoritide (95% CI [1·22-1·93]; two-sided p<0·0001)."
QUOTE: design — "vosoritide 15·0 μg/kg or placebo ... for the duration of the
52-week treatment period administered by daily subcutaneous injections", 121
randomised, 119 completed.
QUOTE, the caveat, kept on a slide: "It is not known whether final adult height
will be increased, or what the harms of long-term therapy might be."

## Mechanism, from the compendium
- "cGMP activates cyclic GMP-dependent protein kinase II (cGKII), which, through
  p38 MAPK activation, functionally antagonizes RAF1 activation of MEK,
  effectively lifting the brakes that FGFR3 signaling puts on chondrocyte
  proliferation; this is why vosoritide works in achondroplasia"
- "cGMP-dependent kinases I and II are expressed at much higher levels (about 5-7
  fold) in hypertrophic cells than in other growth-plate zones" — compendium does
  not attach a hard citation to the fold figure, so the slide says the hypertrophic
  zone is disproportionately responsive without quoting a number.
