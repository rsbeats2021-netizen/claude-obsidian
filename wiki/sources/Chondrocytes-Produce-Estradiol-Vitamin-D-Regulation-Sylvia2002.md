# Vitamin D Regulates Local Chondrocyte Estradiol Production (Sylvia et al. 2002)

From the Boyan/Schwartz lab lineage, this paper adds a new regulatory layer to this domain's local-estrogen-synthesis thread ([[Local-Estrogen-Production-Growth-Plate-Cartilage-Nilsson2001|Nilsson et al. 2001]], [[Aromatase-Transcripts-Human-Growth-Plate-Nawata|human growth-plate aromatase expression]]): rat costochondral chondrocytes directly produce 17β-estradiol locally, and this local production is itself regulated by 1,25(OH)2D3 (active vitamin D) — connecting the vitamin D and estrogen hormone systems at the level of local growth-plate steroidogenesis, a regulatory link this domain hadn't previously mapped.

## Relevance to This Domain

Connects vitamin D signaling to local chondrocyte estrogen synthesis, adding a regulatory upstream layer to [[Local-Estrogen-Production-Growth-Plate-Cartilage-Nilsson2001|this domain's local-estrogen-production thread]].
