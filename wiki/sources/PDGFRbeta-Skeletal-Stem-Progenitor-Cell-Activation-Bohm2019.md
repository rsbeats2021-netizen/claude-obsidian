# PDGFRβ Signaling Activates Skeletal Stem/Progenitor Cells for Bone Repair (Böhm et al. 2019)

Extends [[Differential-Regulation-Skeletal-Stem-Progenitor-Cells-Compartments|Solidum et al. 2023]]'s point that different skeletal stem/progenitor cell (SSPC) niches are independently regulated: here, PDGFRβ signaling is shown to be functionally required (not just correlated) for SSPC activation, proliferation, trafficking, and angiotropism specifically in the bone-injury-repair context — a distinct activation mechanism from the Hedgehog/PTHrP/Wnt pathways governing the growth-plate epSSC population covered elsewhere in this domain.

## Relevance to This Domain

A functionally-validated (loss-of-function) SSPC activation mechanism for the injury-repair context, complementing [[Epiphyseal Skeletal Stem Cells and Hedgehog Signaling]]'s growth-plate-focused Hedgehog/PTHrP/Wnt mechanisms with a distinct PDGFRβ-driven pathway for a different SSPC niche.
