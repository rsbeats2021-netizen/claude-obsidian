# ERα-Interacting Cytokeratins Potentiate Fulvestrant's Antiestrogenic Activity (Long et al. 2010)

A fourth fulvestrant-in-oncology paper encountered in this ingest (after [[Fulvestrant-Breast-Cancer-Review-Nathan-Schmid|Nathan & Schmid 2017]], [[Fulvestrant-Immune-Response-Breast-Cancer-Dabrosin|Abrahamsson et al. 2020]], and [[Fulvestrant-ER-Antagonist-Mechanism-Osborne|Osborne et al. 2004]]), this one identifying specific cytokeratin proteins that interact with ERα to enhance fulvestrant's receptor-degrading activity in breast cancer cells.

> [!contradiction] Flagged as topically unrelated to this domain's growth-plate/bone focus for the same reason as the other fulvestrant/oncology papers: mechanistic breast-cancer drug-resistance research, not growth or bone biology.
