# Research: Hormonal Effects on TDEE (Androgens, Thyroid Hormone, GH)

## Overview

Thyroid hormone is the only one of the three drug classes with a large, direct effect on energy expenditure, and the effect appears only once dosing is frankly supraphysiological. Growth hormone has a moderate, dose-dependent, partly direct effect. Anabolic androgens have **no reproducible direct effect at all** — their TDEE contribution is almost entirely the resting cost of the lean tissue they add, which is far smaller than the niche assumes.

### Per-compound answers to the question asked

- **Testosterone** — the only androgen with directly measured REE data. **+7-10% of BMR at 3 mg/kg/week**, fully explained by lean mass in healthy men. Derived from the dose-response in (Source: [[Bhasin-2001-Testosterone-Dose-Response-Fat-Free-Mass]]): **+45 kcal/d at 125 mg/wk, +68 at 300 mg/wk, +103 at 600 mg/wk**. Honest band including measured values: **+50 to +180 kcal/day**.
- **Nandrolone** — directly measured, **no REE change** at 65 or 200 mg/week over 21 days, despite 0.5-0.9 kg/week of lean tissue accrual (Source: [[Strawford-1999-Nandrolone-REE-Nitrogen-Balance]]).
- **Oxymetholone (Anadrol)** — **no REE data exists.** The HIV-wasting RCTs (89 patients, 50 mg BID or TID, 16 weeks) measured weight (+3.0 to +3.5 kg vs +1.0 kg placebo), body cell mass and appetite. Appetite and food consumption *increased* significantly — that is intake, not expenditure, and it pushes net energy balance the opposite way.
- **Trenbolone** — **no human data of any kind.** The one measurement in any species shows fasting metabolic rate **8-11% lower**, intake-adjusted, in implanted steers (Source: [[Hunter-Vercoe-1987-Trenbolone-Fasting-Metabolic-Rate-Steers]]). The drug was developed and studied explicitly as a maintenance-energy-requirement *reducer* for livestock.
- **Methyltrienolone (M-tren)** — **nothing.** It exists in the scientific literature almost exclusively as R1881, a tritiated radioligand used to measure androgen-receptor binding in vitro. No human pharmacokinetics, no clinical trial, no energy-expenditure data. Any figure quoted for it is invented.
- **Oxandrolone** — the one androgen with abundant REE data, and it **lowers** REE in hypercatabolic patients (Source: [[Oxandrolone-Burns-REE-Herndon-Porro]]).
- **Thyroid hormone** — see the dose-response shape below; the largest lever by an order of magnitude.
- **GH** — **+132 to +172 kcal/day** at medical doses, dose-dependent, with a genuine direct component (Source: [[GH-REE-Pasarica-2007-Burt-2008]]).

### The high-dose bodybuilder question specifically

There is **no study measuring TDEE in bodybuilders using very high doses**. The nearest data (Source: [[Almeida-2023-Bodybuilder-Peak-Week-RMR]]) followed 10 competitors on standardised supraphysiological protocols through peak week and found resting metabolic rate **fell** significantly, along with lean and fat mass — energy restriction and aerobic volume overwhelmed any drug effect.

The practically important inference: at the doses in question, the drugs **do not protect against metabolic adaptation**. A lifter on a gram a week in a deficit has a lower RMR than he had at maintenance.

The second inference, from the arithmetic: dose-response for lean mass is roughly log-linear in testosterone concentration (r = 0.73), so even a 4x dose increase over 600 mg/week buys a modest further lean-mass increment, and therefore a modest further REE increment. **Nothing in the data supports a multi-hundred-kcal resting increase from dose escalation alone.** The large appetite increases reported anecdotally at high doses are an intake phenomenon, not expenditure.

## Key Concepts

- [[Androgens and Energy Expenditure]]: androgens raise TDEE via tissue accrual, not thermogenesis; the sign of the REE response depends on baseline catabolic state
- **Baseline-state dependence**: the same androgen lowers REE in a hypercatabolic patient and raises it in a healthy one, which resolves most of the apparent contradictions in this literature
- **Non-linear thyroid dose-response**: flat within and near the reference range, flat for acute dosing, steep only in sustained thyrotoxicosis
- **The 13 kcal/kg/day coefficient**: skeletal muscle's resting cost, the conversion factor from lean-mass gain to REE gain; whole-FFM figures (20-22) overstate it because androgen-driven FFM includes inert water and glycogen

## Contradictions

**Testosterone raises REE (Welle, Bauman) vs testosterone does nothing (Santosa, Strawford).** Resolved by time course and route. Santosa's 3-week window and Strawford's 21-day window are too short for tissue accumulation to register, and both find nothing — which is exactly what the tissue-mediated model predicts. Welle's 3-month window shows a rise that regression attributes entirely to the accumulated tissue. The studies agree; they are measuring different points on the same curve.

**Trenbolone lowers metabolic rate (Hunter & Vercoe) vs the universal niche claim that it raises body temperature and burns fat.** The calorimetry exists in one species and goes the other way. Subjective heat, sweating and insomnia on trenbolone are real reports but are plausibly sympathetic and neuroendocrine effects — trenbolone is a potent progesterone-receptor and glucocorticoid-receptor ligand — and need not track whole-body energy expenditure. **Tier X creator assertion vs tier C measurement: the measurement wins on evidence, but it is a ruminant, fasted, on a protein-deficient diet.** This one deserves a dedicated claim page.

**GH raises REE (Pasarica, Burt) vs GH does not (Wright 2024 post-COVID; Malpani 2025 short-stature boys).** Dose and substrate availability. The positive trials used adequate doses in subjects with visceral fat to mobilise; the null trials used lower doses or lean paediatric subjects.

## Open Questions

- **No doubly-labelled-water TDEE measurement exists for any anabolic androgen at any dose.** Every number in this synthesis is resting expenditure or derived from lean mass. The free-living total, which is what the question actually asks about, has never been measured.
- **Oxymetholone REE is unmeasured** despite well-designed RCTs that measured almost everything else. Searches for oxymetholone + energy expenditure return zero results in Europe PMC.
- **Trenbolone and M-tren have no human data.** No ethics committee approves administering them, so this gap is permanent.
- **No REE data above 600 mg/week testosterone equivalent**, and none in trained subjects who are simultaneously training hard — the exact population of interest.
- **The activity-expenditure term is entirely unquantified.** Extra training volume and NEAT permitted by faster recovery is plausibly the largest real TDEE effect of androgens and appears in no trial, because trials standardise or forbid training.
- **Does grossly supraphysiological testosterone (>2,400 ng/dL) have a direct thermogenic effect that replacement-range concentrations lack?** Santosa ruled out a direct effect at replacement concentrations only.
- **Compound interactions are unstudied**: GH accelerates peripheral T4-to-T3 conversion, and androgens lower thyroid-binding globulin. A stack is not the sum of its single-agent trials, and no trial combines them.
