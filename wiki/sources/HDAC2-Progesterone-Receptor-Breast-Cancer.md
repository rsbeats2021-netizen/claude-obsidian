# Selective HDAC2 Inhibition Silences Progesterone Receptor Signaling in Breast Cancer (Bicaku et al. 2008)

This paper shows that in ER/PR-positive breast cancer cell lines, selectively depleting the histone deacetylase HDAC2 (via siRNA) — but not HDAC1 or HDAC6 — down-regulates both estrogen receptor alpha and progesterone receptor simultaneously, and potentiates tamoxifen-induced apoptosis. The authors propose HDAC2 as the specific relevant target explaining the known synergy between HDAC inhibitors and antiestrogen (tamoxifen) therapy in breast cancer.
