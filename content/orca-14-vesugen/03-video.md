Vesugen. What it claims, and what was actually measured.

[cover card]

IGF1 mRNA rose 3 to 5.6 fold. In the passages model AED was +4.6x, KED +3x, KE +3.5x; in the stationary model AED +5.6x, KED +2x, KE +5.2x.

[the real measurement]

In that identical experiment, NFκB rose roughly 10 to 35 fold in both models. That was the largest fold-change of any gene measured, several times larger than the IGF1 effect.

[the other gene]

Every human dosing regimen across this family is a pulsed course of 10 to 30 days, and the circulating dose figures are not grounded in pharmacokinetic studies. But zero head-to-head injectable versus intranasal comparisons exist for any of these compounds.

[the pk gap]

The claim that a three amino acid peptide binds DNA with sequence specificity is considered biologically implausible by Western molecular biology standards. Conventional transcription factors need DNA-binding domains of 30 to 60 or more residues to achieve that.

[the core problem]

Being fair about the risk side, because the evidence problem and the safety problem are not the same problem. At 1 to 10 mg a day, these peptides are expected to be hydrolysed into common dietary amino acids.

[the other half]

It targets the correct compartment and there is one real study showing a several-fold IGF1 rise. That same study shows a much larger NFκB rise the authors expect to accelerate ageing, suppressed telomerase, and genes that flip direction by model.

[takeaway card]

Right target. Mixed signal. No growth data.
