# GH Pulses Drive Male-Biased Chromatin Opening in Liver (Rampersaud et al. 2023)

A modern, mechanistically rich paper connecting two threads already separately present in this domain: GH pulsatility (via [[GH-Secretory-Pattern-Peak-Trough-Gevers1996|Gevers et al. 1996]] and related sources) and sexual dimorphism (via [[Chromosomal-Hormonal-Factors-Human-Sexual-Dimorphism|Fukami et al. 2026]]). The core finding: the well-known sex difference in GH secretion pattern (pulsatile in males, more continuous in females) directly drives male-biased chromatin opening at specific liver gene loci — a chromatin-accessibility mechanism explaining how a hormone secretion *pattern*, not just circulating level, can produce durable sex-differentiated gene expression.

> [!key-insight] This paper supplies exactly the kind of modern molecular mechanism this domain's older GH-pulsatility literature (Isgaard 1988, Bick 1992, Gevers 1996) established phenomenologically but couldn't explain at the chromatin level — genuinely bridging classic endocrine physiology and modern epigenetics within this domain's corpus.

## Relevance to This Domain

A significant mechanistic bridge between [[Growth Hormone-IGF-1 Axis]]'s pulsatility literature and [[Chromosomal-Hormonal-Factors-Human-Sexual-Dimorphism|this domain's sexual-dimorphism thread]], supplying a specific chromatin-level mechanism for sex-differentiated GH action.
