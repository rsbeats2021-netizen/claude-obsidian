# Performance follows an inverted U with dose

**Verdict: supported**, and old. Yerkes & Dodson described the arousal-performance relation in 1908; it has been reproduced across stimulant dose-response work since.

## Practical consequence
Past your own peak, raising the dose moves you down the curve you were climbing. Most self-reported "tolerance" is this plus receptor downregulation, not a need for more.
