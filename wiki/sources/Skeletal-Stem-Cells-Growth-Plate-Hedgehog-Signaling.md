# Skeletal Stem Cells in the Growth Plate and Hedgehog Signaling (Trompet et al. 2024)

Authored in part by Andrei S. Chagin — the same investigator behind [[Androgen-Receptor-Modulation-No-Effect-Growth-Plate|Chagin et al. 2009]], already a key source in this domain's AR/growth-plate debate — this paper establishes the first direct functional evidence that a recently-discovered population of resting-zone skeletal stem cells (epSSCs, marked by PTHrP expression) actually drives longitudinal bone growth, not merely correlates with it.

The paper resolves a prior discrepancy in the literature: earlier studies disagreed on whether pharmacological activation of Hedgehog signaling (via the Smoothened agonist SAG) increases or decreases epSSC proliferation. This paper shows the effect is age-dependent — SAG given before the stem-cell niche forms (P10-P16) *decreases* proliferation, while SAG given after niche formation (P30-P36) *increases* it. In contrast, genetic activation of Hh signaling (via conditional Patched1 knockout) increased epSSC proliferation and clonal expansion at both ages, implicating negative systemic side effects of the pharmacological approach at younger ages as the likely explanation for the discrepancy.

The key translational result: implanting an SAG-releasing bead into the secondary ossification center of one rat femur (leaving the other leg as vehicle control) produced measurable, progressively increasing leg lengthening at 1, 2, and 6 months — proof of principle that locally stimulating epSSCs is a viable therapeutic strategy for correcting leg-length discrepancies, a common but under-addressed clinical problem currently treated only with orthotics or surgical growth-plate removal (which reduces final height).

> [!gap] Findings are in mice (genetic tracing) and rats (bead implantation); translation of the local delivery approach to humans, and long-term safety of Hh pathway manipulation in growth plates, remain unestablished.

## Relevance to This Domain

Introduces the epiphyseal skeletal stem cell (epSSC) population and Hedgehog/PTHrP/Ihh signaling axis as a mechanistic layer of [[Epiphyseal Fusion Mechanisms]] and [[Molecular Regulators of Growth Plate Chondrocyte Maturation]] not previously covered in this domain — distinct from the Sox9/Runx2/Dlx5 transcription-factor cluster, this operates one level upstream at the stem-cell-maintenance stage. Also a direct link to [[Androgen-Receptor-Modulation-No-Effect-Growth-Plate|Chagin et al. 2009]] via shared authorship and shared use of similar organ-culture/genetic-tracing methodology in growth-plate research. This paper anchors a small cluster of related epSSC-focused sources: [[Hedgehog-Resting-Zone-Chondrocytes-Osteoblast-Transformation|Orikasa et al. 2023]] (directly cited here, showing Hedgehog activation can redirect epSSC fate toward osteoblast formation rather than just proliferation), [[Human-Growth-Plate-Transcriptional-Atlas-GH-Stem-Cells|Chu et al. 2025]] (extending epSSC biology to human tissue, same lab), and [[Dynasore-Hormetic-Bone-Growth|Marchan-Alvarez et al. 2026]] (showing bone elongation and chondrocyte proliferation can be decoupled via a distinct, non-Hedgehog mechanism).
