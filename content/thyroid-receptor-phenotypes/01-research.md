# Research brief — Same mutation, opposite skeletons

## Core claim
An identical dominant-negative mutation produces skeletal thyrotoxicosis in one
thyroid receptor and skeletal hypothyroidism in the other — and the TRα animals
have normal circulating thyroid hormone.

## Key points (tier-tagged)
- **Tier 2** — TRβPV mice: skeletal thyrotoxicosis — accelerated growth,
  advanced bone age, craniosynostosis. TRβ dominates in the pituitary, so the
  mutation disrupts feedback and floods the system.
- **Tier 2** — TRα1PV mice: the opposite — severe growth retardation, delayed
  ossification, delayed skull suture closure, **despite normal circulating
  thyroid hormone**. TRα dominates in bone, so the tissue itself is deafened.
- **Tier 1** — Generalisation: circulating level is not the whole signal.
  Receptor distribution decides which tissues can hear a hormone, and that is
  not measurable in blood.

## Why this is one of the strongest decks in the library
It's the cleanest demonstration of the signal/receiver distinction, and it
rhymes exactly with the estrogen receptor case (`estrogen-sets-final-height`):
normal hormone, broken receiver, dramatic skeletal phenotype. Slide 8 makes
that link explicitly, which turns two decks into a coherent argument.
