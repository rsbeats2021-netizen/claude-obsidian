# STAT5b as the Molecular Decoder of Pulsatile GH Signaling (Choi & Waxman 2000)

A foundational mechanistic review directly underlying this domain's GH-pulsatility cluster: STAT5b, confirmed via knockout mice, is the key intracellular signal transducer that translates the male-specific pattern of intermittent plasma GH pulses into corresponding liver gene expression and body growth rates — predating and providing the molecular foundation for [[GH-Pulses-Chromatin-Liver-Sex-Dimorphism-Rampersaud2023|Rampersaud et al. 2023]]'s later chromatin-level mechanism work on the same pathway.
