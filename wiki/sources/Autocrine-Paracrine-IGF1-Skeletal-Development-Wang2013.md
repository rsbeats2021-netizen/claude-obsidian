# Autocrine/Paracrine IGF-I Signaling in Skeletal Development: Review (Wang et al. 2013)

A foundational review synthesizing tissue/cell-specific IGF-I knockout mouse data to establish IGF-I's local (autocrine/paracrine) actions in skeletal cells — directly reinforcing this domain's recurring finding (via [[Human-Growth-Plate-Transcriptional-Atlas-GH-Stem-Cells|Chu et al. 2025]], [[IGF1-GH-Differential-Effects-Growth-Plate-Hunziker|Hunziker et al. 1994]], and others) that local, not just systemic liver-derived, IGF-I drives growth-plate biology.

## Relevance to This Domain

A synthesizing foundational review for [[Growth Hormone-IGF-1 Axis]]'s local-action thread, consolidating the tissue-specific-knockout evidence base this domain's individual papers draw on piecemeal.
