# Erdafitinib (BALVERSA) Prescribing Information

Public FDA drug labeling for erdafitinib, an FGFR kinase inhibitor approved for FGFR-altered urothelial carcinoma — the same broad drug class (FGFR inhibition) as [[TYRA-300-FGFR3-Inhibitor-Chondrodysplasia|TYRA-300]], already covered in this domain for its opposite therapeutic goal: TYRA-300 selectively inhibits FGFR3's *gain-of-function* mutations to restore normal growth-plate signaling in chondrodysplasia, while erdafitinib is a broader FGFR1-4 inhibitor used to shrink FGFR-altered tumors by blocking oncogenic signaling.

> [!contradiction] Flagged as unrelated to this domain's growth-plate/bone focus despite sharing a drug target family with [[TYRA-300-FGFR3-Inhibitor-Chondrodysplasia|TYRA-300]]: erdafitinib's approved use is oncology, not growth-plate biology. This document is the publicly-available FDA prescribing information, distinct from and not subject to the confidentiality restriction on the Janssen clinical trial protocol/SAP for this same drug already flagged and left untouched in `.raw/Studies yet to analyze/`.

## Relevance to This Domain

An interesting "same drug target, opposite therapeutic direction" contrast with [[TYRA-300-FGFR3-Inhibitor-Chondrodysplasia|TYRA-300]] — inhibiting the same receptor family can either restore normal growth (blocking gain-of-function FGFR3 mutations) or block pathological growth (shrinking FGFR-altered tumors), depending entirely on which specific mutation and tissue context is targeted.
