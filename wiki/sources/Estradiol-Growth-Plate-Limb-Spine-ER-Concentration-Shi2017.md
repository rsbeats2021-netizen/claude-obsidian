# Estradiol's Dose- and Site-Dependent Effect on Limb vs. Spine Growth Plates (Shi et al. 2017)

Directly compares estradiol's effect on limb versus spine (vertebral) growth-plate chondrocytes, finding both estrogen receptor expression level and estradiol concentration jointly determine the response — a dose-dependent, site-dependent estrogen effect adding anatomical specificity to this domain's estrogen/growth-plate literature (otherwise focused mostly on long-bone limb growth plates).
