# Epiphyseal Plate Injury: A Mechanical/Traumatic Angle on Growth-Plate Vulnerability

Introduces a mechanical-trauma angle to this domain's growth-plate literature — otherwise dominated by hormonal and developmental mechanisms: the growth plate has limited axial stress resistance during active growth phases and can be damaged by excessive mechanical stress, leading to epiphyseal detachment/avulsion with potentially life-long growth consequences if repair fails — a clinically important pediatric-orthopedic complement to this domain's hormone-centric fusion literature.
