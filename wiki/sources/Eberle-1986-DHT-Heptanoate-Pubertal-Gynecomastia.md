# Eberle, Sparrow & Keenan 1986 — DHT-hp for Pubertal Gynecomastia

*J Pediatr* 109(1):144–9. PMID 3088241. DOI 10.1016/s0022-3476(86)80596-0.

Four boys, **IM DHT-hp at 2- to 4-week intervals for 16 weeks**. Breast size fell **67% to 78%** in all four. Baseline gonadotropins, estradiol, testosterone and DHT were normal. Mean plasma DHT rose with the injections and stayed elevated throughout treatment; estradiol, LH, FSH, testosterone and 24-h urinary LH and FSH all fell.

**No regrowth 6 to 15 months after treatment**, although hormone concentrations had returned to near-pretreatment values by 2 months after the last injection.

The per-patient mg doses (200 mg, 400 mg, q2wk vs q3wk), the 190 mg/mL sesame-oil formulation, and a full hormone table are printed in [[Keenan-1990-DHT-Heptanoate-Patent-US4956357]], whose Example 3 describes a cohort matching this one on size, age range, duration and measurements. That it is **the same four patients is an inference** — neither document states it.
