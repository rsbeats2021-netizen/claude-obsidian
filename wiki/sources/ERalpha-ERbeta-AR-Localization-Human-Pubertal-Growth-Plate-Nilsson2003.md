# ERα, ERβ, and AR Localization in the Human Pubertal Growth Plate (Nilsson et al.)

A foundational human-tissue receptor-mapping study: growth-plate biopsies from 16 boys and 8 girls spanning all Tanner pubertal stages were immunostained for ERα, ERβ, and AR, with receptor-positive cell fractions quantified separately across the resting, proliferative, and hypertrophic zones. Directly resolves the "controversial" localization question the abstract itself frames — whether sex-steroid receptors are reliably present, and where, in the actual human pubertal growth plate (as opposed to inferred from animal models).
