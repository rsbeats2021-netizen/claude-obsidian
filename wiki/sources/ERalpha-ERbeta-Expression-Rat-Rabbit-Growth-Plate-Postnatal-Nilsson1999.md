# ERα/ERβ Expression Timing Across Growth-Plate Zones and Postnatal Age (Nilsson et al.)

Maps ERα and ERβ expression across growth-plate zones (resting, proliferative, hypertrophic) at multiple postnatal ages in both rat and rabbit: both receptors are present in resting/proliferative zones throughout life, but hypertrophic-zone expression stays minimal until it rises sharply just before epiphyseal fusion — suggesting the timing of ER expression in the hypertrophic zone specifically, not just its presence, is mechanistically tied to the fusion process itself.

## Relevance to This Domain

A developmental-timing data point for [[Epiphyseal Fusion Mechanisms]] and [[Estrogen Receptor (ER)]] — hypertrophic-zone ER expression rising specifically just before fusion supports a causal (not incidental) role for ER signaling in triggering closure.
