# Elevated cAMP Blocks MMP- But Not Aggrecanase-Mediated Cartilage Degradation (Karsdal et al. 2007)

Tests whether calcitonin's proposed chondroprotective effect (mediated via the second messenger cAMP) actually protects cartilage from degradation: raising cAMP in bovine articular cartilage explants blocked oncostatin-M/TNF-induced, matrix-metalloproteinase (MMP)-mediated cartilage degradation, but had no protective effect against aggrecanase-mediated degradation — direct evidence that cartilage breakdown in osteoarthritis proceeds via at least two mechanistically independent enzymatic pathways, with cAMP elevation protecting against only one.

## Relevance to This Domain

Establishes that cAMP's chondroprotective effect is pathway-specific (MMP, not aggrecanase), directly relevant when interpreting [[PGE2-Apoptosis-Bovine-Articular-Chondrocytes-cAMP-Miwa2000|Miwa et al. 2000]]'s apparently opposite (cAMP-mediated apoptosis) finding in the same broad signaling system.
