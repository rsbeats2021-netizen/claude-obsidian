How steroids. And the switch is the androgen receptor itself.

[cover card]

Cortical neurons grown from rat brain, then dosed with different androgens for 24 hours or 3 days. They measured mitochondrial activity, membrane damage via LDH release, and caspase 3/7, which is the enzyme that executes apoptosis.

[that showed it]

All three killed neurons by apoptosis. Caspase 3/7 activity went up, confirming the death was programmed rather than accidental.

[and trenbolone]

Nandrolone alone showed reduced mitochondrial activity by 24 hours. Mitochondrial failure comes before the apoptosis signal, so this is an early upstream hit rather than a late consequence.

[hits mitochondria first]

Toxicity in these studies was dose dependent and worsened with repeated exposure versus single dose. Compound choice matters too, since trenbolone hit neurite outgrowth and gene expression while others did not.

[takeaway card]

Dose, duration and compound are the only levers here. There is no fourth one.
