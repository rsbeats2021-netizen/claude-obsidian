# Research brief — GH pattern beats GH dose

## Core claim
Pulsatile GH infusion raised IGF-1 mRNA in skeletal tissue more effectively
than continuous infusion. Temporal pattern, not just total dose, determines the
skeletal response.

## Key points (tier-tagged)
- **Tier 1** — Hypophysectomised rats (no endogenous GH); pulsatile vs
  continuous infusion; pulsatile more effectively increased IGF-I mRNA
  specifically in skeletal tissues (Isgaard 1988).
- **Tier 1** — Endogenous GH secretion is strongly pulsatile, largest bursts in
  deep sleep, with low troughs between.
- **Tier 2** — Consequence: protocols delivering identical total hormone can
  produce different skeletal responses.

## Why this deck matters for this audience specifically
Arguments in this space are almost entirely about quantity — units per day,
whether an administered amount "matches" natural production. That comparison
sets a total against a rhythm, which are different kinds of quantity. Slide 7
makes exactly that point.

## Honest limits — slide 8
Hypophysectomised rats; endpoint is IGF-1 mRNA, not final height. Establishes
that pattern matters mechanistically, not how much it matters in humans.

## Deliberately not included
No dosing, no frequency recommendations. The deck argues that a common
comparison is malformed — it does not propose a better protocol.
