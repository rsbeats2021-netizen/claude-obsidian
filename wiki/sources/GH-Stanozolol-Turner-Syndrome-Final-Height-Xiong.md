# GH + Stanozolol: Final Height Outcomes in Turner Syndrome (Xiong et al.)

Directly extends [[GH-Stanozolol-Turner-Syndrome-Fang1999|Fang et al. 1999]] (already in this domain, which showed growth-velocity improvement) with the more clinically meaningful final-adult-height outcome — GH + low-dose stanozolol combined therapy improved both growth velocity and final height in Turner syndrome girls, providing the outcome-level confirmation the earlier paper's velocity-only data couldn't establish.

## Relevance to This Domain

Completes the growth-velocity-to-final-height evidence chain for GH+stanozolol in Turner syndrome, directly building on [[GH-Stanozolol-Turner-Syndrome-Fang1999|Fang et al. 1999]] already in this domain.
