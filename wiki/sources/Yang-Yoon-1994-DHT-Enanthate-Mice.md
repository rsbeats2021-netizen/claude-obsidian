# Yang & Yoon 1994 — DHT Enanthate in Mice

*J Korean Androl Soc* 12(2):83–91. Not PubMed-indexed; KoreaMed RID 1851748. Vehicle and animal numbers not stated in the abstract.

**Single 2 mg intraperitoneal dose** (= 1.44 mg DHT-equivalent) of DHT enanthate or testosterone enanthate in **male ICR mice**. Serum and testes sampled from 1 hour to 24 days.

- **TE:** testis weight and volume rose quickly within 1 day, fell back to control by **day 3** — tracking serum testosterone, not serum DHT.
- **DHTE:** no sharp rise; a **gradual increase to a constant level**. Serum testosterone stayed flat; testis size tracked **DHT**.
- Histology: enlarged testes showed more interstitial space between seminiferous tubules.

Authors' conclusion: TE's effects are transient, DHTE's longer-lasting; DHTE may outlast TE for androgen replacement in male hypogonadism. Consistent with the 7-day vs 4-day terminal half-lives in [[Weinbauer-1990-TE-vs-DHT-Enanthate-Primates]].
