# DHT Heptanoate Patent (Keenan & Sparrow, US 4,956,357)

Priority 1985-03-30 (GB), filed 1986-03-28, granted 1990. The patent prints the **formulation**, which the journal abstracts do not.

> [!note] Why confidence is medium
> The clinical examples describe four pubertal boys aged 14–16 with gynecomastia lasting over 16 months, matching [[Eberle-1986-DHT-Heptanoate-Pubertal-Gynecomastia]] on cohort, breast measurements and hormone values, and share an author. That they are the **same patients is my inference** — neither document says so. A patent is also an applicant's own filing, not peer-reviewed data, and it marks its 10-adolescent series "results not shown.

## The concentration figure

> "To prepare the DHT hep for administration, it was dissolved in sesame oil at 37 °C to a concentration of **190 mg/ml**. (This was the **limit of solubility** of DHT-hep in sesame oil at 37 °C. The warm sesame oil mixture was filtered in a laminar flow hood through a 0.20 micron filter into sterile injection vials. The vials were sealed, tested for sterility and pyrogens, and stored at room temperature."

190 mg/mL ester = **137.1 mg/mL DHT-equivalent** (72.14% by mass).

Alternative vehicles named but not quantified: cottonseed oil, corn oil, peanut oil, other refined fixed oils, polyethylene glycol, propylene glycol.

## Synthesis and purity

20.0 g (0.069 mol) DHT in 150 mL dichloromethane + 39 mL (36.4 g) heptanoyl chloride; purified by preparative HPLC from hexane, recrystallised from acetone-water. **Melting point 92.2–94.4 °C**, confirmed by IR, NMR and GC-MS. The patent concedes <1% esterified androstanediols may co-purify.

## Claimed dosing

- Claims 5 and 10: "from about **200 mg to 400 mg** of dihydrotestosterone heptanoate is administered **every three weeks**."
- Claims 1, 6, 11, 13: "a pharmacologically effective amount" (gynecomastia, hypogonadotrophism, androgen-response testing).

## Example 3 — gynecomastia, 4 pubertal boys 14–16 y

Breast duration >16 months, diameters 4.0–7.3 cm, areas 13.5–41.4 cm² (mean 24.3 ± 4.1).

| Patient | Dose | Interval |
|---|---|---|
| M.S. | 200 mg | q3wk |
| S.S. | 400 mg | q3wk |
| J.D. | 200 mg | q2wk |
| J.A. | 400 mg | q3wk |

First dose at baseline, second at 4 weeks, then q3wk (3 pts) or q2wk (1 pt); 16 weeks total. S.S. and J.A. were escalated 200 → 400 mg because **estradiol was not adequately suppressed at 200 mg**.

Breast area 24.3 ± 4.1 → **7.0 ± 1.5 cm²** (p<0.002), −66.6 to −77.5%. No regrowth at 2, 6 months; one patient followed 9 months.

| Analyte | Pre | 2 wk | 4 wk | 16 wk | 2 mo post |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| DHT (ng/dL) | 42.1 ± 5.1 | 283.6 ± 51.3 | 112.8 ± 21.2 | 278.2 ± 36.9 | 61.3 ± 14.7 |
| Testosterone (ng/dL) | 283.8 ± 87.5 | 29.5 ± 3.3 | — | 33.8 ± 3.2 | 214.9 ± 34.8 |
| FSH (ng/mL) | 190.5 ± 50.7 | 58.8 ± 18.5 | — | 40.3 ± 7.3 | 121.5 ± 17.4 |
| Estradiol (pg/mL) | 23.8 ± 3.2 | 10.8 ± 0.8 | — | 12.8 ± 1.6 | 18.8 ± 2.5 |

Individual DHT at 16 wk: 171, 292, 313, 336 ng/dL. Urinary LH 13.0 ± 1.3 → 7.4 ± 3 IU/day and urinary FSH 8.4 ± 0.8 → 2.9 ± 0.1 IU/day after one week. Plasma LH was already low in 3 of 4, so further suppression was not significant.

A further **10 adolescents** (data not shown) reached plasma DHT **300–800 ng/dL**, with reduced breast size and reduced LH, FSH, T and E2. Doses not given.

## Example 4 — DHT-hp as an androgen

- **26-year-old man, hypogonadotrophic hypogonadism**, pretreatment T and DHT both <15 ng/dL: **200 mg/month IM × 6 months** → penile length 4 → 8 cm, diameter 1.5 → 2.5 cm; pubic hair increased; erections and ejaculations reported; "significant increase in muscle mass."
- **Boy in early puberty with gynecomastia**, 4 months (dose not stated): penile length increased significantly **despite suppressed plasma testosterone and LH**; pubic and axillary hair increased; 3.5 kg weight gain.

## Example 5 — DHT-hp as a hormonal probe

Claims 11–15 cover using DHT-hp to separate testicular from adrenal sources of testosterone and estradiol: in 2 of the 4 boys, estradiol persisted while LH and testosterone were suppressed, implicating adrenal estradiol precursors.

## Caveats the patent itself states

In roughly **1/5 of subjects absorption was poor with no benefit**, and in about a third breast regression was incomplete. These are the applicant's own figures, not independent trial data.
