# Human Metabolism of Trestolone (MENT) (Piper et al. 2025)

An anti-doping analytical chemistry paper characterizing how trestolone (7α-methyl-19-nortestosterone, "MENT") — a potent synthetic androgen notable for resisting conversion to a DHT-like 5α-reduced metabolite while still being aromatizable to estrogen — is metabolized and excreted in humans, aimed at improving doping-detection methods.

> [!gap] An analytical/forensic chemistry paper, not a physiological or clinical efficacy study; no data here on trestolone's actual growth-plate, bone, or tissue effects, only its metabolic fate and detectability.

## Relevance to This Domain

Adds a pharmacologically distinct tool to [[Testosterone]] and [[Dihydrotestosterone (DHT)]]'s catalog of androgens used to dissect aromatization-dependent vs. 5α-reduction-dependent effects: trestolone is aromatizable but 5α-reduction-resistant, the mirror image of DHT (5α-reduced but non-aromatizable) — a useful complementary experimental tool this domain's other sources haven't yet used directly on growth-plate tissue.
