# DHT Esters — Dosing and Concentrations

Two naming notes first, because they decide what you are reading.

**Heptanoate = enanthate.** Both name the same seven-carbon acyl group (C7, heptanoic/enanthic acid). The literature splits by lab, not by molecule: the Baylor/Texas group (Keenan, Sparrow, Eberle) wrote **DHT heptanoate / DHT-hp / DHTH**, the Münster group (Weinbauer, Nieschlag) wrote **dihydrotestosterone-enanthate / DHTE**. CAS 33776-88-4, C26H42O3, MW 402.62. Brand: *Anaboleen Depot*.

**Propionate = propanoate.** Same three-carbon ester. CAS 855-22-1, C22H34O3, MW 346.51. Brand: *Pesomax* (Crinos SpA, Italy; authorisation now revoked).

> [!warning] Do not conflate with drostanolone
> The 1960s breast-cancer literature on "**2α-methyl**-dihydrotestosterone propionate" is **drostanolone propionate** (Masteron), a different molecule. Five oncology papers (PMIDs 13706491, 13920749, 14202595, 4316818, 5632753) are drostanolone, not DHT propionate, and are excluded from the tables below.

## The mass-fraction arithmetic (this is the key to every number)

DHT (androstanolone) is C19H30O2, MW 290.45. The ester is a prodrug; only the DHT moiety is active.

| Ester | MW | DHT by mass | 100 mg ester delivers |
|---|---|---|---|
| DHT propionate (C3) | 346.51 | **83.82%** | 83.8 mg DHT |
| DHT heptanoate / enanthate (C7) | 402.62 | **72.14%** | 72.1 mg DHT |

This is independently confirmed by [[Weinbauer-1990-TE-vs-DHT-Enanthate-Primates]], who dosed **32.7 mg DHT-enanthate = 23.6 mg pure steroid** — 72.2%, matching the calculated 72.14% exactly. Use this factor on every dose below.

> [!info] What this page is
> A literature report. Every human regimen below comes from an investigational programme — a 1980s FDA IND in adolescents, or a doping-control study — not from any approved label. DHT heptanoate has never been marketed anywhere. Nothing here is dosing guidance.

## Concentration (mg/mL) — the hard numbers

Four mg/mL figures exist in the accessible literature across the two esters. All are given verbatim or with their arithmetic shown.

| Preparation | Concentration | Vehicle | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| DHT heptanoate, clinical | **190 mg/mL** (= 137.1 mg/mL DHT-equivalent) — a solubility ceiling, not a fill strength | sesame oil, dissolved at 37 °C, 0.20 µm filtered into sterile vials | [[Keenan-1990-DHT-Heptanoate-Patent-US4956357]] |
| DHT enanthate, primate (WHO / NICHD prep) | **32.7 mg/mL** (= 23.6 mg/mL DHT-equivalent) | 1 mL sesame oil, IM | [[Weinbauer-1990-TE-vs-DHT-Enanthate-Primates]] |
| DHT enanthate, rodent | **5.0 mg/mL** (= 3.6 mg/mL DHT-equivalent) | 90% olive oil / 10% DMSO, s.c.; Steraloids | [[Hanson-2020-DHT-Enanthate-Castrate-Rat-Hypertension]] |
| **DHT propionate, commercial** — *Pesomax*, Crinos SpA (Italy) | **15 mg per 2 mL ampoule ≈ 7.5 mg/mL** (= 6.3 mg/mL DHT-equivalent) | oil solution, IM, 10 ampoules per pack | AIFA/Italian registry listing, AIC **015012014**; status **revocato** (withdrawn) |

Note the 40-fold spread between the clinical heptanoate preparation and the two research preparations of the same ester. The 190 mg/mL figure is not an arbitrary fill strength — the patent states it as a **ceiling**:

> "it was dissolved in sesame oil at 37 °C to a concentration of 190 mg/ml. (This was the limit of solubility of DHT-hep in sesame oil at 37 °C."

Alternative vehicles the patent names but does not quantify: cottonseed, corn, peanut oil, other refined fixed oils, polyethylene glycol, propylene glycol.

Implied injection volumes at 190 mg/mL (arithmetic, not stated in the source): 200 mg → **1.05 mL**; 250 mg → **1.32 mL**; 400 mg → **2.11 mL**.

Weinbauer's 32.7 mg/mL sits far below the heptanoate's solubility ceiling in the same vehicle, so research concentrations were set by dose convenience rather than by what would dissolve.

**The Pesomax figure comes from the registry listing, not a label.** The Italian packaging designation is "PESOMAX IM 10F 2ML 15MG" — ten 2 mL ampoules at 15 mg, AIC 015012014, Crinos SpA, prescription class RR (repeatable 10× in 6 months), listed retail ~€1.76, and marketing authorisation **revoked**. Italian packaging nomenclature states strength per unit, so 15 mg is the per-ampoule content and 7.5 mg/mL follows; *the listing does not spell out "per ampoule", so treat the per-mL figure as my arithmetic on that convention.* No SmPC or foglio illustrativo text was recoverable. Note this also corrects the common secondary-source claim that DHT propionate is "marketed in Italy" — it is withdrawn.

No DHT propionate *study* states a concentration; every one reports mg or mg/kg only.

## DHT heptanoate / enanthate — every study found

PubMed phrase searches across `"dihydrotestosterone heptanoate"`, `"DHT heptanoate"`, `"DHT-hp"`, `"dihydrotestosterone enanthate"`, `"dihydrotestosterone-enanthate"`, `"DHT enanthate"`, `"DHTE"`, `"androstanolone enanthate"`, `"stanolone enanthate"` return **8 studies plus one patent family**; a ninth, [[Yang-Yoon-1994-DHT-Enanthate-Mice]], is not PubMed-indexed and was found via a secondary index. This is a genuinely tiny literature. Every human dose that any accessible source states has now been recovered; three regimens remain unstated at source (marked below).

|---|---|---|
| pre-treatment | 42.1 ± 5.1 | 1.45 |

Co-suppression on the same regimen: testosterone 283.8 → 29.5 ng/dL (2 wk) → 33.8 (16 wk); FSH 190.5 → 58.8 → 40.3 ng/mL; estradiol 23.8 → 10.8 → 12.8 pg/mL; urinary LH 13.0 → 7.4 IU/day, urinary FSH 8.4 → 2.9 IU/day after one week.

So a 200 mg DHT-hp injection is a **5–8× plasma DHT multiplier at two weeks** and near-castrate testosterone. It is a suppression drug as much as a replacement drug.

The patent also records its own failure rate, in the applicant's words: absorption was poor with no benefit in about **1/5 of subjects**, and breast regression was incomplete in about **a third**.

**The 284 vs 848 ng/dL gap is unexplained by the sources.** Both are two-week post-injection samples after a 200 mg dose, so timing does not account for it. Two differences are visible in the papers and may: the patent's 284 follows a **single** injection while Keenan's 848 follows the **third of three monthly** injections, so accumulation is plausible; and the assays differ, Keenan's using Celite chromatography before RIA ("99.9% effective in separating T and DHT") while the patent does not describe its method. *Both of those are my inference, not stated by either source.* The cohorts also differ in age, Tanner stage and indication.

What the table does support, without inference: every regimen from 50 mg q2wk upward puts plasma DHT **at or above the top of the adult male reference range**, and the 50 mg dose — the lowest ever given — already produced a 30-fold rise. No source establishes a dose–level curve, and no study assayed the highest regimen at all.

### Animal

| Study | Species | Dose (ester) | DHT-equiv | Concentration | Vehicle / route | Interval |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[Weinbauer-1990-TE-vs-DHT-Enanthate-Primates]] (PMID 2333732) | orchidectomised cynomolgus; GnRH-agonist-suppressed rhesus | **32.7 mg single** | **23.6 mg** (stated) | not stated | IM | single, then wk 10/14/17/20 in expt 2 |
| [[Yang-Yoon-1994-DHT-Enanthate-Mice]] | male ICR mice | **2 mg single** | 1.44 mg | not stated | intraperitoneal | single |
| [[Hanson-2020-DHT-Enanthate-Castrate-Rat-Hypertension]] (PMID 32843085) | castrated Sprague-Dawley rats, ~519–670 g | **1.00 mg/kg** (vs TE 1.75 mg/kg) | 0.72 mg/kg | **5.0 mg/mL** | 90% olive oil / 10% DMSO, s.c. | **2×/week × 5 wk** |

Weinbauer's PK, the only proper comparison against testosterone enanthate at equimolar steroid:

- DHT rose to **800% of baseline** within 24 h (TE → 400% for testosterone), held 3–5 days, back to baseline at 4–5 weeks.
- AUC did not differ: **2290 ± 340** (DHTE) vs **2920 ± 485** (TE) — equivalent steroid release from the two esters.
- Terminal t½: **7 days DHTE vs 4 days TE** (castrate model); **7 vs 5 days** in the GnRH-suppressed model.
- Both esters fully restored ejaculatory response, ejaculate size, masturbatory behaviour and seminal carnitine.

Yang & Yoon's mouse result is the clean shape contrast: TE spikes testis weight within 1 day then collapses by day 3; DHTE climbs gradually to a plateau tracking DHT. "Longer-lasting than TE" is the authors' own conclusion.

## DHT propionate — every study found

The asymmetry is stark: DHT propionate has a **large animal literature and almost no human literature**. Full extraction of 148 PubMed abstracts containing the phrase yields ~50 distinct dosing regimens, catalogued in [[DHT Propionate Animal Dose Ledger]]. Highlights and the human data:

| Study | Subjects | Dose | DHT-equiv | Route | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[Forti-1983-DHT-Propionate-Klinefelter]] (PMID 6417219) | 6 men, Klinefelter syndrome | **100 mg daily × 4 days** | 83.8 mg/day | IM | late, transient LH fall; very late FSH fall; **DHT less effective than T at suppressing gonadotropins**; PRL did *not* rise (unlike TP, 100 mg × 4 d) |

Rodent — the bulk of the literature, typically s.c. in oil, per-rat or per-100 g:

- **Prostate / 5α-reductase bioassay standard:** DHTP **1 mg/kg s.c. × 4 days** in juvenile castrate male rats, the DHT-arm control that a 5α-reductase inhibitor must *fail* to block (PMID 8645628). Two further inhibitor papers use a DHTP arm for the same purpose but state no DHTP dose (PMIDs 9180934, 7734405).
- **Androgen replacement after castration:** 0.25 mg/rat/day in corn oil (PMID 14657609); 40 µg/rat/day (PMID 10529621); 0.5 mg every other day (PMID 2017462); 2.5 mg/day (PMIDs 2418428, 8142561); 2 mg/day (PMID 9212065).
- **Spermatogenesis in hypophysectomised rats:** 0.5 mg/day × 35 days (PMID 908450).
- **Tfm (AR-defective) rats:** 50 µg/100 g b.w. daily × 12 d had **no** effect on LH/FSH; 2 mg/100 g b.w. did suppress (PMID 991822) — a 40-fold threshold.
- **Lordosis inhibition in ovariectomised females:** 0.2 / 0.4 / 0.8 mg/day (PMID 6662517); 200 µg × 3 d (PMID 2606464); 40 or 80 µg/100 g daily (PMID 6168340).
- **Male calling and copulatory behaviour:** a subthreshold 50 µg/day becomes active when combined with 1 µg/day estradiol benzoate (PMID 6871034); 200 µg activated ultrasound production where 50 µg did not (PMID 6494276).
- **Neonatal masculinisation:** 60 or 180 µg/100 g on days 2,4,6,8,10 (PMID 1175516); 50 µg/day × 10 d from birth in mice (PMID 923520); 0.1 mg on days 1,3,5 (PMID 7391715).
- **Estrogen-induced prostatic inflammation model:** DHTP **2.5 mg/kg/day** + E2 0.25 mg/kg/day in **sesame oil** × 30 days (PMID 16516042) — one of the few that names the vehicle.
- **Ovariectomised-plus-estrogen models:** 7.5 mg/kg daily, against 3α-androstanediol at 3.75 mg/kg (PMIDs 21130793, 9878277). Neither paper frames itself as a PCOS model.
- **Sheep:** 10 mg/day — totally ineffective at masculinising behaviour where TP 10 mg/day worked (PMID 99256).
- **Boars:** prepubertally castrated, **1 mg/kg twice weekly from 16 to 28 weeks of age** (PMID 6747955).
- **Prenatal sheep:** twice-weekly injections, gestational days 30–90 (PMID 22539681). A prenatal guinea-pig study (PMID 1873381) states a dose only for its testosterone propionate arm.

## Free (unesterified) DHT for comparison

Two non-ester reference points, because they bracket what the esters were competing against:

- **Crystalline DHT aqueous suspension, 40 mg IM**, in 2 × 6 hypogonadal men, split by particle size (<50 µm vs 100–150 µm). Coarse particles kept serum DHT above baseline **>50 days** vs 17 days for fine; AUC 55.1 vs 14.5 ng·day/mL; absorption t½ 21 vs 6 days (Díaz-Sánchez 1989, PMID 2920848). Particle size, not esterification, bought the duration.
- **Andractim 2.5% transdermal gel**, 5–10 g/day ≈ **125–250 mg DHT/day**, the only DHT product still marketed. Historical oral: 10 mg + 300 mg L-lysine (*Lysinex*); sublingual 25 mg 2–3×/day (*Anabolex*, *Anaprotin*, *Anaboleen*); microcrystalline aqueous suspension 300 mg 3×/week for breast cancer in women.

Note the arithmetic: 200 mg DHT-hp q3wk delivers ~144 mg DHT over 21 days ≈ **7 mg DHT/day** equivalent, against 125–250 mg/day *applied* transdermally — a ~20–35× difference in nominal mg. That compares applied dose to administered dose, not delivered DHT; no source measured the two routes head to head, and attributing the gap to skin absorption is my inference.

## What is simply missing

Stating the gaps precisely, because the absence is itself the finding:

1. **No DHT ester concentration is published beyond the four above**, and only one of the four (*Pesomax*) is a marketed product — recovered from a registry listing rather than a label.
2. **No human PK study of DHT propionate.** Ever. The 1983 Klinefelter study measured plasma DHT but reported no PK parameters.
3. **Three regimens are unstated at source**: the per-dose mg in [[Keenan-1987-DHT-Heptanoate-Pharmacokinetics]], the dose for the patent's second androgen-example patient, and the doses for its 10 further adolescents ("results not shown"). Everything else has been recovered from full text.
4. **No DHT ester contraception trial was ever published — but the ester was in the pipeline.** The 1986 patent lists male contraception as a possible use, and [[Weinbauer-1990-TE-vs-DHT-Enanthate-Primates]] states that its DHT-enanthate came from the **WHO** and the **NICHD Contraceptive Development Branch**, which places the compound inside a formal contraceptive-development programme. Nothing records a human trial or what became of it. The suppression data (testosterone to 29.5 ng/dL) are consistent with it being viable; that it *would have worked* is my inference.
5. **Coutts 1997's preparation remains undescribed.** The 250 mg dose has no stated vehicle or concentration, and the 1.32 mL figure above is inferred from the patent's 190 mg/mL only. The full text is genuinely unreachable: Unpaywall reports it as `oa_status: green`, but the location it offers (`clinchem.org/content/43/11/2091.abstract`) is the abstract page, not a deposited full text — a false positive. Not on any mirror checked.
6. ~~Weinbauer 1990's vehicle~~ — **closed**: 32.7 mg in 1 mL sesame oil, WHO/NICHD preparation.
7. ~~*Pesomax* ampoule strength~~ — **closed**: 15 mg per 2 mL ampoule, AIC 015012014, now withdrawn.

## How to think about this body of evidence — interpretation

Everything in this section is my reading, not a claim any source makes.

The DHT ester literature is small, and the shape of what exists is suggestive. A non-aromatisable androgen at replacement-equivalent doses reliably suppresses estradiol: [[Keenan-1993-DHT-Heptanoate-Pubertal-Growth]] drove height velocity to 8.9 cm/yr with integrated GH falling by half and **plasma estradiol below assay sensitivity in every treated subject**, and [[Saad-2001-DHT-Heptanoate-Insulin-Sensitivity]] reproduced the undetectable-estradiol result at 50 mg q2wk. The authors of both papers draw the mechanistic conclusion themselves — that the pubertal GH rise is estrogen-dependent — and that makes DHT-hp a good **hormonal probe**, which is exactly what the patent claims it as (claims 11–15).

Whether that is *why* the programme stopped, no source says. [[Saad-2001-DHT-Heptanoate-Insulin-Sensitivity]] records FDA IND 29,139 and the literature then goes quiet; nothing documents what became of it.

The propionate's distribution is the mirror image — two human studies against ~150 animal papers — and in the three places where DHTP failed while testosterone propionate worked (sheep behaviour PMID 99256, primate sexual behaviour PMID 3770656, human gonadotropin suppression in [[Forti-1983-DHT-Propionate-Klinefelter]]), the authors of those papers attribute the difference to aromatisation. Read together, that is consistent with the ester persisting as a reagent for separating androgenic from estrogenic mechanisms rather than as a therapeutic — but no source states that as its history.
