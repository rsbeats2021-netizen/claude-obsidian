# Epigenetic and Metabolic Regulation of Skeletal Stem Cells via Ptip

Introduces a novel regulatory layer to this domain's skeletal stem cell literature: rather than the signaling-pathway focus (Hedgehog, PTHrP, Wnt) already central to [[Epiphyseal Skeletal Stem Cells and Hedgehog Signaling]], this paper maps the epigenetic landscape across SSC developmental stages, identifying Ptip as a key regulator of SSC activity, with glycolytic metabolism also implicated as a contributing factor — suggesting SSC fate/activity is governed not just by extracellular signaling but by intrinsic epigenetic and metabolic state.

## Relevance to This Domain

A novel epigenetic/metabolic regulatory dimension for [[Epiphyseal Skeletal Stem Cells and Hedgehog Signaling]], complementing this domain's signaling-pathway-focused SSC literature with an intrinsic-cell-state angle.
