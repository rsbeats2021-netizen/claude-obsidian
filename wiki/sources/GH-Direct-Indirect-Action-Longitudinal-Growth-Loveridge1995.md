# GH Neutralization Reveals Direct and Indirect Growth Effects (Loveridge et al. 1995)

Using a polyclonal anti-rat-GH antiserum to neutralize endogenous GH in vivo, this study directly measures the resulting changes in growth-plate chondrocyte DNA synthesis (BrdU incorporation), serum IGF-I (via two independent RIAs), and bone formation/resorption enzyme activities — a methodologically rigorous approach to dissecting whether GH's growth-promoting effect is direct (acting on the growth plate itself) or indirect (mediated entirely through liver IGF-I), directly engaging the debate [[Somatotropic-Axis-GH-IGF-Related-Independent-Roles-Butler2001|Butler & Le Roith 2001]] later formalized theoretically.

## Relevance to This Domain

A methodologically direct (GH-neutralization) approach to the direct-vs-indirect GH action question central to [[Growth Hormone-IGF-1 Axis]], complementing [[Somatotropic-Axis-GH-IGF-Related-Independent-Roles-Butler2001|Butler & Le Roith 2001]]'s theoretical synthesis with an experimental manipulation.
