# MSC-Secreted IGF-2 Rejuvenates Senescent Chondrocytes via Glutathione Status (Cho et al. 2025)

A mechanistically rich paper connecting three threads that had been separate in this domain: MSC-based cartilage therapy, IGF signaling, and chondrocyte cellular senescence. MSCs with high intracellular glutathione secrete more IGF-2, which acts paracrinely on nearby senescent osteoarthritic chondrocytes to reverse their senescent phenotype — directly complementing [[ERalpha-DNA-Damage-Chondrocyte-Senescence-Osteoarthritis|Zhang et al. 2023]]'s ERα-mediated senescence-suppression mechanism and [[SASP-Chondrocyte-Osteoarthritis-Peptide-Chondroprotection-Linkova|Linkova et al.]]'s SASP review, but via a completely independent pathway (MSC-paracrine IGF-2 signaling, gated by antioxidant status).

> [!key-insight] This domain now has at least three independent mechanisms for reversing/preventing chondrocyte senescence in osteoarthritis: ERα-mediated DNA-damage suppression (Zhang et al.), SIRT1-mediated Sox9 protection (Linkova et al.), and MSC-paracrine IGF-2/glutathione signaling (this paper) — suggesting chondrocyte senescence is a convergence point for multiple independent protective pathways, any of which might be therapeutically exploitable.
