# BPC-157 and Musculoskeletal Healing (McGuire et al. 2025)

A scoping review of BPC-157 (Body Protective Compound-157), a synthetic pentadecapeptide originally isolated from human gastric juice, covering its molecular mechanisms (notably VEGFR2 and nitric oxide signaling), preclinical evidence for tendon/ligament/muscle/bone healing, and regulatory controversies given its increasing off-label popularity.

> [!contradiction] Flagged as topically unrelated to this domain: BPC-157 is a peptide acting through growth-factor and nitric-oxide signaling pathways, not a steroid hormone or a modulator of the androgen/estrogen/aromatase pathways this domain otherwise covers. Despite the shared surface-level connection to "musculoskeletal healing," the pharmacological mechanism is entirely distinct.
