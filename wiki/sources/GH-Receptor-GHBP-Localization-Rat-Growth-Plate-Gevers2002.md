# GH Receptor and GHBP Localization Across Growth-Plate Zones (Gevers et al. 2002)

Directly tests the "dual effector theory" of GH action (GH acts on resting-zone stem cells to expand the proliferative pool, IGF-I then drives their downstream proliferation) by precisely mapping GH receptor and GH-binding protein expression across growth-plate zones — from the same Evelien Gevers research lineage already central to this domain's GH single-cell mechanism literature ([[GH-Single-Cell-Enzyme-Activity-Proliferation-Gevers1996|1996]], [[GH-Rapid-STAT5-Phosphorylation-Resting-Chondrocytes-Gevers2009|2009]], [[GH-Secretory-Pattern-Peak-Trough-Gevers1996|1996 secretory pattern]]).

## Relevance to This Domain

Directly tests the dual-effector theory for [[Growth Hormone-IGF-1 Axis]] and [[Epiphyseal Skeletal Stem Cells and Hedgehog Signaling]] — which cell populations actually express the GH receptor, a foundational question underlying this domain's whole GH-acts-on-stem-cells thread.
