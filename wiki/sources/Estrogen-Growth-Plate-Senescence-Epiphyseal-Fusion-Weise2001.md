# Effects of Estrogen on Growth Plate Senescence and Epiphyseal Fusion (Weise et al. 2001)

A foundational paper from the same NICHD (Jeffrey Baron) laboratory lineage behind [[Depletion-Resting-Zone-Chondrocytes-Growth-Plate-Senescence|Schrier et al. 2006]] and [[Estrogen-Irreversible-Resting-Zone-Depletion-Nilsson2014|Nilsson et al. 2014]] already central to this domain's [[Epiphyseal Skeletal Stem Cells and Hedgehog Signaling]] cluster — this earlier (2001) paper directly establishes the cellular mechanism connecting estrogen exposure to growth-plate senescence and epiphyseal fusion, likely the primary foundational source that the later, more specific papers in this same research program built upon and cited.

> [!key-insight] Finding this paper closes an important citation-lineage gap: this domain's epSSC/estrogen-senescence cluster has been assembled somewhat piecemeal across many batches (Schrier 2006, Nilsson 2014, Abad 2002, and others), and this 2001 paper appears to be an early, if not the earliest, direct experimental demonstration in that same research program — worth treating as a load-bearing foundational citation for the whole cluster.

## Relevance to This Domain

A foundational NICHD paper underlying this domain's [[Epiphyseal Skeletal Stem Cells and Hedgehog Signaling]] cluster and [[Epiphyseal Fusion Mechanisms]], likely predating and informing [[Depletion-Resting-Zone-Chondrocytes-Growth-Plate-Senescence|Schrier et al. 2006]] and [[Estrogen-Irreversible-Resting-Zone-Depletion-Nilsson2014|Nilsson et al. 2014]] from the same lab.
