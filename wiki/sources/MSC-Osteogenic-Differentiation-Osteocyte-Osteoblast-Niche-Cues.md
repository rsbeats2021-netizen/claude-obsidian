# Osteocyte/Osteoblast Niche Cues Regulate MSC Osteogenic Differentiation

Extends this domain's stem-cell niche literature by showing that MSC osteogenic differentiation is regulated not just by intrinsic cell programming but by biochemical stimuli actively supplied by surrounding osteocytes and osteoblasts within the bone niche — relevant context for [[Epiphyseal Skeletal Stem Cells and Hedgehog Signaling]]'s niche-dependent regulation model, though for the bone (not growth-plate) stem-cell compartment specifically.

## Relevance to This Domain

Niche-dependent regulation evidence for the bone stem-cell compartment, complementing [[Epiphyseal Skeletal Stem Cells and Hedgehog Signaling]]'s growth-plate-specific niche model.
