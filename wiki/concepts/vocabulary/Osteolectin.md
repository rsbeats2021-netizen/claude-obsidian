# Osteolectin

Osteolectin is a growth factor previously known only for maintaining adult bone mass. A 2023 PNAS paper found it has a distinct juvenile function: promoting growth-plate chondrocyte proliferation and thereby increasing body length in young mice — with a corroborating human genetic variant supplying rare direct human evidence for the gene's role in growth, a higher evidentiary bar than most of this domain's animal-model-only growth-factor sources meet.
