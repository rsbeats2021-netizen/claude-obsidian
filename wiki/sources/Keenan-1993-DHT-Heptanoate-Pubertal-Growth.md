# Keenan et al. 1993 — DHT-hp and the Pubertal Growth Spurt

*J Clin Endocrinol Metab* 76(4):996–1001. PMID 8473416. Full text obtained 2026-10-17; the dosing below is quoted from the Study Protocols section, which the abstract omits.

## Dosing — recovered from full text

**Delayed-puberty cohort (groups A and B):**

> "patients were treated with **three im injections, 1 month apart**, of either T enanthate (T treatment) or DHT heptanoate (DHT treatment), **100 mg for the first dose and 200 mg for the next two doses**. Two weeks after the third dose, the patients were readmitted…"

So **500 mg DHT heptanoate total over 2 months** (= 360.7 mg DHT-equivalent), given monthly. Rationale stated: "Previous studies have shown that plasma levels of T or DHT remain elevated for at least 3 weeks after an injection of these steroids." Assignment was initially alternating; once the GH effect replicated, more subjects were put on DHT.

**Gynecomastia cohort (group C):**

> "Treatment with DHT was given as an im injection (**300 mg every 2 weeks**) for 3 months."

Six injections, **1800 mg total** (= 1298 mg DHT-equivalent). This is the **highest DHT heptanoate regimen in the literature** — above the 200–400 mg q2–3wk claimed in [[Keenan-1990-DHT-Heptanoate-Patent-US4956357]].

## Subjects

| Group | n | Age (yr) | Ht (cm) | Ht age (yr) | Wt (kg) | Bone age (yr) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A (T enanthate) | 3 | 14.7 ± 0.45 | 146.8 ± 2.3 | 11.6 ± 0.4 | 36.0 ± 2.3 | 11.4 ± 0.7 |
| B (DHT heptanoate) | 6 | 14.5 ± 0.1 | 144.6 ± 3.8 | 11.3 ± 0.6 | 37.1 ± 2.7 | 11.8 ± 0.5 |

All nine aged 13 8/12 to 15 4/12 y, heights below the 5th percentile, Tanner 1–2, bone age delayed 1–4 y. Three were clinically and hormonally prepubertal, six early pubertal. Group C: 7 boys, 13 7/12 to 15 11/12 y, Tanner 4, mean height 168.2 ± 2.2 cm, weight 55.5 ± 2.6 kg, bone age 14.5 ± 0.35 y.

## Results at 2.5 months

| | TE (group A) | DHT-hp (group B) |
|---|---|---|
| Height velocity | 12.6 ± 2.8 cm/yr | **8.9 ± 1.7 cm/yr** (P = NS) |
| Integrated GH (ICGH) | 3.12 ± 0.9 → **13.67 ± 6.0** µg/L (P<0.05) | 4.32 ± 0.61 → **2.39 ± 0.42** — a ~50% fall (P<0.025) |
| IGF-1 | 126.7 ± 2.5 → **350.3 ± 20.3** µg/L (P<0.01) | 218.3 ± 39.2 → 184.0 ± 15.8 (NS) |
| Plasma T | 0.8 ± 0.5 → 33.8 ± 11.0 nmol/L (P<0.001) | 2.0 ± 0.5 → 2.7 ± 0.8 (NS) |
| LH response to LHRH | 27.6 ± 10.7 → 5.9 ± 2.5 IU/L (NS) | 45.7 ± 14.5 → 10.7 ± 5.8 (P<0.05) |
| Weight | 36.0 ± 2.3 → 39.8 ± 2.2 kg (P<0.01) | 37.1 ± 2.7 → 39.6 ± 3.1 kg (P<0.01) |
| Pubic hair | stage 1.1 → 2.0 | stage 1.2 → 2.2 (NS between groups) |

**Testis size did not change appreciably in either group** (A: 5.5 → 5.8 mm; B: 4.0 → 4.7 mm).

Plasma estradiol rose to 25.7 ± 0.5 pmol/L on T; **in the DHT group every value stayed below assay sensitivity** — the cleanest available demonstration that DHT-hp delivers androgen with zero estrogen.

Long-term height outcome (height percentile vs bone age, 1.5–3 y after treatment) did not change significantly in either group: A 0.18 ± 0.67 → −0.33 ± 1.7; B −0.41 ± 0.45 → −0.58 ± 0.47.

## Gynecomastia cohort on 300 mg q2wk × 3 months

Six of seven showed clinically significant breast-size reduction.

| | Pre | Post | P |
|---|---|---|---|
| ICGH | 9.84 ± 2.2 | 6.1 ± 2.5 µg/L | NS |
| Integrated testosterone (ICT) | 20.3 ± 2.4 | **5.6 ± 4.0** nmol/L | <0.05 |
| Plasma estradiol | 77.1 ± 18.7 | **20.2 ± 5.1** pmol/L | <0.01 |

Significant linear correlation between ICT and ICGH (r = 0.77, 18 df, P<0.001).

## Why this matters

DHT-hp produced a **normal-range pubertal growth spurt with GH falling by half and estradiol undetectable**. The authors' conclusion: the pubertal GH rise is estrogen-dependent, and the spurt does not require it. Assay note: plasma T and DHT were separated by Celite chromatography before RIA, "99.9% effective in separating T and DHT."
