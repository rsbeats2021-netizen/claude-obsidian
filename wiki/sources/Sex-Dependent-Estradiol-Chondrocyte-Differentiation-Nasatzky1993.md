# Sex-Dependent Estradiol Effects on Chondrocyte Differentiation: Foundational 1993 Paper (Nasatzky et al.)

The earliest paper in this domain's Boyan/Schwartz/Ornoy sex-dimorphism research lineage, establishing the basic phenomenon — sex-dependent chondrocyte response to estradiol — that [[Estrogen-IP3-Calcium-Growth-Plate-Sex-Specific-Ekstein2005|Ekstein et al. 2005]] later mechanistically dissected (capacitative calcium entry) and [[Membrane-Effects-Sex-Hormones-Growth-Plate-Chondrocytes-Thesis|ElBaradie's thesis]] extended further (membrane receptor localization). This confirms the lab's sex-dimorphism research program spans at least 1993-2013, a 20-year sustained investigation of the same core phenomenon.
