# GH Directly Stimulates Human Osteoblast Proliferation and Differentiation (Kassem et al. 1993)

A foundational 1993 human-cell-culture demonstration that GH acts directly on normal human osteoblast-like cells to stimulate both proliferation and differentiation — relevant evidence for the "GH acts directly, not just via liver IGF-I" side of the somatomedin-hypothesis debate already covered in this domain via [[Somatotropic-Axis-GH-IGF-Related-Independent-Roles-Butler2001|Butler & Le Roith 2001]].

## Relevance to This Domain

Direct human-osteoblast evidence for [[Growth Hormone-IGF-1 Axis]]'s "GH acts locally, not just systemically via IGF-I" thread, predating and supporting [[Somatotropic-Axis-GH-IGF-Related-Independent-Roles-Butler2001|Butler & Le Roith 2001]]'s later theoretical synthesis.
