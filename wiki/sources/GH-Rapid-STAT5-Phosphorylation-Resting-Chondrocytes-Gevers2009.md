# GH-Induced STAT5 Phosphorylation in Resting-Zone Chondrocytes and Liver (Gevers et al. 2009)

Directly demonstrates GH-receptor/JAK-STAT signaling activation (via STAT5 phosphorylation, the pathway's canonical molecular readout) specifically within resting-zone growth-plate chondrocytes — the same PTHrP+ stem-cell population central to [[Epiphyseal Skeletal Stem Cells and Hedgehog Signaling]] — and shows this response is modulated by feeding status, linking GH's local growth-plate action to nutritional state in a way this domain's other GH sources don't address.

> [!key-insight] This paper mechanistically anticipates [[Human-Growth-Plate-Transcriptional-Atlas-GH-Stem-Cells|Chu et al. 2025]]'s finding (16 years later) that GH activates JAK/STAT signaling in human resting-zone stem cells — Gevers et al. established the same pathway, in the same cell population, in rodents, well before the human single-cell confirmation.

> [!gap] Rodent model; the feeding-status modulation finding raises an unaddressed question for this domain — whether nutritional state independently gates GH's effect on the epSSC population covered in [[Epiphyseal Skeletal Stem Cells and Hedgehog Signaling]] — that no later source in this vault directly follows up on.

## Relevance to This Domain

Directly links [[Growth Hormone-IGF-1 Axis]] to the specific PTHrP+ resting-zone stem-cell population in [[Epiphyseal Skeletal Stem Cells and Hedgehog Signaling]], and adds nutritional/feeding status as an unexplored modulating variable for GH's action on this population.
