# GH + Stanozolol Combined Therapy in Turner Syndrome (Fang et al. 1999)

An early (1999) demonstration that combining GH with the anabolic steroid stanozolol improves growth rate in Turner syndrome girls — extending this domain's GH+anabolic-steroid combination-therapy thread (already covered via the 1974 GH+fluoxymesterone hypopituitary-dwarf trial) to a different underlying condition (Turner syndrome) and a different anabolic agent (stanozolol).

## Relevance to This Domain

Adds a Turner-syndrome-specific data point to the GH+anabolic-androgen combination-therapy thread spanning [[Growth Hormone-IGF-1 Axis]] and [[Testosterone]], complementing [[Growth-Hormone-Androgen-Synergy-Hypopituitary-Dwarfs|the 1974 hypopituitary-dwarf trial]] with a different patient population and drug.
