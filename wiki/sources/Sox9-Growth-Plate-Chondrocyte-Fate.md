# Sox9 Is Required to Maintain and Then Terminate Growth Plate Chondrocyte Proliferation (Dy et al. 2012)

Dy et al. (2012) demonstrate that Sox9, a master transcription factor of chondrocyte identity, plays two sequential and seemingly opposite roles in growth-plate biology. Early on, Sox9 maintains chondrocytes in a proliferative state and actively suppresses premature transition to hypertrophy, in part by antagonizing Runx2 and Wnt/β-catoken signaling. Later, in a switch, Sox9 becomes directly required to drive hypertrophic differentiation itself, partnering with the transcription factor Mef2c to directly activate Col10a1, a defining marker of hypertrophic chondrocytes.

> [!key-insight] Sox9 is not simply "pro-growth" or "pro-maturation" — it performs opposite functions depending on the maturation stage of the chondrocyte, which is a more sophisticated regulatory picture than a single linear pathway.
