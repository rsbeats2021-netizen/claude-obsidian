# Video script — "The organ missing from the steroid risk list"

60s · 176 words · ~178 wpm. **Only `SPOKEN:` lines are read aloud.**
⚠️ Register: this argues for a complete risk list, not a scare. Evidence is in vitro.

### [0:00–0:07] HOOK
SPOKEN: The steroid risk list everyone repeats is missing an organ. People know
        about the liver and the heart. The brain is on that list too, and
        almost nobody says so.
  [VISUAL: organ checklist, brain unticked]

### [0:07–0:16] SETUP
SPOKEN: The usual harm profile stops at the neck. Reproductive suppression,
        liver strain, kidney load, cardiac remodelling. That's the standard
        catalogue.
  [ON-SCREEN: "THE STANDARD LIST"]

### [0:16–0:34] EVIDENCE
SPOKEN: Applied directly to primary rat cortical cell cultures, anabolic
        androgenic steroids showed direct neurotoxic effects — on the cells
        themselves, not downstream of hormonal changes. Direct toxicity, not a
        secondary consequence.
  [ON-SCREEN: "DIRECT, NOT SECONDARY"]

### [0:34–0:48] PAYOFF
SPOKEN: Why that matters more than it sounds: most steroid side effects get
        discussed as reversible on cessation. Neurons are among the least
        replaceable cells you have. And anyone using these to affect height by
        definition still has open growth plates — which means an adolescent
        brain still structurally organising.
  [ON-SCREEN: "REVERSIBILITY DOESN'T TRANSFER"]

### [0:48–0:60] CAVEAT + CTA
SPOKEN: Now the limits, honestly. This is cell culture. Concentrations in a dish
        aren't doses in a person. But nobody has run the study showing
        adolescent use is neurologically safe either — and only one side of
        that uncertainty ever gets mentioned. Unstudied isn't cleared. Don't
        forget, it's never over.
  [ON-SCREEN: "UNSTUDIED ≠ CLEARED"]
