# Claim audit — 7 Sep

Scanned all decks for absence and exhaustiveness claims. 21 found. Each checked
against a literature search outside the vault.

## FALSE, now fixed

**"The entire evidence base is 4 randomised trials, 207 participants"**
(aromatase inhibitors)
Came from one systematic review's inclusion count. The field contains Hero 2006,
Mauras, Salehpour, a 2024 anastrozole vs letrozole 3-year trial in 79 males, an
anastrozole+GH multicentre trial, a 2019 monotherapy study, and Jacobson 2005
which I had already cited elsewhere. Deck rewritten around what actually
determines outcome: population, timing, and pairing with androgen.

**"Nobody has tested anything against AAS neurotoxicity"** (v2-aas-brain)
False. Aerobic and resistance exercise improved hippocampal plasticity and
reversed neurocognitive changes from nandrolone decanoate in rats, measured
physiologically, biochemically and histologically. That is a direct head to head
test and it worked.

**"The toxicity is androgen receptor mediated"** stated as universal
Wrong for at least one compound. Testosterone and nandrolone toxicity is AR
dependent and blocked by flutamide. Methandrostenolone is **glucocorticoid
receptor** dependent and flutamide does nothing to it.

**"GH gives 4 to 6 cm"** stated as a fixed effect (v2-gh-truth)
It is dose dependent. 0.16-0.28 mg/kg/week gives under 4 cm. 0.32-0.4
mg/kg/week gives 7-8 cm. One study reported 9.5 cm in males over untreated
controls. The deck was understating the upper end.

## VERIFIED, kept

**"Four competing theories for fusion, none confirmed in humans"**
Holds. Apoptosis, autophagy, hypoxia, transdifferentiation. Human fusing growth
plates contained no TUNEL positive cells and no electron microscopy signs of
apoptosis or autophagy. Nuance added: transdifferentiation is now considered the
best fit for observed human morphology, so the four are not equally weighted.

**"No trial tested GH for height in normal statured people"**
Searching found only idiopathic short stature and deficiency populations.
Reworded from a flat absence claim to "I could not find one, and there are
obvious reasons nobody would run it."

## NOT LITERATURE CLAIMS, left alone

- "Zero conversion" (aromatase deficient man) describes the patient's enzyme, not
  the field.
- "Almost nobody knows this" is about audience awareness.
- "No evidence for" in trenbolone-bone-length refers to that study's own result.

## MOST VALUABLE THING THIS AUDIT FOUND

Aromatisation and local estrogen generation underlie **testosterone induced
neuroprotection**. Aromatisable androgens carry a built in defence that 19-nors
like nandrolone and trenbolone do not, which is a mechanistic explanation for
why those two look worst in the toxicity data.

It also creates an interaction nobody in this space discusses: running an
aromatase inhibitor to hold the growth plate open simultaneously removes the
local estrogen protecting your neurons.
