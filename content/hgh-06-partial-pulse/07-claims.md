# Claims — hgh-06-partial-pulse
## Gevers et al. 1996, Endocrinology (hypophysectomised rat)
`.raw/.../Growth, GH-Binding Protein, and GH Receptors Are Differentially Regulated - Gevers et al - 1996.pdf`
QUOTE: "the combined treatment (36p + 36c) was more effective than the same dose
all given continuously (72c) but less effective (P < 0.05) than the same dose all
given in pulses (72p)"
→ Continuous < half-pulsed < fully pulsed, at one total dose. A gradient.
QUOTE: "The 36p + 36c combined infusion was much more effective than 36c alone
(P < 0.01) but not significantly better than 36p alone."
→ Adding a continuous background on top of a pulse added nothing measurable.
QUOTE: "pulsatile infusion increased weight gain more than a continuous infusion
(P < 0.01) and this was apparent over the entire range of doses tested"
QUOTE: "Body growth, measured by nose-anus length gain, was also more effectively
stimulated by pulsatile infusions (P = 0.014)"
## Limit stated on the slide
Hypophysectomised rats, intravenous infusion. No human trial has tested a real
pulse; the one human split-dose trial (Lundberg 2015) found no height difference.
