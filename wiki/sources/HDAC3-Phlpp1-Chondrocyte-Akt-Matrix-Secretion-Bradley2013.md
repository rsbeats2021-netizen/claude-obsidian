# HDAC3 Regulates Chondrocyte Akt Signaling and Matrix Secretion via Phlpp1 (Bradley et al. 2013)

Extends this domain's HDAC-mediated chondrocyte epigenetic regulation thread — already covering HDAC4's role in the PTHrP/MEF2 pathway ([[PTHrP-Ihh Feedback Loop|Kozhemyakina et al. 2009]]) — to a second, mechanistically distinct HDAC isoform: HDAC3 suppression increases Phlpp1 expression, which in turn suppresses Akt signaling and reduces extracellular matrix secretion in chondrocytes, showing HDAC-family enzymes regulate chondrocyte behavior through multiple independent downstream pathways.

## Relevance to This Domain

A second, mechanistically distinct HDAC-family regulatory pathway (HDAC3/Phlpp1/Akt) for [[Molecular Regulators of Growth Plate Chondrocyte Maturation]], alongside [[PTHrP-Ihh Feedback Loop|HDAC4's role in the PTHrP/MEF2 pathway]].
