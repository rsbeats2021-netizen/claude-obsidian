# The largest GH pulse is tied to the first N3 block

**Verdict: supported.** Described since Takahashi, Kipnis & Daughaday 1968 (*J Clin Invest*) and reviewed by Van Cauter & Plat 1996 (*J Pediatr*).

## Consequence
Deep sleep is front-loaded in the night, so a fragmented or delayed first cycle costs the largest pulse, and sleeping in later does not recover it.
