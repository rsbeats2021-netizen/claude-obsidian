# Research brief — GH and IGF-1 do different jobs

## Core claim
GH and IGF-1 act on different stages of the same chondrocyte lineage. GH
recruits resting-zone cells into the proliferative pool; IGF-1 expands cells
already proliferating. They are not interchangeable.

## Key points (tier-tagged, Hunziker)
- **Tier 1** — Hypophysectomised rats (no endogenous pituitary hormone) infused
  with IGF-I or GH separately — a clean single-variable separation.
- **Tier 1** — GH primarily recruits resting-zone chondrocytes into the
  proliferative pool. It sets how many cells enter.
- **Tier 1** — IGF-I acts on already-proliferating cells, driving expansion. It
  sets how far each goes.
- **Tier 1** — Among the earliest direct in vivo evidence against a purely
  "GH acts only via liver IGF-I" model; supports dual local action.
- **Tier 2** — Modern confirmation: GH acts directly on human growth plate stem
  cells via JAK/STAT and autocrine TGF-β, independent of the liver axis
  (Chu 2025).

## Why this deck is structurally useful
It corrects the single most common simplification in this topic — treating
GH and IGF-1 as one axis with two names — using a 1994 experiment whose design
(hypophysectomy) is cleaner than anything achievable in humans.
