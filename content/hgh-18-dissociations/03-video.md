Nine times. Every one of them is a published experiment.

[cover card]

The growth genuinely broke. And circulating IGF-1 was unchanged throughout: 10.3 against 10.7 nmol/l at 4 days, which is noise.

[loveridge 1995]

GH shortened the reserve cell cycle to 8 days against IGF-1's 15, shortened the dividing cell cycle to 3 days against 4.5, and beat IGF-1 at every single stage that was measured. It did all of that while producing a lower level of circulating IGF-1 than the group given IGF-1 directly.

[hunziker 1994]

Energy deficit raises GH and lowers IGF-1 simultaneously, because the liver downregulates its GH receptors and stops answering the signal. Rats fasted for 3 days dropped one tibial growth plate to 30% of control and stopped the other one completely.

[fasting]

The same range of GH doses, 0.01 to 0.10 mg/kg/day, produced IGF-1 results above and below the 2 SDS line, with the dose ranges for the two groups overlapping almost completely. Pubertal stage moves it.

[ly 2020]

If nine experiments across forty years can move your blood marker one direction and your actual growth the other, then that marker is not the thing you are trying to change. It is one readout, with its own set of confounders, sitting several steps downstream of the process that actually matters to you.

[takeaway card]

Stop grading yourself on a number that has gone the wrong way nine times.
