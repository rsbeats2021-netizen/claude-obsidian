# Pulsatile GH Infusion Is More Effective Than Continuous for Skeletal IGF-I mRNA (Isgaard et al. 1988)

Establishes that GH's secretion PATTERN — pulsatile vs. continuous — matters independently of total dose for its skeletal-tissue IGF-I-mRNA-inducing effect, using the hypophysectomized-rat model already used elsewhere in this domain. This directly complements [[GH-Rapid-STAT5-Phosphorylation-Resting-Chondrocytes-Gevers2009|Gevers et al.]] and other GH-pulsatility-relevant sources.

## Relevance to This Domain

Foundational GH-pulsatility evidence for [[Growth Hormone-IGF-1 Axis]], establishing that temporal secretion pattern (not just dose) governs GH's skeletal IGF-I-inducing efficacy.
