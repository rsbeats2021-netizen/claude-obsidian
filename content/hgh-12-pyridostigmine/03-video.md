Pyridostigmine. It doubled the GH response to exercise.

[cover card]

The same exercise after pyridostigmine produced 587.3, at P < 0.005. Peak GH went from 6.5 to 13.5 µg/l, P < 0.01.

[what it did to exercise gh]

This is the one nobody quotes and it changes what you would do with the drug. They gave two GHRH stimuli 90 minutes apart on a pyridostigmine background and measured both.

[the finding that matters]

They ran it in young and old men during exercise. It raised the GH area under the curve during maximal exercise, 20.9 against 4.9, and during submaximal exercise, 12.3 against 4.7, both at P < 0.05.

[marcell 1999]

Every human study quoted here uses pyridostigmine as a research probe, to ask how much somatostatin is currently holding GH down in a given person. That is what the pyridostigmine test is for and that is why these experiments exist.

[takeaway card]

The mechanism is solid. The application is not studied.
