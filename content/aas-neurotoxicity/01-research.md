# Research brief — AAS and the central nervous system

## Core claim
Anabolic androgenic steroids show direct neurotoxic effects in primary rat
cortical cultures. The standard AAS risk catalogue stops at reproductive,
hepatic, renal and cardiac — the CNS is routinely omitted.

## Key points (tier-tagged)
- **Tier 2** — Direct neurotoxic effects in primary rat cortical cell cultures
  (Zelleroth). Effects on the cells themselves, not downstream of hormonal
  change.
- **Tier 2** — Most AAS effects are discussed as reversible on cessation.
  Neurons are among the least replaceable cell types.
- **Tier 2** — The population using AAS for height necessarily has open growth
  plates, i.e. an adolescent brain still structurally maturing.

## Honest limits — slides 6 and 7
Cell culture. Dish concentrations are not human doses; cortical cultures are
not a behaving brain. Slide 7 makes the symmetric point: no study has shown
adolescent AAS use is neurologically safe either, and only one side of that
uncertainty usually gets mentioned.

## Tone requirement
This deck argues for a **complete risk list**, not for a scare. Slide 8's framing
("incomplete risk lists are how bad decisions look reasonable") is the correct
register. Do not escalate it into asserted human harm — the evidence is in vitro.
