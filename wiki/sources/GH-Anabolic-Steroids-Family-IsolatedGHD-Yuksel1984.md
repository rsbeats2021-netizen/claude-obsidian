# Low-Dose GH + Fluoxymesterone Matches High-Dose GH Alone in Isolated GHD Siblings (Yüksel et al. 1984)

A four-sibling family case series (Turkish, Ankara Tıp Mecmuası) directly extending this domain's GH-androgen-synergy thread ([[Growth-Hormone-Androgen-Synergy-Hypopituitary-Dwarfs|the 1974 hypopituitary-dwarf trial]] already in this domain): combining low-dose hGH with the anabolic steroid fluoxymesterone (already separately covered in this domain via [[Fluoxymesterone-Accelerates-Delayed-Growth-Puberty-1982|the 1982 fluoxymesterone paper]]) in continuous or intermittent regimens over 32 months produced growth velocities (7.7 and 4.6 cm/year respectively) comparable to historically higher, costlier hGH-monotherapy doses — without disproportionate bone-age advancement, a favorable result relative to this domain's other anabolic-androgen-accelerates-skeletal-maturation findings (Stirling 1994).

> [!key-insight] Unlike [[Growth-Promotion-Short-Normal-Child-Thesis|Stirling 1994]]'s finding that oxandrolone accelerated skeletal maturation disproportionately to growth, this paper reports the opposite outcome for a different combination (low-dose GH + fluoxymesterone): comparable growth without undue bone-age cost — suggesting the specific androgen, dose, and GH-co-administration pattern matter for whether the growth/maturation tradeoff this domain repeatedly documents actually manifests.

## Relevance to This Domain

A cost-effectiveness and safety data point for [[Growth-Hormone-Androgen-Synergy-Hypopituitary-Dwarfs|this domain's GH-androgen-synergy thread]], and a favorable counterpoint to [[Growth-Promotion-Short-Normal-Child-Thesis|Stirling 1994]]'s growth/skeletal-maturation-tradeoff finding for a different drug combination.
