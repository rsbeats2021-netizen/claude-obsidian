# Uniform pattern of growth and skeletal maturation

**Design.** Two longitudinal cohorts followed with serial radiographs and anthropometry to final adult height: **Brush Foundation (n=54: 35F, 19M)** and **Berkeley Guidance (n=135: 69F, 66M)**. Tier B.

## Key claims
- **Stage 2** (epiphyses as wide as metaphyses): 85% of adult height, ~2 years before peak growth.
- **Stage 3** (all digits capping): peak height velocity, 90% of final height.
- **Stages 4-5** (distal phalanges begin to fuse): 96% of final height, 1.5 years after peak.
- Growth approaches adult height asymptotically by ~3.5 years after peak.
- Normalised to final height and standardised by peak growth age, **boys' and girls' growth patterns become identical**.

## Limits
Mid-century American cohorts, modest n. Generalisation beyond them is an assumption, not a finding.
