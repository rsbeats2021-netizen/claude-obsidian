# Thyroid Hormone Gates GH/IGF-I Axis Function: A Clinical Review (Laron 2003)

By Zvi Laron (of Laron syndrome fame), this review establishes that normal GH/IGF-I secretion and action is conditional on a euthyroid state — thyroid hormone replacement must be established before any GH-axis testing or treatment is meaningful in hypothyroid patients, since thyroid status itself gates the axis's function rather than merely modulating it. Also identifies ghrelin (the natural hypothalamic GH-secretagogue-receptor ligand) as a specific crosstalk point: circulating and gastric ghrelin rises in hypothyroidism (high TSH) and falls in hyperthyroidism and GH-deficient states.

## Relevance to This Domain

A clinically-oriented review establishing thyroid status as a gating condition for [[Growth Hormone-IGF-1 Axis]] function, complementing [[Thyroid-Hormone-Receptor-Skeletal-Phenotypes|O'Shea et al. 2005]]'s more mechanistic thyroid-receptor-mutant mouse work already in this domain.
