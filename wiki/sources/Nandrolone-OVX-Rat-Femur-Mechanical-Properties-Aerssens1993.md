# Nandrolone Decanoate Effects on OVX Rat Femur Mechanics and Composition (Aerssens et al. 1993)

Directly cited as prior reference material by [[DHT-Estrogen-Combined-Bone-Density-Growth-Ovariectomized-Rats|Coxam et al. 1996]] already in this domain, this paper characterizes nandrolone decanoate's effects on femur mechanical properties, mineral content, and composition (collagen, osteocalcin, IGF-I) in the same ovariectomized-rat model — extending this domain's nandrolone catalog to detailed bone-composition and biomechanical outcomes.

## Relevance to This Domain

Supplies the detailed biomechanical/compositional data underlying the nandrolone-in-OVX-rats precedent that [[DHT-Estrogen-Combined-Bone-Density-Growth-Ovariectomized-Rats|Coxam et al. 1996]] built on, extending [[Testosterone]]'s nandrolone catalog with mechanical-property and IGF-I-linkage detail.
