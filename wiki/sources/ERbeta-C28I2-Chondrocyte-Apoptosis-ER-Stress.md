# ERβ Regulates Chondrocyte Proliferation/Apoptosis via ER Stress in C28I2 Cells

A mechanistically precise study using both gain-of-function (adenoviral ESR2/ERβ overexpression) and loss-of-function (ESR2 siRNA knockdown) approaches in the human chondrocyte cell line C28I2: estradiol's effect on chondrocyte proliferation and apoptosis operates through endoplasmic reticulum (ER, in the cell-biology sense — unfortunately a name collision with estrogen receptor) stress pathway proteins, a specific cellular-stress mechanism distinct from the classical genomic, GPER-1, and mechanotransduction mechanisms already catalogued in [[Three Estrogen Receptor Mechanisms in the Growth Plate]].

## Relevance to This Domain

A fourth distinct ERβ-specific mechanism (endoplasmic-reticulum-stress-mediated apoptosis) for [[Three Estrogen Receptor Mechanisms in the Growth Plate]] and [[Estrogen Receptor (ER)]], using rigorous gain- and loss-of-function methodology in a human chondrocyte cell line.
