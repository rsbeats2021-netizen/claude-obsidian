# DHT's Sex-Dependent Rapid Membrane Response in Growth Plate Chondrocytes (ElBaradie et al. 2012)

The actual published journal article behind Chapter 4 of [[Membrane-Effects-Sex-Hormones-Growth-Plate-Chondrocytes-Thesis|ElBaradie's 2012 PhD thesis]], which this vault previously read only via front matter and summary. Confirms DHT produces rapid, sex-dependent, non-genomic membrane signaling in growth-plate chondrocytes — extending the same rapid-membrane-response paradigm already established for estradiol (via [[DHT-Rapid-Membrane-Response-Sex-Dependent-ElBaradie2012|this paper]] and [[Estrogen-SexSpecific-Membrane-Response-ElBaradie2013|ElBaradie et al. 2013]]) to the androgen side.

## Relevance to This Domain

Directly supplements the summary-level [[Membrane-Effects-Sex-Hormones-Growth-Plate-Chondrocytes-Thesis|ElBaradie 2012 thesis]] entry already in this domain with the actual published experimental detail, extending [[Androgen Receptor (AR)]]'s mechanism catalog with a rapid, non-genomic, sex-dependent membrane pathway.
