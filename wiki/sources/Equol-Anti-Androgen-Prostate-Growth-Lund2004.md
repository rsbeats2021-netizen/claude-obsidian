# Equol as a Novel Anti-Androgen Inhibiting Prostate Growth (Lund et al. 2004)

An earlier paper (2004) by Kenneth Setchell — co-author of [[S-Equol-Estrogen-Receptor-Anti-Androgen|Setchell et al. 2005]] already in this domain — establishing equol's anti-androgen action in a different tissue context: prostate growth and hormone feedback inhibition, a year before the 2005 paper's ERβ/DHT-sequestration mechanism was published.

## Relevance to This Domain

A direct predecessor paper to [[S-Equol-Estrogen-Receptor-Anti-Androgen|Setchell et al. 2005]], extending equol's anti-androgen mechanism to a prostate-growth context and confirming this research group's sustained interest in equol's anti-androgen properties across multiple tissue systems.
