# Tension Band Plate Epiphysiodesis for Limb Length Discrepancy: Clinical Outcomes

A direct clinical application of growth-plate manipulation for a structural (rather than hormonal/endocrine) indication: reviews outcomes in 66 patients treated with tension-band-plate tethering — a reversible epiphysiodesis technique — for limb length discrepancy, discussing optimal timing and technique based on complications observed. Complements this domain's otherwise hormone-focused growth-plate-manipulation literature (aromatase inhibitors, GH therapy) with a purely mechanical/surgical intervention.
