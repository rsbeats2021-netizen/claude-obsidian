# Androgens and Skeletal Homeostasis: A Review Favoring Estradiol Dominance

A comprehensive review of both gonadal (testosterone) and adrenal (DHEA, DHEA-sulfate, androstenedione) androgens' skeletal effects throughout life, directly engaging this domain's central estrogen-vs-androgen controversy and concluding — consistent with [[Estrogen vs Androgen in Male Bone Health|the "it's really estrogen" camp]] already documented in this domain — that current evidence favors estradiol as the dominant mediator of androgen's skeletal benefit, via aromatization.

## Relevance to This Domain

A comprehensive review adding to the "estradiol-dominant" side of [[Estrogen vs Androgen in Male Bone Health]]'s central controversy, covering both gonadal and adrenal androgen sources.
