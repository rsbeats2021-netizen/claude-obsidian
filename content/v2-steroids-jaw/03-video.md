Steroids grew the jaw. THE STATS ARE VERY SPECIFIC

[cover card]

Wistar-Kyoto rats, juvenile and adult, 70 days of nandrolone against control. Lateral head films before and after, digitised at 300% for measurement precision.

[the study]

Height rose 41.2% in treated animals against 32.0% in controls. Length rose 27.2% against 24.5%.

[in juveniles]

Length rose 4.7% in treated against 0.3% in controls. Height rose 2.0% against 1.4%, and that difference was not significant.

[in adults]

Not that steroids grow the jaw in general. Juveniles gained height.

[which is the real finding]

Adult controls gained 0.2 mm of mandibular length over 70 days. Adult treated animals gained 3.6 mm.

[the adult result]

In juveniles, IGF-1 levels were comparable between treated and control. In adults, treated animals ran 10% higher IGF-1, significant at P<.01.

[and the mechanism]

Female Wistar-Kyoto rats on supraphysiologic nandrolone. Rodent craniofacial growth is not human craniofacial growth.

[takeaway card]

Real, measured, and much narrower than it gets quoted as.
