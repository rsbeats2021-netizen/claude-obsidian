# DHT Propionate Animal Dose Ledger

Every dosing regimen for **5α-dihydrotestosterone propionate (DHTP)** extractable from PubMed abstracts, as of 2026-10-17. Method: phrase search on `"dihydrotestosterone propionate"`, `"DHT propionate"`, `"5alpha-dihydrotestosterone propionate"` and `"DHTP"` in title/abstract (154 unique PMIDs), abstracts fetched via E-utilities, then filtered to records containing both the literal phrase and a dose figure. 148 records matched the phrase; ~50 carried an explicit DHTP dose.

**Conversion:** DHTP is 83.82% DHT by mass. Multiply any dose below by 0.838 for DHT-equivalent.

**No study in this set reports a mg/mL concentration.** Vehicle is named in only a handful (noted below); route is subcutaneous in oil unless stated.

Rows are keyed by PMID alone, because the doses were extracted programmatically from abstracts rather than read in full text. Resolve any row with `https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/<PMID>/`. Where an abstract names a comparator's dose but not the DHTP dose, the row says so rather than carrying the comparator across.

## Patterns worth keeping

- **The 5:1 TP:DHTP ratio** recurs in primate work (100 mg TP vs 20 mg DHTP), reflecting DHT's higher AR affinity rather than any PK equivalence.
- **The threshold structure is sharp.** Tfm rats: nothing at 50 µg/100 g, suppression at 2 mg/100 g — a 40-fold window. Male calling and copulatory behaviour: 50 µg/day inactive alone, active with 1 µg/day estradiol benzoate (PMID 6871034). Dose-finding in this literature is almost always interaction-finding.
- **Where DHTP fails and TP succeeds, the authors of those papers attribute the difference to aromatisation** — sheep behaviour (PMID 99256), primate sexual behaviour (PMID 3770656), human gonadotropin suppression ([[Forti-1983-DHT-Propionate-Klinefelter]]). That the ester was *kept in service* for that property is my reading of the literature's shape, not a claim any of these papers makes.
