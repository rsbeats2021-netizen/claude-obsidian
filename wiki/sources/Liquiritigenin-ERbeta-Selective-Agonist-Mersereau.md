# The Original Discovery of Liquiritigenin as an ERβ-Selective Agonist (Mersereau et al.)

The foundational primary-research paper behind this domain's cluster of liquiritigenin-related sources: motivated by the Women's Health Initiative's finding that conventional hormone therapy risks outweighed benefits, this study explored Traditional Chinese Medicine botanicals for ERβ-selective activity and isolated liquiritigenin — from licorice root (Glycyrrhizae uralensis), one component of the herbal formula MF101 — as a highly selective ERβ agonist. This is likely the original discovery paper that [[Liquiritigenin-Isoliquiritigenin-Pharmacology-Review-Ramalingam|Ramalingam's later review]] and this domain's other liquiritigenin sources build upon.
