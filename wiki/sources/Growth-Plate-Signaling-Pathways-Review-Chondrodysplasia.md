# Growth Plate Signaling Pathways and Skeletal Disorders: A Review

A broad synthesizing review of the major morphogen pathways (and systemic/tissue-specific factors) governing growth-plate development, and how their disruption — via mutation or environmental factors — produces severe skeletal disorders, situating this domain's many individual pathway-specific findings (PTHrP/Ihh, FGFR3, Sox9/Runx2, estrogen/androgen receptors) within a unified developmental framework.
