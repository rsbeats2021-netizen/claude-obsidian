# Androgens for Constitutional Delay of Growth: Final Adult Stature Data (1973)

One of the earliest clinical data points in this domain's androgen-for-constitutional-delay-of-growth-and-puberty (CDGP) literature: a 1973 retrospective study comparing 19 boys treated with one of three androgens (methyltestosterone, fluoxymesterone, or testosterone enanthate) against 21 untreated controls, examining final adult stature outcomes — directly complementing [[Fluoxymesterone-Accelerates-Delayed-Growth-Puberty-1982|the 1982 fluoxymesterone paper]] already in this domain and extending the same clinical question (does androgen treatment for CDGP compromise or preserve final height) back to its earliest documented instance.

## Relevance to This Domain

An early (1973) clinical data point for [[Testosterone]]'s CDGP-treatment literature, directly complementing [[Fluoxymesterone-Accelerates-Delayed-Growth-Puberty-1982|the 1982 fluoxymesterone paper]] on the same clinical question.
