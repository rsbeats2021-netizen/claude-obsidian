# Idiopathic Short Stature in the Genomic Era (Paparella et al. 2025)

A modern update to [[Short-Stature-Children-Classic-Review-Frasier1981|Frasier 1981]]'s classic short-stature review already in this domain, integrating auxology, classical endocrinology, and emerging genetic diagnostic insights — illustrating how the clinical approach to idiopathic short stature has evolved from purely growth-chart/hormone-measurement diagnosis toward genomic evaluation over the 44 years between these two reviews.
