# APEX1: A Novel Transcriptional Hub for Endochondral Ossification (2025)

Identifies APEX1 as a transcriptional hub coordinating the endochondral ossification process specifically in the context of bone fracture repair — a novel transcription-factor node for this domain's [[Molecular Regulators of Growth Plate Chondrocyte Maturation|multi-layer transcription-factor network]] (already covering Sox9, Runx2, HDAC4), extending it to the fracture-repair (rather than developmental-growth-plate) context of endochondral ossification.
