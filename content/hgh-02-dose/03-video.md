# Video script — Doubling your HGH dose

213 words, ~71s at 178 wpm. Target band 45-90s.

Spoken lines only. Visual cue in brackets.

---

Doubling your HGH dose. There is a randomised trial on this.

[cover card]

Standard group: 0.3 mg per kg per week. High dose group: 0.7 mg per kg per week.

[the randomised trial]

Subjects in the high dose group were significantly taller at near-adult height by an average of 4.6 cm, P < 0.001, n = 75, with a 95% confidence interval of 2.6 to 6.5 cm. For those treated at least 4 years the gap widened to 5.7 cm.

[the answer]

Before the study started both groups were growing at 8.5 cm per year, which tells you the randomisation worked. In the first year on study the high dose group hit 9.8 cm per year against 8.2 in the standard group, P = 0.001 between groups.

[centimetres per year]

At any given point in time roughly 28% of the high dose group had IGF-1 above the normal range, against about 9% in the standard group. And the high dose group showed a greater increase in fasting insulin, P = 0.011, and C-peptide, P = 0.019.

[where the cost showed up]

Treat the dose response as a curve that bends rather than a line that climbs. The trial that more than doubled the dose bought 4.6 cm over years, and an independent review summarises it the same way.

[takeaway card]

Doubling the dose buys centimetres, not inches.
