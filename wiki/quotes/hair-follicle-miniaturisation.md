# Quotes — hair follicle miniaturisation

---

> "KRT15hi stem cells were maintained in bald scalp", while "CD200hi ITGA6hi and CD34hi cell populations were markedly diminished."

- **Source:** Garza LA et al., *J Clin Invest* 2011;121 · paired bald/haired biopsies, same men
- **Tier:** B — human tissue, paired design
- **Shows:** the follicle is not lost; the stem-to-progenitor conversion is what fails
- **Figure:** [[fig-2011-garza-marker-loss]]
- **Used in:** [[deck-a-hair]] slides 03-04

---

> The finding suggests "AGA can be reversed by re-activating the conversion of HFSCs into progenitor cells."

- **Source:** Garza LA et al., *J Clin Invest* 2011 · **Tier:** D for the reversal claim
- **Shows:** biological plausibility of reversal — **not** evidence any product achieves it
- **Used in:** [[deck-a-hair]] slide 07
- **Note:** this is exactly the sentence the niche over-reads. Keep the tier split visible.
