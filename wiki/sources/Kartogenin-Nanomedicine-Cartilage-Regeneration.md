# Nanomedicine Delivery of Kartogenin for Cartilage Regeneration (Bhuyan et al.)

A review of nanomedicine-based delivery strategies (nanoparticles, hydrogels, scaffolds) for kartogenin — a small molecule already known to promote chondrogenic differentiation of mesenchymal stem cells — addressing kartogenin's poor native bioavailability as the key translational barrier to clinical cartilage-regeneration use.

> [!gap] A drug-delivery-technology review rather than a mechanistic or clinical efficacy study; kartogenin's underlying chondrogenic mechanism is assumed rather than newly investigated here.
