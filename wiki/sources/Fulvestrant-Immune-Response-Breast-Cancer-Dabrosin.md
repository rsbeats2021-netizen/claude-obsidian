# Fulvestrant, Innate Immunity, and ER+ Breast Cancer (Abrahamsson et al. 2020)

Another fulvestrant paper in the same oncology tradition as [[Fulvestrant-Breast-Cancer-Review-Nathan-Schmid|Nathan & Schmid 2017]], this one experimental rather than a review: fulvestrant (SERD) reduces ER+ breast cancer tumor growth more effectively than tamoxifen (SERM) specifically by suppressing innate immune cell (macrophage, neutrophil) infiltration into the tumor stroma, even in the presence of physiologic estradiol levels representative of premenopausal patients. A 234-protein microdialysis panel found immune-associated proteins uniquely altered by fulvestrant, with matching upregulation confirmed in human patient tumor samples.

> [!contradiction] Flagged as topically unrelated to this domain's growth-plate/bone focus, for the same reason as [[Fulvestrant-Breast-Cancer-Review-Nathan-Schmid|the Nathan & Schmid review]]: same drug (fulvestrant) and receptor (ER) already covered via [[Fulvestrant-Precocious-Puberty-McCune-Albright|Sims et al. 2012]]'s pediatric use, but this paper's tumor-immunology mechanism has no growth-plate or bone relevance.
