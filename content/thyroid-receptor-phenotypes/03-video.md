The same mutation. Same mutation, same hormone levels, opposite outcome.

[cover card]

High, low or normal. A number on a blood test telling you how much hormone is floating around.

[as a level]

Mice with a dominant negative mutation in thyroid hormone receptor beta showed skeletal thyrotoxicosis. Accelerated growth, advanced bone age, craniosynostosis.

[in receptor beta]

The identical mutation aimed at TR alpha 1 gave the reverse. Severe growth retardation, delayed ossification, delayed skull suture closure.

[in receptor alpha]

The TR alpha animals had normal circulating thyroid hormone. A blood test would have shown nothing wrong at all.

[that breaks the model]

TR beta dominates in the pituitary, so breaking it wrecks feedback and floods the system. TR alpha dominates in bone, so breaking it makes the tissue itself deaf.

[is division of labour]

A man with normal testosterone never stopped growing because his estrogen receptor was broken. Different hormone, identical principle.

[takeaway card]

Signal and receiver are separate failures.
