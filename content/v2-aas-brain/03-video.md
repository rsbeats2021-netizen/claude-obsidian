Steroids kill neurons. THE DIFFERENCE IS AROMATISATION

[cover card]

Cortical neurons grown from rat brain, dosed with different androgens. They measured mitochondrial activity, LDH release which means membranes are leaking, and caspase 3/7, the enzyme that executes apoptosis.

[the experiment]

reduced neurite outgrowth. Nandrolone hit mitochondria by 24 hours, earlier than the others.

[section: the worst one]

killed by necrosis instead of apoptosis, and did not raise LDH the same way. Necrosis is uncontrolled rupture, which inflames surrounding tissue.

[stanozolol]

Memantine blocks excitotoxic calcium influx upstream of apoptosis. TAK-653 and low dose ketamine raise BDNF.

[others in that class]

Aromatisable androgens carry protection that 19-nors do not. Exercise has direct evidence against this specific damage.

[section: what to actually do]

Aerobic and resistance training improved hippocampal plasticity and reversed neurocognitive changes induced by nandrolone decanoate in rats. This is a direct test against AAS neurotoxicity, not a general claim.

[takeaway card]

The one intervention with a head to head result.
