# Weinbauer et al. 1990 — TE vs DHT-Enanthate in Monkeys

*Acta Endocrinol (Copenh)* 122(4):432–42. PMID 2333732. DOI 10.1530/acta.0.1220432. The definitive head-to-head. Full text obtained 2026-10-17.

## Formulation — the third published DHT ester concentration

From Methods, verbatim:

> "Groups of four orchidectomized monkeys received a single im injection of **32.7 mg DHTE** (5.8–9.9 mg/kg) or 32.8 mg TE (7.1–9.9 mg/kg), i.e. 23.6 mg pure steroid, **dissolved in 1 ml sesame oil**. The DHTE preparation was provided by the **World Health Organization** (Geneva, Switzerland) and **Dr G. Bialy (Contraceptive Development Branch, Center for Population Research, National Institutes of Child Health and Human Development**, Bethesda, MD)."

So **32.7 mg/mL DHT enanthate in sesame oil = 23.6 mg/mL DHT-equivalent**, in a 1 mL IM injection. Experiment 2 used the same 32.7 mg in 1 mL sesame oil at 2.1–3.2 mg/kg in the larger rhesus monkeys.

Two things follow from the provenance line. First, this is a **WHO/NICHD contraceptive-development preparation**, not a commercial product — so DHT enanthate was formally in the male-contraceptive pipeline, which no published trial ever reports (see the open gaps in [[DHT Esters — Dosing and Concentrations]]). Second, 32.7 mg/mL is nowhere near the 190 mg/mL solubility ceiling the [[Keenan-1990-DHT-Heptanoate-Patent-US4956357]] establishes for the same ester in the same vehicle, so the concentration was chosen for dose convenience, not forced by solubility.

## Dose — and the mass-fraction confirmation

Single IM injection of **32.8 mg testosterone enanthate** or **32.7 mg dihydrotestosterone-enanthate, "i.e. 23.6 mg of pure steroid."**

23.6 / 32.7 = **72.2%**, against the calculated DHT mass fraction of the C7 ester (290.45 / 402.62 = 72.14%) — agreement to within the rounding of the stated 23.6 mg. This single phrase validates every ester→DHT conversion in [[DHT Esters — Dosing and Concentrations]].

## Experiment 1 — orchidectomised cynomolgus (*M. fascicularis*)

- Serum testosterone rose to **400%** of baseline (TE); serum DHT to **800%** of baseline (DHTE), both within 24 h.
- Held in range **3–5 days**, then continuous decline to baseline at **4–5 weeks**.
- **AUC did not differ significantly: 2290 ± 340 (DHTE) vs 2920 ± 485 (TE)** — equivalent steroid released from the two ester preparations.
- Terminal elimination t½: **TE 4 days, DHTE 7 days**.

## Experiment 2 — GnRH-agonist-suppressed rhesus (*M. mulatta*)

Buserelin-loaded biodegradable polylactide:polyglycolide rods s.c. every 4 weeks, last injection week 20. Agonist treatment suppressed bioactive LH, testosterone, DHT, testicular size, sperm production and seminal carnitine, and abolished the electrostimulated ejaculatory response and masturbatory behaviour.

Androgen injections at the same doses in **weeks 10, 14, 17 and 20**: serum T stimulated **9-fold**, serum DHT **4-fold**. Mean t½ **TE 5 days, DHT 7 days**.

**Both esters completely restored** ejaculatory response, ejaculate size, masturbatory behaviour and seminal carnitine.

> "androgen substitution with dihydrotestosterone-enanthate, in equivalent doses, is as effective as testosterone-enanthate in restoring reproductive functions in hypogonadal monkeys."
