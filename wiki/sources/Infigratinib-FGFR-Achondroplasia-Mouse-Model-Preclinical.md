# Infigratinib in a Mouse Model of Achondroplasia: The Preclinical Foundation

The preclinical mouse-model study directly preceding and underlying [[Infigratinib-FGFR-Achondroplasia-Savarirayan2024|Savarirayan et al. 2024]]'s later NEJM human clinical trial already in this domain: using a mouse model precisely engineered to mimic human achondroplasia (Fgfr3^Y367C/+), daily infigratinib (BGJ398) at 2 mg/kg significantly improved the skeletal phenotype, providing the mechanistic and dosing foundation that justified subsequent human trials.

## Relevance to This Domain
