# Research brief — Anabolic steroids and craniofacial growth

## Core claim
Nandrolone altered mandibular growth in rats — altered, not enhanced — and the
effect was site-dependent, which makes distortion more likely than improvement.

## Key points (tier-tagged)
- **Tier 2** — Nandrolone altered mandibular growth in juvenile **and** adult
  female rats, by radiographic cephalometry and immunoradiology (Gebhardt).
- **Tier 2** — Craniofacial effects are site-dependent; a second data point
  exists across craniofacial sites (Barrett-Harris 1993, Fischman).
- **Tier 2** — Effects appeared in adult animals, not only juveniles.
- **Tier 2** — Craniofacial growth is not one process: mandible, condyle and
  cranial base differ in mechanism and hormone sensitivity.

## Honest limits — slide 8, non-negotiable
Rat cephalometry, largely female animals, supraphysiologic exposure. Rodent
craniofacial growth differs substantially from human. This does not transfer
cleanly and the deck says so.

## Why the framing must stay negative-to-neutral
Jaw content is heavily searched and heavily monetised with claims far beyond
the evidence. The honest reading of this literature is that multiple
craniofacial sites respond by different amounts — which distorts proportion.
Slide 7 states this. Do not soften it into an enhancement narrative.
