Same dose of GH. Continuous infusion produced HIGHER serum IGF-1 and LESS growth
than pulses did.

"Receptor, GH-BP, and IGF-I are stimulated more by continuous GH delivery,
whereas growth is enhanced more by pulsatility." — Bick 1992

Three rat studies agree. And the one human trial that split a daily dose in two
found nothing, because splitting a subcutaneous depot is not pulsing. That
experiment has not been run in humans.

Clark 1985, Isgaard 1988, Bick 1992, Lundberg 2015. Comment SOURCES.
