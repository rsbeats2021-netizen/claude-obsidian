# Short Bioactive Peptides and Plant Callus Growth (2017)

A Russian-language biochemistry paper testing three short synthetic peptides from the Khavinson peptide-bioregulator research tradition (epitalon, bronchogen, vilon) on tobacco plant callus tissue culture, finding effects on growth and leaf regeneration at very low (nanomolar) concentrations, interpreted as hormone-like or epigenetic signaling activity in plant cells.
