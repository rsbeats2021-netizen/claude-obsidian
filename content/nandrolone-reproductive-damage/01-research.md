# Research brief — Nandrolone and reproductive tissue

## Core claim
High-dose nandrolone caused severe histopathological damage to rat testis and
epididymis — structural destruction, not only hormonal suppression. Those are
different categories with different recovery profiles.

## Key points (tier-tagged)
- **Tier 2** — Nandrolone decanoate 15 mg/kg twice weekly for 10 weeks in male
  rats: destruction of basement epithelial lining, tubular degeneration and
  necrosis in testis and epididymis.
- **Tier 1** — Mechanism: exogenous nandrolone suppresses the
  hypothalamic-pituitary-testicular axis, reducing LH, FSH and intratesticular
  testosterone; this reduces Sertoli cell number and spermatogonia count.
- **Tier 1** — The testis requires intratesticular testosterone at
  concentrations far above serum. Serum levels can look adequate while the
  testis is deprived.
- **Tier 2** — Structural cell loss and signalling suppression are routinely
  discussed as one thing. They aren't.

## Honest limits
High-dose rat model, deliberately supraphysiologic. It demonstrates what high
exposure does to tissue, not what typical human use does. Slide 3 says "the
point is what high dose does to tissue" — keep that qualifier.

## Tone
Same register as `aas-neurotoxicity`: completing a risk picture, not asserting
a specific human outcome.
