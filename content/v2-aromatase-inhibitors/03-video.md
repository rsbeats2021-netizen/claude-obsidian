Aromatase inhibitors. THE DIFFERENCE IS THE PROTOCOL

[cover card]

Boys with constitutional delay of puberty. Testosterone plus placebo against testosterone plus letrozole, for 12 months.

[where it worked]

Mauras ran aromatase inhibitor against growth hormone against both together in pubertal boys with idiopathic short stature. Combination beat either alone over 24 to 36 months.

[combination]

were compared head to head over 3 years in 79 pubertal males. Letrozole produced higher testosterone, lower estradiol, and slower bone age advance.

[anastrozole vs letrozole]

A lot of AI studies report predicted adult height. Wickman reported a 5.1 cm gain in predicted height.

[section: what to check]

24 adolescent males, 12 months of letrozole. Bone age progression fell from 1.51 to 0.68.

[section: what nobody mentions]

Paediatric height studies generally do not screen for the sensory and cognitive outcomes that oncology treats as routine for these drugs. Your brain expresses aromatase and makes estrogen locally.

[takeaway card]

No reported harm is not the same as no harm.
