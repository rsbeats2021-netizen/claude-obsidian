# Video script — There is a second

233 words, ~78s at 178 wpm. Target band 45-90s.

Spoken lines only. Visual cue in brackets.

---

There is a second. Your adrenal glands run it, not your testes.

[cover card]

The headline compound is 11-ketotestosterone, usually written 11-KT. The defining step is an adrenal enzyme called CYP11B1, which installs an oxygen atom at carbon 11 of the steroid backbone.

[11-oxyandrogens]

Adrenal androstenedione gets the 11-oxygen installed by CYP11B1 in the zona reticularis. Then AKR1C3, working out in your fat, liver and immune cells, turns it into 11-ketotestosterone.

[adrenal, then periphery]

The enzymatic efficiency of AKR1C3 is 8-fold greater for the 11-oxygenated precursor than for classical androstenedione. And 24-fold greater than for the 5α-androstanedione route.

[the number that matters]

Classical androgens follow LH from your pituitary, which is why they track with testicular function and respond to things like hCG. 11-oxyandrogens follow ACTH, the same signal that drives cortisol.

[is acth, not lh]

In 21-hydroxylase deficient congenital adrenal hyperplasia, the blocked pathway shunts accumulated precursor through CYP11B1 and 11-ketotestosterone becomes the dominant circulating androgen. In Cushing's disease, chronically elevated ACTH drives CYP11B1 overexpression and 11-KT becomes a primary androgenic driver.

[the proof cases]

The 5α-reduced version, 11-KDHT, circulates at levels 20 to 89 fold lower than DHT. So this pathway is highly efficient at the 11-KT step and distinctly inefficient at the step after it.

[where it runs out]

A standard panel measures testosterone and sometimes DHT. It does not measure 11-ketotestosterone, which binds the same receptor and in several documented conditions is the larger signal of the two.

[takeaway card]

Your androgen panel is not measuring all your androgens.
