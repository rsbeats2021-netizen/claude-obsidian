# Tetrapeptide KEDW and Gene Expression (Khavinson et al. 2015)

Studies KEDW (Lys-Glu-Asp-Trp), a short synthetic tetrapeptide previously shown to lower blood glucose in diabetic rats, examining its effects on pancreatic differentiation gene expression (PDX1, NGN and related genes) and direct DNA interaction.

> [!contradiction] Flagged as topically unrelated to this domain: part of the broader Khavinson "peptide bioregulator" research tradition (see also [[Peptide-Regulation-Chondrogenic-Stem-Cell-Differentiation|Linkova et al. 2023]], the one paper from this research group that IS on-topic for this domain). This specific paper concerns diabetes/pancreatic gene regulation, not steroid hormones or growth-plate biology.
